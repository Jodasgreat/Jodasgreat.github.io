## [V12C7] Promise to Perish Together

The Wedge Tower's funerals were mostly held in the surrounding villages. The graves were there as well.\
While the majority of the Wedge Tower's mages followed the Ras Belsch Orthodoxy, some followed the Spirit God Faith or other religions, so it was common practice to head to a village that could accommodate those rites and honor the dead there.\
On this day, Saint Helena led a few members of the Ras Belsch Orthodoxy to a gravesite.\
The Medical Office's Branch Director Meine wore a properly fitted dress in place of her usual disheveled lab coat as she stood before her childhood friend's grave.

"Hey Dammer… You know, I thought you were the worst, but I didn't hate you. Funny how you can be the worst without me hating you, isn't it?"

The image of him snarling "*What the fuck are you yapping about, bitch?*" appeared in her mind.\
He really was the worst, but even so, she couldn't be mad at him.

"Though you were the worst, you were my friend weren't you? I actually feel a little lonely now, like I almost miss you. Now I know you'll be like '*What the hell does almost miss you mean?*' but it's hard to explain it, okay?"

Meine's childhood friend Dammer died in the battle to retake the Wedge Tower. While there were some who claimed that Dammer fell into the Font of the Abyss and turned into a Monster, Tia and Frederik firmly rejected that notion.

—*That wasn't Dammer.*

—*She's right, the thing we killed wasn't Dammer at all. If it was, I would be a murderer, wouldn't I?*

If they both said so, no one was really in a place to argue against them. Thus, Dammer was laid to rest as a human, according to the teachings of the Ras Belsch Orthodoxy.

Did Dammer die for his honor, or did he die blinded by honor? … A part of her thought it was probably the latter, '*this is Dammer we were talking about after all.*' Regardless, a kind childhood friend would chose to believe he died an honorable death anyway.

"Either way, you've earned your rest, Dammer."

***

Among the list of all the Wedge Tower's human casualties was the name Anneliese Röhm.\
Röhm died in the course of betraying her fellow humans, so she could not be buried in the same graveyard as the others. Nevertheless, the new Headmaster Sevil ordered Liese be honored in an isolated gravesite, communicating that message to only those who were closest to Röhm.

"Our new Headmaster allowed us to bury Liese as a human victim of all this… I can't thank her enough."

While Haydn stood before the tombstone and prayed, a beautiful silver-haired girl — Saint Helena — spoke softly to him.

"I do suspect that she considered that your gratitude would increase your loyalty, Director."

There was no sign of her usual '*how sad, it's so sad*' verbal tic. Even 'Eurydice, the Cackling Foam' sat silently on her chest today.\
Haydn lifted his face up from the tombstone and smiled bitterly.

"You don't think it was genuine? Your posture just now…"

"I have my own thoughts about it. After all, I am Saint Helena of the Ras Belsch Orthodoxy."

The Ras Belsch Orthodoxy clearly decreed that Monsters were an evil to be eradicated.\
She might have been the one most upset with the decision to accept the Monsters' surrender. Saint Helena wasn't alone; anyone from the Ras Belsch Orthodoxy would be troubled by it.\
However, it was an Imperial Princess carrying the Krewing surname who accepted that surrender.

"Are you going back to the Ras Belsch Orthodoxy?"

To be honest, Haydn wouldn't have been surprised if Saint Helena decided to run back to the Ras Belsch Orthodoxy.\
But Saint Helena quietly answered no to Haydn's question.

"No. The Ras Belsch Orthodoxy will likely decry the Wedge Tower's decision to accept the Monsters' surrender. That only makes my presence at the Wedge Tower all the more valuable."

"…Are you sure?"

"I do not intend to doubt God. The teachings instruct us to offer our hands to those in need. I simply have come to realize that I have misjudged who '*those in need*' are."

While Saint Helena wasn't particularly close to Demon Queen Folulutia, the two of them had crossed paths many times before.\
According to Rikard, she must have been particularly impacted by what Tia said when Frederik was injured at the Lange Hamlet.

(Which means that somehow Helena is getting along with Frederik, Rikard, and Tia!)

The man with a tenuous grasp on the interpersonal relationships around him convinced himself of that interpretation in a flash.\
Wasn't having friends such a nice thing? Haydn turned to her with a bright smile.

"Then, as a member of the Extermination Office, I hope you, Frederik, and Rikard do your best as a team from now on as well! I'm counting on you guys!"

All of a sudden, Saint Helena held her face in her hands and broke down.

"Ahhh, how sad… After everything I did to explain my importance, my dense boss still wants to pair me up with those *dumbasses*… how sad… it's so sad…"

***

"Pyofuu!"

Yawning, Tia made her way to a room within the First Spire: White Smoke.\
The night before had been a girl's party in Roswitha and Ella's room. Or, more accurately, a '*console Roswitha as she cried about Former Headmaster Möbius leaving on his trip*' party.\
When Roswitha, who had been secretly harboring feelings for Headmaster Möbius, said "*I'm sure he'll come back some day. I'll just have to steel myself until that day comes.*" So, Tia shouted "*Rukiye, get us some steel! We'll help her put it on!*" but they just laughed at her. Apparently humans steeled their minds, not their bodies.

"Excuse me!"

Eventually, she arrived at a room where Spiremaster Hegelich and a crowd of other mages were waiting. Many of them were mages from the Management Office. Rukiye was there as well.\
Then, in the center of the room, sat a crimson flower ornament — the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Rebecca, the Chromatic Garden.'\
Once a month, Tia used this 'Chromatic Garden' to create food for the Monsters.

"Great Witch, the colors from the Crystal Territory are here."

Tia pinned the crimson flower ornament to her hair, then held a collection of mana-filled gems and plants sent from the Crystal Territory in her hands.\
When she closed her eyes, she could feel another presence inside of her. The Witch.\
Her magic drained the color from the gems and plants.

"So how much mana have you extracted, exactly?"

Director Hegelich — now Spiremaster Hegelich, the supervisor of this event, asked, and Tia responded cheerfully.

"A whole lot!"

"……You…"

"Oh, um… I feel like if I made a month's worth of food, I'd still have a little left over… Like, if a month's worth was a hundred, it'd be like a hundred and twenty or so!"

"Better."

Tia got right to making the meat and medicine, lining all of it up on the table.\
The rule now was that there had to be at least three people, of whom at least one was a Spiremaster or above, present every time Tia used the 'Chromatic Garden.'\
That said, the Wedge Tower mages crowding around Tia now were not wary of her in the slightest.

"Just now, did it activate the magic through Tia's body?"

"So it draws from the mana stored in the 'Chromatic Garden' itself rather than using Tia's mana?"

"But isn't that inefficient? Wouldn't it be better to consume the user's mana and have the 'Chromatic Garden' fill in the gaps…"

The Management Office mages excitedly chattered away around her. Among them, she recognized Old Man Kappel, flight magic specialist Barrett, and even Rukiye. As far as they were concerned, it was like they were captivated by a strange new toy.

"What does it even mean to make 'meat' out of mana?"

Barrett muttered, and Rukiye answered.

"Maybe you could think of it like mana particles that don't yet have an attribute assigned to them yet? Maybe then you could give that mana the 'human' attribute, or something…"

"Oh, that's a new theory! Guys! We're going to try and prove it! My instincts are telling me we can make money from this one! GEHAHAHA!"

When Old Man Kappel started cackling, Hegelich shouted "Meeting adjourned! Shoo! Shoo!" He waved his hands like he was shooing away a feral dog.

"Tia, I've made some adjustments to the Magical Flying Device. Stop by the Management Office later to pick it up."

"Piyopp! Thanks Rukiye!"

"You're welcome."

The Management Office mages hauled away the provisions Tia made with the 'Chromatic Garden' like it was an afterthought. The next step was for Gerald and the others to prepare the meat so it wouldn't spoil.\
Among the Monsters, there were some who subsided off of human emotions, dreams, or the like. For those Monsters, Tia gathered volunteers, absorbed small traces of their emotions and dreams, and stored them in the 'Chromatic Garden.' There, she could convert them into a liquid medicine one could drink. That was the reason there were bottles of liquid among the things she made.\
Neither Classical nor Modern mages could comprehend how it all worked. It was only possible through the power of the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Rebecca, the Chromatic Garden.'

(I wonder what the Witch thinks… having her freedom taken away like this and used just to feed the Monsters.)

What if she would have been better off staying contracted to Kai, living in that quiet forest? Maybe she would have been happier crossing the Western Wall with him and traveling the world freely.\
When those doubts bubbled up in her chest, Tia heard a voice in the back of her head.

—*Thinking you know what would make another happy is naught but hubris.*

Tia let out a surprised chirp and gently touched the flower ornament.\
Then, like thinking loudly, she spoke inside her head.

(Um, Great Witch, I don't know how much time it will take, but…)

From the moment she decided to become King of Monsters, a certain thought had weighed on her mind.\
She hadn't mentioned this to Hütter, Sevil, Wren, nor anyone else. It was something Tia had decided all on her own.

(At the end, when the Monsters of the Crystal Territory all die out, let's perish together.)

She didn't hear a refusal.\
This would be Tia and the Witch's secret promise.

(When it's all over, I would like to die as a Monster, and you as a human.)

—*Nothing will change.*

(…? What do you mean?)

Her next words were beautiful as a song, a little scary, and very powerful.

—*When I see an enemy, I destroy it. When I see something I like, I love it. Until the end of my finite life.*

"That's very human, isn't it?"

Tia inadvertently said that last part out loud, and Hegelich shot her a suspicious glance.

"Did you say something?"

"Pefufu… It's a secret!"
