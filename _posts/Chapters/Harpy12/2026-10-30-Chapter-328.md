## [V12C1] The Final Lesson

The Monster occupation of the Wedge Tower and the ensuing battle came to a close with the surrender of the new (self-proclaimed) Demon Queen to the new (self-proclaimed) Headmaster.\
That said, not every Monster immediately went along with it. Tia urged them to surrender by waving the white flag, but a significant fraction of Monsters refused to abide by her instructions, some even choosing to fight to the death regardless of the result.\
One of the biggest issues complicating the surrender was that of the Monsters' food supplies.\
Quite a few Monsters could not survive without eating humans in some form. From those Monsters' perspectives, surrendering with a promise never to attack a human again was tantamount to starving to death.\
So, when Tia returned to the Wedge Tower with the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Chromatic Garden' in hand, she negotiated with both the humans and the Monsters.\
When Tia first gave her colors and mana to the 'Chromatic Garden,' she attained a human form. Which meant that Artifact had the power to create human flesh… So she wondered: would it be possible to just make the meaty parts?\
Thus, using the power of the 'Chromatic Garden,' Tia offered substitute rations for the Monsters to eat instead of humans. In doing so, she urged the last holdouts among the Monsters to surrender.\
Importantly, the next strongest Monsters after the fear-spreading King — The Primordial Beast and the Vampire Jill — had given up the fight as well, which helped push the other Monsters towards surrender.\
The Primordial Beast had said "*I don't care who wins, I just want to get ready for the next battle.*" While the Vampire Jill apparently just stopped resisting, saying "*I'm bored of all this fighting now.*"\
Another reason the surrender went relatively smoothly was the fact that only Monsters who could wear a Crystal Rivet participated in this attack.\
The Monsters once again had a Crystal Rivet embedded in them, weakening them but allowing them to survive areas of low mana density. Once they were all set, they were sent back to the Crystal Territory.\
Now the humans had to locate all the Monsters and Crystal Rivets, attend to the wounded, cleanup the rubble of the Third Spire: Water Bubble, call the other mages back from the surrounding villages, and more that kept them working long past sunset.\
Tia, the newly-minted Demon Queen, received stern gazes from both humans and Monsters alike.\
From the human perspective, Tia was a Monster who concealed her identity to deceive the humans this whole time; while from the Monsters' perspective, she was a traitor who killed the previous King and handed the battle to the humans.\
Tia was in a difficult position, but with the help of Hütter and Director Hegelich, her negotiations with the human side proceeded surprisingly smoothly.\
As Director Hegelich put it: "*You might not have intended it, but it was the best decision in the end, wasn't it, you?*" — Hegelich spoke with a dour expression, but behind him Hütter kept trying to mimic his expression, so Tia and the other Apprentice Mages couldn't help bursting out in laughter. No fair. Thanks to him, all the tension in the room lifted at once, and the Apprentices all offered Tia their support.

The Ancient Magical Artifact 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower' was completely destroyed, and no one had seen 'Ignatius, the Severer' since it fell into the Font of the Abyss. Sophie, the most knowledgeable on the Abyss, said it might be impossible to retrieve it.\
When all was said and done, the Western Wall had completely disappeared, so the soldiers who came as reinforcements now had the duty of running around to keep watch where the Wall used to be.\
As of now, the human side had three Ancient Magical Artifacts left.\
'Despina, the Chain of Fools,' which Sophie was temporarily borrowing.\
Saint Helena's 'Eurydice, the Cackling Foam.'\
And 'Rebecca, the Chromatic Garden,' which Tia stole from Kai.\
With regards to the 'Chromatic Garden,' Tia had become its user on account of the fact that it was only usable by Monsters and would kill any human who tried, but the humans still seemed resistant to let Tia go walking around with an Ancient Magical Artifact on her person.\
Therefore, while Tia was its contractor, the 'Chromatic Garden' itself was left in Director Hegelich's care. She didn't like that they had to restrict the Witch's freedom like that, but that was just how things were. Besides, she trusted Director Hegelich, so Tia handed it over without a fuss.

Otto's daughter and 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower's contractor, Fiene, lost consciousness soon after and was now in the Medical Office's care.\
Having grown up in the unnatural mana density of Chariclea's Cradle, her body was poorly suited to adapt to the sudden change in mana density.\
Medical Office Director Treu reckoned it might be best to treat her by putting her in a room with relatively high mana density, then gradually weaning her off as she grew accustomed to the lower levels.\
As for the issue of Fiene's lifespan, there wasn't much she could say at the current state.\
All previous contractors lived short lives inside the Cradle with its thick mana density, but what would happen to Fiene after she was released from that fate prematurely was anyone's guess.\
Tia hated Fiene to the point just thinking about her made her skin crawl, but that didn't mean she wished for her death.\
Tia only hoped that, surrounded by Otto and the other adults from the Wedge Tower, the girl would find a new obsession. Something that wasn't Tia.

***

The humans kept at it late into the night. With everyone resting on shifts, they kept working to rebuild the Tower.\
The new Demon Queen, Tia, was directed to rest under the watchful eyes of human guards — which, of course, meant her usual companions.\
Tia tried to catch some sleep, surrounded be Sevil, Ella, Roswitha, and Sophie, when she heard someone's voice from outside the window as dawn approached.

"Tia, Sevil, wake up."

The fog outside the window blurred her view, but even then Wren's luscious golden locks stuck out like a sore thumb.\
Tia cracked the window open, and Wren whispered through the gap.

"Wake Sevil up and come with me. Mr. Hütter is calling for us… He says it's time for the 'final lesson,' whatever that means."

***

To the West of the Wedge Tower, a short distance away from the campus, laid an out-of-season flower garden Tia had made with the power of 'Rebecca, the Chromatic Garden.'\
In the mists of the morning fog, one could just barely make out the radiant colors of the flowers. The sun had yet to rise fully, only illuminating the horizon as a dim twilight still hung over the area.\
If anyone found out the (self-proclaimed) next Headmaster and (self-proclaimed) Demon Queen had snuck out together to a place like this, people back on campus would lose their minds.\
Tia used the Magical Flying Device to shuttle Wren and Sevil out of campus, then the three of them walked the rest of the way to the designated spot.

"Oh hey, morning guys."

Standing in the mist-covered flower garden, Hütter waved at them just as he always did. He must have still been a little tired, as he visibly suppressed a yawn.

"What's all this about, Mr. Hütter? What do you mean by the final lesson?"

"I may be set to become the Wedge Tower's Headmaster shortly, but I don't recall ever graduating from your class. There is much I have still yet to learn."

"Me too! There's a whole lot I still want you to teach me, Mr. Hütter!"

Wren, Sevil, and Tia all voiced their gripes, to which Hütter responded while wiping the morning dew from his hair.

"Anything I can teach you, you can learn just as well elsewhere."

If Hütter said so, it must have been true. Even so, Tia wanted it to be Mr. Hütter who taught her. She was sure Wren and Sevil felt the same way.\
Hütter glanced at each of his students. His face beamed with gentle pride.

"Wren, you should go see the wider world. There's more to it than just the Wedge Tower. You should try going abroad, there's all kinds of interesting stuff out there."

Wren looked like he was about to cry.\
Hütter then turned his gaze to Sevil and smiled bitterly.

"Sevil… I know anything I tell you is going to be pointless, but…"

"Nonsense. None of your teachings are pointless."

"That sounds nice and all, but… I know you'll just ignore anything I teach you that you didn't already want to do…"

As expected of Hütter. He understood Sevil so well!

"Eh, whatever, I think you've started thinking about the bigger picture now. However, that won't be enough to compare to His Majesty. Don't get ahead of yourself, okay? If you want to go after him, you'll have to be much more careful than that."

"Are you implying that all of this was just part of my brother's plan?"

"Who knows? All I'm saying is he mobilized those reinforcements a lot faster than he should have. Maybe you taking over the Wedge Tower was within his expectations as well."

"……"

How many people in this world could successfully shut Sevil up? Hütter truly was amazing.\
Lastly, Hütter turned to face Tia.

"Tia. You really have grown a lot. You've taken my lessons to heart, and you've learned how to self-reflect and improve yourself. It makes me so proud to see how far you've come."

"There's all kinds of things I want you to teach me from now on, too! Since I'm the Demon Queen now, there's so much more I need to learn about humans."

There was so much she wanted to say, but she couldn't put them into words, every emotion caught in her chest.\
She wanted to scream "*Don't go, Mr. Hütter!*" but Hütter had that distinctive soft and unshakable smile of someone who'd already made his decision.

"I can't be both your instructor and a pawn of the Black Lion Emperor at the same time. Think about the situation the Wedge Tower is in right now… You get it, don't you?"

"Then just betray my brother and stay!"

"I knew you'd say that! That's it! Mr. Hütter's final lesson is over!"

Hütter cut off Sevil's outrageous provocation and clapped his hands.

"Now time for your graduation exam. There is no time limit. The test is simple… *Catch me.*"

Tia instinctively warbled "Piroron?"\
How unexpected. If anything, this assignment gave Tia way too much of an advantage. Tia could fly, and her ears were excellent.\
Wren looked like he was thinking the same thing. He asked with a bitter laugh:

"…No matter how you put it, doesn't this put you at a disadvantage, Mr. Hütter?"

"Oh? … If you think so, then why don't you give it a shot?"

Suddenly, there was a loud '*BANG!*' The sheer volume of it made Tia's head shake and her ears ring, leaving her unable to pick up subtle sounds.\
Along with that noise, a cloud of white smoke concealed Hütter's position.

"There he is!"

Sevil dashed through the mist and leapt at a black silhouette she could just barely make out. However, Sevil found herself falling over in the middle of the flower garden.\
What Sevil leapt at was a black cloth of the same color as Hütter's robe. There was nothing inside.\
Then, another *BANG!* erupted from above. They all looked up, and a single card floated down from the sky.\
Wren picked the card up and held it up so Tia and Sevil could read.\
There, in Hütter's handwriting, was a single word.

"*Bye!*"

***

The third-rate con man Smoke Fox earned his name through his understanding of the terrain and weather, and his ability to use sounds and smoke to throw off his pursuers and escape into the smoke without a scratch.\
While Tia and the others were focused on the card floating from overhead, they were oblivious to what was going on around them. In the fog, Hütter had covered his back with dirt and flowers and stealthily crawled his way to freedom.

On that day, the Dream Mage Caspar Hütter disappeared from the Wedge Tower **like a puff of smoke**.
