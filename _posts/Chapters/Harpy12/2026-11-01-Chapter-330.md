## [V12C3] Wren's Decision

*Dear Wren,*

*Thank you for your letter.*\
*Nothing's changed back at home.*\
*Mother is still trying to interfere with the Wedge Tower, and if she knew how things have changed around there, I expect our family's fortune will be an annoyance to you guys going forward.*\
*So, while I was surprised to hear of your decision, I thought it was an excellent idea.*\
*More than anything, I am honored that you turned to me for advice.*\
*It's not much, but I've included some pocket change in this envelope. I hope it will be of some use to you.*\
*I'll be rooting for you, Wren. But if you ever get tired of it, you can always come back home.*\
*If you do, I'll have plenty of snacks for us to eat as we talk like we did when we were kids.*

*Love,*\
*Kevin Bayer*

***

The winds blowing across the Eastern Empire were still cold, but the color gradually returning to the foliage heralded the coming of Spring.\
Snow no longer covered the roads, making it a perfect day to set off on a trip. On this day, Wren scurried across the Wedge Tower's campus in a hurry.\
Recovery operations at the most heavily damaged Third Spire: Water Bubble had finally come to a pause. The most valuable items and the books sealing Monsters thankfully had fire-resistant barriers on them, so most of them made it out intact.\
Mana was still thick around where the mana forge, and thus the Font of the Abyss, used to be. There were already talks about building another mana forge on the site.\
High-quality magical devices made with the mana forge were an important income stream for the Wedge Tower. As the Headmaster, Sevil wanted it rebuilt as soon as possible.

(Sevil knows what she's doing… She's even accepted the funds from His Majesty the Emperor.)

The Wedge Tower had completely cut ties with the Emperor for a while now, but now that the Emperor's sister Sevil was installed as Headmaster, negotiations with the Emperor have reopened. As Sevil put it: "*I let him know of my engagement with my darling… and while I was at it, I threw in some details about what happened.*" As expected of Sevil. She knew what she was doing.\
The Black Lion Emperor dispatched troops to patrol the area now that the Western Wall had fallen, and they remained on the lookout for any Monsters escaping the Crystal Territory into human lands.\
Furthermore, the Emperor's support funds that had been paused for years now flowed to the Wedge Tower once more. Sevil didn't hesitate to take them, saying "*Of course I'll take all the money I can get!*" With how magnanimous she was being, one could almost forget she also sent that coup declaration.\
Thanks to that money, they were able to seriously consider rebuilding the Third Spire: Water Bubble.

(I wonder if the new Spire will be done by the time I return.)

All about the remains of the Third Spire: Water Bubble, the mages of the Management Office were as chipper as always as they worked to rebuild the Spire. As Wren walked past, he could hear the sounds of construction interspersed with Old Man Kappel's guffawing "Gehahaha!"\
The busiest spot on campus these days was the First Spire: White Smoke. Those who had no other place to go gathered there, at least for the time being.\
As of now, the Guidance Office was all but defunct. While the Wedge Tower was shorthanded wherever you went, the situation was especially dire in the Guidance Office considering Anneliese Röhm's betrayal, Hütter's disappearance, and now Director Hegelich had been appointed as Spiremaster for the First Spire.\
Thus, Sevil sent out a request to the Imperial Mage Association, asking them to dispatch some staff to fill the gaps.

(That said, I'm surprised… I never would have guessed that Roswitha would choose to work in the Guidance Office after she graduates as an Apprentice.)

Previously, Roswitha had ardently insisted that she would join the Extermination Office and accept no substitutes, but sealing away the Primordial Beast must have changed her mind. Perhaps one could say she was released from whatever was possessing her on that day. Now she busied herself studying alongside Ella with the goal of joining the Guidance Office.\
Said Ella seemed to be thrilled that her surgery now allowed her to use mana normally. Just about every day she could be seen practicing her skills somewhere. If you saw a gigantic pillar of water erupt in the gardens, it was almost always Ella's doing. Protection Office Director Böll even remarked that she might be on the track to be the next big magic idiot, following in Anneliese Röhm's footsteps. Some day, she and Roswitha will make for excellent instructors who could teach both Classical and Modern Magecraft.\
By now, all of the Apprentice Mages had already decided where they will work.\
Sophie said she and Finn would breathe some life into the Library Office. Whenever she had spare time, she could often be seen reading books aloud to Finn. Every once in a while, one could also catch a glimpse of Assistant Headmaster Julius sitting in on their sessions. When asked "*Are you skipping out on work right now?*" He would answer with a confident smile "*Of course I am.*"

(…Julius has it hard too, doesn't he?)

After all, his Headmaster was Sevil and his fellow Assistant Headmaster was Abel, of all people.\
Abel, the old lady who was previously Spiremaster of the First Spire: White Smoke, and who bore the burden of the Wedge Tower's dark side all that time. There was no telling what went on in Sevil's head when she decided to keep her by her side.

"*I'm sure some would object if I only surrounded myself with loyal yes-men. Also, I would like to have someone who is familiar with the old ways. After all, I was never a Spiremaster before this. It puts me at ease knowing I have someone who knows the ins and outs of the organization.*"

Despite all the crazy things she did and said, she had an eye for that sort of balance that fit an Imperial Princess.\
After losing 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower,' Abel had apparently been in a total fugue state. Anyone would, in her position. She had given her whole life, and the life of her own child, for Chariclea's sake.\
After the battle ended, Abel was essentially a hollow shell, but Sevil insistently strongarmed her into serving as her right hand.\
Julius and Abel were bitter enemies to start with. Whether or not things would go well with these two working alongside each other relied on Sevil's skills as a leader.

When Sevil assumed the role of Headmaster, one of the first proposals she put forth to revitalize the Wedge Tower was the establishment of two new offices: the Communications Office and the Negotiations Office.\
The Communications Office was comprised of those with skills in horseback riding and/or flight magic, an organization tasked with relaying information across long distances. Apparently Oliver and Tia already had plans to join them.\
Chosen to lead the new Communications Office was Winston Barrett, the flight magic user previously employed in the Management Office.\
In Tia's case, the fact that she was both a Harpy and the new Demon Queen was by know well known across the Wedge Tower. Nonetheless, Tia was still treated as one of the Wedge Tower's Apprentice Mages.\
As Sevil put it, involving Tia more closely in the organization meant there were fewer who would try to take up arms against her.\
The Negotiations Office was divided into a section that handled both political negotiations with human powers and one responsible for talks with the Monsters of the Crystal territory, with Gerald recently choosing to join their 'Monster division.' After all, his activities preparing food for the Monsters played a very important role in any negotiations with Monsters going forward.\
However, just because the issue of food was solved did not mean that the Monsters' obsessions with humans were resolved. Monsters' obsessions took many forms.\
From here on, the Negotiations Office would conduct surveys with Tia and Finn's help in order to understand these Monsters' Obsessions and formulate strategies to address them.

"Oh hey, Oliver! Gerald!"

He came upon a somewhat open space where Oliver and Gerald looked to be working on something.\
Atop a wooden table, flattened and salted strips of meat were lined up to dry. This was a form of jerky made from the substitute meat they were giving the Monsters. Dried meats would last longer and be easier to carry, one of the various ideas Gerald was experimenting with to get the most out of the ingredients.\
It seemed like Gerald and Oliver were collecting this batch for use. Both of them paused their work to face Wren.

"Are your preparations in order, Wren?"

"Yep, thanks again for resizing my shoes and clothes for me, Oliver."

"No need to thank me, it was a piece of cake."

Oliver nodded, and beside him Gerald took out a bundle something wrapped in cloth and handed it to Wren.

"Good timing, Wren. Here's some food that'll last the trip. Please, take it with out."

"Oh, thanks!"

As Wren said his thanks, he shot a glance at the Monster jerky lined up right in front of him.\
Then, hesitantly, he had to ask Gerald:

"…This isn't Monster food too, is it?"

Gerald held his hand over his mouth and muttered "Ah…"  and Wren's eyes went wide.

"*Ah*!? Did I just hear you say *Ah*!?"

"Sorry, that was a joke."

Wren let his shoulders relax.

"…Gerald, you cheeky little… You've finally learned how to tell a joke and *this* is what you hit me with…!"

Wren had always wanted to trade jokes and goof around with Gerald.\
It was a bittersweet joy knowing that dream had finally been realized. After all, today was the day that Wren would leave on his trip.

***

"Head West, Wren. You should go see the Kingdom of Ridill."

Those were Sevil's instructions. Immediately after saying that she liked having Wren around and did not want to let go of him, she offered that suggestion.\
The graduation exam Hütter had given them — "*Catch me*" — was not something his three students would give up on so quickly.\
That said, Sevil was Headmaster now, and Tia was the Demon Queen. Neither of them could just leave the Wedge Tower on a whim anymore.\
Tia often traveled back and forth to the Crystal Territory in her Communications Office duties, while also gathering intel in the area around the Wedge Tower.\
Sevil remained at the Wedge Tower, then used that intel to inform her next directives.\
In that light, Sevil turned to Wren and asked if he would like to head West to the Kingdom of Ridill, and see if he could gleam any intel on Hütter over there.

"Piyo… Aren't there a lot of other countries around the Empire? Why Ridill?"

"In fact, there are a number of reasons. First, I believe Wren would benefit from studying their advanced technology and magecraft."

The Wedge Tower gathered magical technology from all across the Empire. However, as Headmaster, Sevil desired the Kingdom of Ridill's technology as well. Her greed truly knew no bounds.

"The other reason is Rose. Rose will be heading back to Ridill in the near future, won't he?"

"Oh, right! So if Wren goes together with Rose, then he'll be safer!"

"Exactly."

The journey between the Wedge Tower and the Kingdom of Ridill was a long one that crossed the whole Empire from East to West. For Wren, who was an inexperienced traveller at best, it would be a very dangerous voyage.\
Even when he did arrive in Ridill, he had to consider his status and where he would stay.\
Apparently Sevil had already consulted Rose on the idea. And Rose had accepted without having to be asked twice.

"…Are you really okay with that? You won't get lonely without your pretty boy around?"

Wren mumbled, and both of them shouted in response.

"I will be lonely! Super lonely!"

"Of course I would!"

Tia and Sevil squeezed Wren from both sides. Wren recognized the meaning of this action.\
This was their formation to make sure Wren wouldn't be alone.\
Sevil rustled Wren's hair as she spoke.

"I put some thought into it, you know. Perhaps Mr. Hütter's graduation exam was meant for you to go out there and see the world, Wren."

'*Yeah, that totally sounded like something he'd do.*' After all, Hütter had said it himself: "*Listen to what all kinds of people have to say.*" "*You should go see the wider world.*"\
Were it not for Hütter's words, Wren would have been satisfied with just the Wedge Tower. He would have accepted that this place was his whole world.\
But now, Wren's mind wandered out of the Wedge Tower's gates. There was just too much out there he wanted to try.

"I will learn to see the big picture, and in doing so, follow Mr. Hütter's path."

"And I'll learn as much as I can and think about it! And then, I'll be able to think about where Mr. Hütter is!"

"And you, Wren. You will see the world, and be the one to catch Mr. Hütter."

All the way to the very end, Hütter had been thinking about them.\
This graduation exam gave him the nudge he needed to go forwards. To see the world.\
"Pefun!" Tia chirped, as she pressed her cheek against Wren's shoulder.

"You know, Wren…"

"What's up?"

"I get to see all kinds of pretty things from up in the sky… So if you see something pretty while your walking on the ground, could you tell me about them?"

Her amber eyes twinkled beautifully.\
She had the inhuman mind of a Monster, and yet Tia drew closer to Wren and smiled at him.

"You've figured it out too, right? Now that I'm contracted to the Chromatic Garden… I've got lots and lots of time."

A Harpy's life was short. That said, with the power of the Chromatic Garden, Tia could extend her life dramatically. She could live even longer than a human, just as that male Harpy had done.\
Wren was well aware that Tia did not wish for a long life. Tia surely still thought, in her heart of hearts, that there was no point in living that long. That there was no meaning to stretch out a Harpy's life any longer than it had to be.\
However, now that she had chosen the mantle of the King of Monsters and become the contractor to the Chromatic Garden, Tia had accepted that life regardless.

"I'll fly and I'll sing and I'll wait for you to come back home… here to the Wedge Tower. That will be Demon Queen Folulutia's obsession!"

Sent off on the orders of the Imperial Princess and obsessed over by the Demon Queen. Was there anything more fitting for a pretty boy?\
So, Wren swallowed that creeping sense of loneliness, and smiled with confidence.

"I wouldn't be a pretty boy if I wasn't up to the task!"

***

Oliver considered how best to lecture Monsters on living a healthy lifestyle as he recorded the process for making the jerky for Monsters. Then, he stopped and called out to Gerald.

"Rose should be about ready by now. How about we go see them off?"

"Yes, let's go."

A part of him still felt it was all too soon, but even so, it was almost time to depart.

"Make sure you send off your pretty boy with style!"

Just as Wren, Gerald, and Oliver started to walk in that direction, someone came running toward them in a panic. It was Sophie.

"Everyone… wait…"

Sophie's face was a mess as she said something they could not overlook.

"It's terrible! Rose… Rose just…!"
