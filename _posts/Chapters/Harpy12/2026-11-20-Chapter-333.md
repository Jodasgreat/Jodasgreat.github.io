## [V12C6] Humble Advice from a Favored Pretty Boy

"*Before we head back to Ridill, there's a little detour I wanna make*" — So Rose had said, so Wren found himself following Rose to the Imperial Capital.\
Along the way, he considered the fact that Rose was (while he didn't look the part) an incredibly important mage in his home country. A Sage. So Wren figured whoever he was detouring to meet had to be someone important.

"So basically, I wasn't able to find the 'Chromatic Garden,' and my identity got out, so Sev–… I mean, the new Headmaster chased me out of the Wedge Tower, so now I'm on my way home!"

"Oh?"

Sitting across from them and listening to this energetic report with the thinnest of smiles was a man with wavy black hair and a chiseled face. — Emperor Leonhard of the Schwargald Empire. The one they called the Black Lion Emperor.

(This isn't just a 'little detour'! This is a really big deal, dude!)

On top of that, Rose's report was a total fabrication.\
He did find the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Chromatic Garden.' Despite that, Rose made no attempt to retrieve the 'Chromatic Garden' for himself. All he did was return the First Thorn Witch's soul inside him to it.\
When asked, Rose told Wren nothing except "*I just thought it would be better that way.*" In fact, thanks to him giving up on the 'Chromatic Garden,' the surviving Monsters now had a reliable supply of food.\
However, from the Black Lion Emperor's perspective, the way this matter concluded could not have pleased him.\
With all said and done, he wasn't able to get his hands on the powerful Artifact he was after. On top of that, his coup-starting traitor of a sister now served as the Wedge Tower's new Headmaster.\
It would be pretty bad if he figured out that the 'Chromatic Garden,' and its contractor Tia with it, was in the palm of Sevil's hand now.\
Tia probably wouldn't use the 'Chromatic Garden' for anything aside from producing food for the Monsters. However, if she so desired, Tia could easily use those black flames to burn down a town or two. Naturally, the Wedge Tower was keeping a close eye on it to make sure that didn't happen, but that was beside the point.

(In the worst case, couldn't Rose get executed for this? There won't be a war, will they?)

Wren knelt down beside Rose with his head bowed and his stomach churning.\
Meanwhile, the Black Lion Emperor spoke with an unbothered demeanor.

"I see. So you had a greater calling."

*The next moment, armed soldiers burst from a hidden chamber and surrounded Rose and Wren* — He anticipated such a thing happening, but it didn't look as if it actually would.

"Oh, right, Your Majesty. I have a request."

"Then speak."

"There's a kid from the Schwarzenberg family in the Wedge Tower, but it sounds like her family is really poor these days… Would you be willing to take them up as Palace Shamans?"

"Oh? Doesn't this family have a long-standing grudge against the Imperial Family?"

"Umm, well, I guess they have some choice words to say, but they would rather put practicality first… I think."

Rose's direct words made the Emperor pause and think for a second.

"…Hm. Then I shall send a courier to the Schwarzenberg family. I'll offer to support them in recognition of the young Shaman's accomplishments at the Wedge Tower."

Whether that would mean appointing Sophie to the Palace or the Schwarzenberg family sending someone else to the capital was unclear.\
Either way, however, Sophie's status would only improve.

(But, is that okay…?)

"You, boy."

Wren jolted up at the sudden call out. There was only one minor in the room — him. Even Wren wasn't audacious enough to contend that "*I'm not just a boy, I'm a pretty boy!*"\
After all, simply being in the same room as the Black Lion Emperor was enough to cow him into silence. He expected nothing less from Sevil's brother. He had a presence like no other. If he wasn't already used to Sevil, this frail little pretty boy might just have passed out.

"Your face says you have something to say. Very well, then speak."

"Forgive me, Your Highness. I simply… I was wondering why you were willing to grant our request when Rose… um, I mean, the Thorn Witch couldn't complete his mission."

He wasn't talking funny, right? How should he word it? Panicking the whole time, Wren nervously voiced his train of thought.

"Complete his mission, you say? Tell me, what do you suppose my objective was?"

"Uh, um… You didn't want the 'Chromatic Garden' to fall into the hands of Monsters that threaten the Empire, so you wanted the Roseberg family to deal with it… or something?"

"That was but a side goal. My true objective was reestablishing contact with the Wedge Tower."

"Oh…"

For many years, the Wedge Tower and the Emperor had not been on speaking terms.\
Thus, the Black Lion Emperor was unable to get a read on the magical knowledge accumulated at the Wedge Tower or the Monsters in the Crystal Territory.\
However, now that Sevil served as Headmaster, talks with the Emperor opened anew. Sevil had no qualms about taking the Black Lion Emperor's subsidies, and accepted the deployment of troops where the Western Wall used to run. Could those troops perhaps be there in part as a threat to keep Sevil in check?

(It feels like there's a *super* complicated tug-of-war going on… Did Mr. Hütter know all this would happen too?)

"Certainly, the 'Chromatic Garden' is a formidable Ancient Magical Artifact, but I doubt it alone would be enough to tip the scales. I have more than enough Ancient Magical Artifacts in my possession, after all."

His words oozed with rock-solid confidence.\
In all likelihood, the Black Lion Emperor did not rate the 'Chromatic Garden' as particularly important, considering its critical weakness: that humans could not use it.

"More importantly, re-opening talks with the Wedge Tower is crucial. Doing so makes it easier for my men to infiltrate. A Headmaster can always be replaced if necessary."

Wren's spine tingled under the pressure of his last sentence.\
Wren tried to bring his gut under control as he looked the Black Lion Emperor in the eyes.

"Sevil is not so naïve."

He retorted without even thinking. Rose's eyes went wide as the Emperor's lips curled into a devilish smile.

"…You would carry water for a traitor?"

"I'm the Headmaster's favorite, so I think you'll get more use out of me alive rather than dead."

Wren resisted his body's instinct to shudder all over while Rose cut in nonchalantly.

"Your Majesty, Princess Adelheid favors Wren to the extent that she and him were willing to get up on stage and dance together~."

He worded it as if it had been some kind of formal ball, but in reality it was Poppo Showtime.\
The Black Lion Emperor looked down at Wren with the eyes of a cat toying with a mouse.

"And what is it that this individual can do, exactly?"

What can a pretty boy do, you ask? The answer is obvious.\
Wren responded with the most radiant pretty boy smile he could.

"When Sevil does something wild, I can call her out in the bluntest way possible. I'm such a pretty boy that Her Highness allows me that privilege!"

All of a sudden, the Black Lion Emperor burst out with laughter. He was positively guffawing. *What next? If he takes a swing at me, I hope Rose saves me…* Wren nervously thought as the Emperor addressed him again.

"Hah! Hah! Haha! I see, so you are Adelheid's personal jester!"

"I'm not a jester, I'm a pretty boy!"

He shouted without thinking, but the Emperor did not seem to mind.\
Chuckling all the while, the Black Lion Emperor waved his hand dismissively. 

"You may leave now. I would like to write a letter to the Roseberg family, so I would like you to stay here in the meantime. I will prepare a room for you."

"Thank you very much, Your Majesty."

Rose said, bowing politely. Wren followed along, all the while imagining sticking his tongue out at him.

***

"You call this pretty boy a jester? I hate that stupid old man, I hope he goes bald!"

"Now, now, since he's treating you as a jester, he was willing to forgive you for how you misspoke, you know~. Jesters are allowed to say whatever they want, after all."

"You really think that's true…?"

Rose soothed Wren's grumblings while setting down their luggage in a luxurious guest room.\
Then, when the servant that guided them there left the room, Rose realized he forgot something.

"Oh, crap."

"What's up, Rose?"

"Hmm…"

Rose knew that the delightful Mr. Hütter who performed the Poppo Dance at the Wedge Tower was not the real Dream Mage.\
The actual Dream Mage was in the Kingdom of Ridill to guard Princess Cecelia who went there to marry. Rose frequented the Royal Palace as one of the Seven Sages, so he'd seen the Dream Mage many times.

(Should I really be the one to break it to Wren that the Hütter he knew was a fake? I was planning to have the Emperor confirm it, but I totally forgot…)

Said Mr. Hütter of the Poppo Dance did not stop at merely doing his espionage job. He was incredibly talented as a history teacher, a singer, and a dancer, and more. Highly capable men like him were hard to come by. He must have been one of the Black Lion Emperor's favorite intelligence agents.\
If so, maybe he shouldn't just bring up that man casually?\
Rose owed a lot to the Black Lion Emperor's fake Hütter. He didn't want to be a bother to him or his collaborators.\
Completely unaware that said fake Hütter was a third-rate con man currently on the run from the Emperor's legions, Rose crossed his arms and pondered.

(Eh, I guess it's fine.)

In due time, Wren may well come to know the true Caspar Hütter. They would cross that bridge when they got to it. Until then, he would let the Dream Mage's existence remain a dream.\
That decided, Rose opened up his luggage. He figured now was a good time to straighten out some of his things. He also needed to work out the report for his friend.\
While Rose dug through the bags for his stationary, Wren glanced over his shoulder at the luggage.

"Hey, Rose. Are you… sure about taking that book home?"

'*That book,*' Wren pointed at a book sticking out from the bag. It was the book he'd received from the First Thorn Witch, Rebecca Roseberg.

"Huh? Wren, do you know what's written in this book?"

"Er, uh, no… I don't know… but, well… I was just wondering if, maybe, y'know… it might cause a bit of a problem with your family…"

Wren was right. This book recorded the cruel acts the Roseberg family committed in their worship of Rebecca Roseberg.\
Rebecca's soul was turned into an Ancient Magical Artifact, and when they learned that Artifact was too much for a human to wield, they then broke off half of that soul and implanted it in a member of the Roseberg family.\
All of this was in the name of resurrecting the First Thorn Witch, Rebecca Roseberg.\
Then, a Harpy grew attached to the 'Chromatic Garden,' and flew away with her. The Roseberg family remembered that fact as '*A bird Monster stole the 'Chromatic Garden.'*"

(It doesn't change anything for me, though.)

Blessed by his mana reserves from brith and able to use the Rose Fortress, he was often treated as the second coming of the First Thorn Witch.\
When he learned that a part of the First Thorn Witch's soul resided in his body, he wondered what might happen if it went away. He was afraid he would no longer be himself.

'*—Even so, I have to give it back,*' he thought.

In the end, returning the Witch's soul didn't cause his mana reserves to drop, nor did it remove his ability to use the Rose Fortress.\
If his soul was a large rose bush, it was like a single flower had been plucked from the stem. Just plucking a single flower wouldn't cause the tree to wilt.

(Even without my ancestor's soul, I was always me the whole time.)

The Rose Fortress the Fifth Thorn Witch Raul Roseberg used was not something he borrowed from his ancestor; it was entirely his own.

"You're right, you know… This book definitely has all kinds of things to say about my family's past. It's not very kind about it, so my grandmothers might burn it if they saw it."

"…They're gonna burn it?"

"So instead, I was thinking of handing it to a friend I can trust once we got back. He knows all about the libraries in our country, but instead of deciding whether or not this book is true, I think he would catalogue it as an important reference. Something like of '*this is a possibility that could be true.*'"

***

"Uh-huh." Nodding along, Wren thought about it.\
The truth was always in the shadows. There was always more than one version of history out there. He was sure that this history would be passed down to future generations, filtered through various perspectives and ideologies.\
And when those future generations searched for the truth, that book — the book Wren wrote — would be one of those possibilities… He figured that alone was an amazing thing.
