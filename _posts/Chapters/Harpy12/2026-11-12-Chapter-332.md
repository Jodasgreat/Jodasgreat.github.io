## [V12C5] Misery Loves Company

A few days after Wren set out on his trip, a shock ran through the Wedge Tower.\
It was a shock of unprecedented proportions. Sevil practically popped a vein, the Spiremasters went paler than ever before, and Tia howled in rage.

"LUAAAAAA!! AAAAAAHHH!!"

Even as she lost herself to her rage, Tia still refused to even let that accursed name leave her lips.\
Ever since they had rescued the girl who was 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower's last contractor and who was raised as the Daughter of God — Fiene — she had been recovering under the care of the Medical Office, but now she had stolen the book in which the Primordial Beast was sealed and fled from the Wedge Tower.\
And what's worse, the message she left at the scene of the crime was…

***

"Ahahahaha! Gramps, they really turned you into a book!? Ahahaha! Hehehe, you look like shit!"

"*I don't want to hear it from you, you little runt.*"

"What, you think I'm gonna be intimidated by a bo~ok?"

Though it marred his beautiful face to do so, the Vampire Jill burst out in uproarious laughter as he flipped through the pages of the book.\
Recorded on these pages was the story of the Primordial Beast's life. It concluded with the passage: *Defeated by mages of the Wedge Tower and Henrik Blanke, Margrave of Warmberg. Sealed at the hands of Sophie Schwarzenberg.*\
Jill slammed the book shut and walked onwards. Ahead of him lied a human town.\
After acquiring enough Crystal Rivets for himself and briefly returning to the Crystal Territory as instructed, Jill placed the rivets in his body once more and put the Crystal Territory behind him.\
The Western Wall was long gone. So long as he had these Crystal Rivets, a Monster like him could go anywhere he pleased.

"First, I'm going to the city~, I'm going to check out the latest fashions~, then I've decided I'm going to watch all the operas I can. Jack will be probably back when Winter rolls around, and I know he'd *love* to see what happened to you, Gramps~! Hohohoho!"

"*Don't get me wrapped up in your hedonism.*"

"But Gramps~! It's less fun when you do it alone~!"

"*What happened to that brat, anyway? Wasn't she with you earlier?*"

That brat — referring to the girl who helped steal the Primordial Beast's book.\
The girl had been waiting for a Monster to approach the Wedge Tower intending to get their hands on the book. She made the proposition herself, convincing him to help her in her escape.

"Oh, that Fiene kid? She said she had other things to do and ran off with two humans behind her."

Jill didn't particularly care much for Fiene, so he lost interest in her almost immediately.\
In his mind, whatever awaited him up ahead was far more enticing.

"Oh right, maybe I should go looking for that little servant of mine as well! You know, recently I found this fascinating little one~. I gave him my blood, so I can find him if we get close enough. When that happens, I'm sure we'll have all kinds of fun together!"

***

When the third-rate con man Smoke Fox saw the group of three ahead of him, his eyes widened and he hurriedly spun around to go the other way… but he was too late.

"I've been looking for you, Mister Hütter!"

"Mr. Hütter~, why would you just leave me behind…"

There was a sketchy man with stars in his eyes he knew as Sombart, the smiley bastard himself.\
Then, loaded down with everyone's luggage and on the verge of tears, there was the doll lover Armster.\
Finally, stepping in front of these two men and greeting him with an elegant curtsy was a delicate young girl with black hair.

"A pleasure to see you here, Uncle Hütter."

It was Otto's daughter, Fiene.

(What the hell is with this group? I can't even begin to understand… But whatever they're doing, I've got a terrible feeling about this…!)

Thousands of questions came to mind, but the first thing that bothered him was this:

"…How did you know where I was?"

"Oh, um, you see… Mr. Hütter, you invited one of my dolls to come with you, didn't you?"

'*At least just say I 'took' it.*' Hütter bristled internally.\
The doll in Hütter's bag was one he snatched from Armster's bags, figuring the man owed him at least that much for all the trouble he caused anyway. After all, considering that he had decided to go on the run from the Black Lion Emperor, he didn't expect any payment for all the work he did. He would need some way to make money on the road.\
As a result of the mission to retake the Wedge Tower, Sevil made herself Headmaster, while the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Chromatic Garden' fell into the hands of Folulutia, the new King of Monsters. While those who knew what was going on might have seen this outcome differently, from an outside perspective it looked as if Sevil now controlled the Artifact, the Crystal Territory, and all the Monsters it by subjugating the Monsters' ruler.\
And after all was said and done, the Black Lion Emperor's coup d'état maniac of a sister now wielded tremendous power in the Autonomous Territory, practically taking over the full force of the Eastern Empire. And Hütter was the one who set the pieces in place for her to accomplish this.\
After all that, there was no way he could just rock up to the Black Lion Emperor and say "*Give me my reward!*" If anything, it would play out like "*You can have your reward… in hell! (slice)*" "*Gyahh!*"\
With that in mind, the least he could do was take a little something from Armster after all the trouble that man had been.

"Mr. Hütter, I always know where all of my dolls are… Hehe, they are my precious little dolls, after all."

"Urgh…"

Hütter understood why the Black Lion Emperor had tried to bring Armster into his fold. The speed of information meant life or death on a battlefield, and knowing where things were took the highest priority. If each allied general and courier took one of Armster's dolls with them, their army would be able to read the progress of battle and intelligence like the back of their hands.

(And if they can perform basic tasks like they did during my Poppo Showtime… it would be no challenge for them to slip into the enemy camp and spread poison all over the place.)

Armster surely had considered no such thing, but the Black Lion Emperor must have thought it through at least that far.\
In that case, he should have just sold the doll right away. He knew Armster's dolls could sell for quite a high price if the buyer knew what they were looking at, so he had thought it a waste to sell it off to some backcountry merchant. 

(Besides, if you think about how enamored Armster was with Fiene's appearance… No wonder she had an easy time getting him under her thumb.)

Hütter bit his lip as the smiley bastard shot a big toothy grin at him.

"Say, Mr. Hütter. What kind of devious tricks are you gonna pull next? Don't worry, whatever it is, I'll help out. Just make sure I've got front-row seats to Mr. Hütter's next great act of villainy."

'*What is this guy's problem?*' The man looked at a weaselly old third-rate con man and he saw hallucinations of a supervillain.\
Hütter took a step back, and now Armster pressed toward him with tears in his eyes.

"Mr. Hütter… I'm begging you, I need your help! All I want to do is make dolls, but I don't want to go back to His Majesty's side! Look, you want the same things too, right? Right?"

Ever since getting his help with the Poppo Showtime, Armster had apparently developed the impression that Hütter was some kind of kindred spirit of his.\
If he knew it would turn out like this, he never would have complimented those stupid dolls. His smooth tongue got the better of him once again.\
Hütter suppressed the urge to hold his head in his hands and instead looked down at Fiene.

"…So what is Otto's little lady doing at a place like this, I wonder…?"

Fiene smiled gently, without giving off the slightest hint of malice.

"If I'm with you, then Tia is sure to come chasing after me, is she not?"

There was no malice, yet there was certainly something sinister about her.

"…Hold on there, the Wedge Tower is definitely going to send people to go find you. There's no way you can escape them forever. Why don't you just give up and head on home?"

"When I made my escape, I worked with the Monsters to steal the Primordial Beast's book."

Hütter felt all the blood drain from his body at once.\
Sevil had to be absolutely furious.

"The Wedge Tower's priority is going to be the Primordial Beast… They won't have time to come chasing after me, will they?"

'*I knew this kid was bad news, but damn. She's worse than I imagined.*' Hütter shuddered.\
But then, he remembered something. Since she grew up in the elevated mana of 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower's Cradle, her lifespan should have been cut short.

"…How long do you have to live, exactly? I'm sure you could live longer if you stayed back at the Medical Office."

"No one knows how long I have to live. Director Treu said as much. If so, I would rather spend the time I have left with Tia chasing after me."

Which meant that, if she hung around at the Wedge Tower, Tia would just ignore her.\
It was a little late, but Hütter felt enormous sympathy for Tia.

(Tia… Looks like both of us have a hard time because these good-for-nothings like us too much…)

The good-for-nothing girl held out her delicate hand.

"Come with me, uncle. There is so much I want to learn from you."

What a pain. He thought he was done playing teacher, yet after everything was over, the worst student imaginable shows up. He can't even teach anything except poppo exercises! 

(Anyone who sees this is gonna suspect me of kidnapping… If Otto knew she was with me I'd be toast! … Dammit, I've got to run away before it gets too bad…!)

A smiley bastard, a doll-maker, a girl who was once the Daughter of God, and a disgraced former traveling performer had only started their next journey… If possible, Hütter would love it if that journey ended quickly, but his wishes would never come true.\
Chased around by Wedge Tower mages and the Black Lion Emperor's subordinates, toyed with by a book-wielding Vampire, and relentlessly pursued by his students: the Demon Queen, the princess, and a grown former pretty boy… The third-rate con man Smoke Fox's chaotic life was just getting started.

***

Two middle-aged men walked the streets of the Imperial Capital.\
One was an unassuming black-haired man, while the other had silver hair and only one arm. Both wore traveling clothes and carried swords on their hips.

"Look, Lutz. There's a storm drain."

"Man, how many times have we done this?"

Otto sighed in frustration as Möbius continued to marvel at the water infrastructure.\
If only Miriam were here at times like this. Without her, he alone wasn't enough to get Möbius to knock it off. Klaus Möbius was the kind of man who would never stop until at least two people called him out on it.\
Möbius, having lost his good arm during the attack on the Wedge Tower, didn't hesitate to officially hand over his title of Headmaster to Princess Adelheid once the Monsters surrendered.\
Now that the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower' was lost and Fiene was free, there was no need for Möbius to keep fighting anymore. Yet, just as Otto allowed himself to relax and awkwardly tried to reach out to his old friend to discuss what lied in store for Fiene… Fiene disappeared.\
Otto and Möbius were distraught. But no one was as furious as the new Headmaster and Demon Queen.\
Of all things, Fiene left a message saying '*I'm off to go see Mr. Hütter.*' That alone was more than enough to rub the new Headmaster and Demon Queen the wrong way.\
Sevil picked up her curved sword and Tia hissed and shrieked with killing intent while shouting at everyone "Find Mr. Hütter this instant!" "Come back, pretty boy! We need you!"\
At this rate Sevil and Tia would see Fiene's head on a chopping block. Otto and Möbius rushed to take responsibility for all of this and promised to bring Fiene back themselves. Along the way, they promised to also help with the search for Hütter too.\
There was no way he could forget that look in Tia's eyes.\
Tia's childish face contorted with the full anger of a Monster as she stomped up to Möbius and said:

"*I haven't forgotten what you people have done to me. Mr. Hütter being there is the only reason I didn't kill both you and that girl.*"

The Harpy, well known for using songs as her weapon, declared with naked hostility in her sharply-honed voice.

"*If that girl lays even a finger on Mr. Hütter, I won't be so forgiving.*"

Thus, Otto and Möbius headed out on their own journey. To search for Hütter, secure Fiene, and while they were at it, to be on the lookout for any Monsters who have wandered in to human settlements.\
That said, to Möbius who had spent years doing nothing but going back and forth between the Wedge Tower and the Crystal Territory to exterminate Monsters, he couldn't help but gawp in amazement at every curiosity the city held. When Möbius flittered around, sucked in by the brand-new carriages or the buildings, Otto grabbed him by the collar and sighed.

"Hah… This town has totally changed since the last time I was here, huh. I hear things are getting unstable these days, there's talks of civil war, and I'm worried about Fiene… Well, I'm sure she'd be fine if she's with Mr. Hütter but…"

"That's right. If she's with Mr. Hütter, she'll be just fine."

Unaware that the Hütter in question — the third-rate con man — was fretting over '*What do I do if Otto thinks I kidnapped her?*', both of them repeated the same sentiment: '*If she's with Hütter, she's fine.*'\
If anything, the two of them were more concerned that Fiene might be bothering Hütter. Their concerns were well-founded.

"If we do find Mr. Hütter, I'd love to go drinking with him again."

"…Yeah."

"Oh right, Klaus. How about you teach me some flight magic? We could cover a lot more ground that way."

Möbius' eyes went wide in surprise. He knew just how reckless it was to start learning flight magic at his age.\
Even so, he nodded with delight. "Sure."

"You're right. Fiene is hard to pin down. It would be good to have some more mobility."

"Alright! Just you wait, my delinquent daughter! I've given up on giving up! I'm taking you home, no matter what!"
