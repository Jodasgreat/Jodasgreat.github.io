## [Finale] The Song of a Monster who Sings for Life

On her way to the Management Office, Tia spotted Julius in the Guidance Office's classroom. After ascending to Assistant Headmaster, he started holding language, arithmetic, and basic magic lessons for kids from the nearby villages. All of his lessons only taught the most basic of basics, but it seemed like his priority was to get them interested in learning first and foremost.\
Every once in a while, a black wolf — Finn — would join in on these lessons.\
Some opposed the idea of having a Monster in the room with kids, but no one could raise an objection after seeing the even bigger red lion sitting in the corner of the classroom.

"Alright, can you tell me which of these ways of linking formulas is wrong, number one two or three? … Agniol."

"Ooh! Mr. Young Master! I don't have a clue!"

"Oh, I know this one. It's number two. That one would make the whole formula just fall apart."

"Kuku… You should try being more like Finn, Agniol."

"Yes! This lesson is so much fun! I don't get any of it though!"

Tia sometimes sat in when she had time, but she had work to do today. She dashed outside and headed for the Management Office.\
The Management Office set up a small shack near the ruins the Third Spire: Water Bubble. This was their new (temporary) workshop. Even with the Spire and the mana forge destroyed, nothing could keep an artisan from making what their heart desires. Today as always, the Management Office's artisans were hard at work banging away at their projects.\
Noticing Tia, Rukiye stopped what she was doing and ran to grab the Magical Flying Device.

"Here you go. I'll be out of town for a while starting tomorrow, so if something happens just let Director Kappel know."

"Piyo! Oh right, you did say you were going somewhere… Uhh, You and someone from the… Finance Office are going to… the Second Imperial Capital?"

"That's right."

Previously, she'd heard that Rukiye had a pretty hostile confrontation with someone from the Finance Office.\
Apparently the guy said that Rukiye's looks made her better suited as a sales girl, and Rukiye took offense to that. Tia wasn't there when it happened, but just hearing about it she could tell how pissed off Rukiye must have been.\
Yet now Rukiye apparently offered to accompany the Finance Office and sell magical devices out of her own volition. As she put it: "*This is part of my studies too, you see? Compared to Wren going overseas, this is nothing.*"\
Rukiye tended not to elaborate what was going on in her mind, but she gave the impression that she wasn't just going to let Wren outdo her. Maybe it was something similar to how Tia felt about her own rival.

"Would you like me to get you a souvenir? Gerald already asked me for spices. Ella and Roswitha wanted a bunch of new books."

Rukiye's words came as a surprise to Tia.

"I thought those two still had all those books Ms. Röhm left behind to read? They still need more?"

"They need more books in order to understand the books she left them. That's just how it is with technical works sometimes."

"Wow, humans really are amazing…"

"Monsters are too."

Rukiye smiled a little when she said that, so Tia delightedly chirped "Pefufun!" back at her.

"Instead of things, you should bring me some stories as a souvenir. I want to hear all about the things you saw and heard out there."

"That's a valuable souvenir. It's going to cost you."

"Piyopp! I'll pay with Harpy songs!"

The mages of the Management Office listened in as Tia began to sing.\
Some sang along, others whistled. It was just another day at the Management Office.

***

As Abel reached the end of a long report, she squeezed her forehead. Reading small words has been increasingly challenging for her as of late.\
She cast her eyes out of the window in an attempt to refocus her vision, but the scene she saw out there caused her to frown and avert her eyes.\
Outside of that window lied the destroyed Third Spire: Water Bubble. The Ancient Magical Artifact 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower,' long buried beneath it, was no more.\
The very thing that Abel had spent her whole life defending was now nothing but a broken heap of rubble.

"Excuse me."

There was a knock, then Spiremaster Alto entered the room. In her hands she held a teapot, cups, and a plate of sweets.\
Alto poured the tea into a cup, set it in front of Abel, and spoke like she was just making casual small talk.

"Finance Office Director Eigen's youngest daughter is here with her mother. Apparently her first birthday is coming up soon."

She knew all about that child.\
When Fiene turned against humanity, that girl was one of the candidates she planned to hand to 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower.'

"…I'm sure you have plenty to say to me, after all the sacrifices I have made. Very well, I am prepared to accept whatever admonishments you may have."

Abel spoke quietly, while Alto downed her tea with a pout.\
Then, she frowned and glared at Abel.

"Then I'll just say it straight up! You're way too secretive! Why can't you just show a little emotion once in a while!?"

"Ah, it would be uncouth of me to be so emotional at my age, you see."

"You've been like that since you were a teenager. Always hiding your true thoughts behind that neat little smile of yours."

"And you've always had a flair for the dramatic, Miss Alto the genius."

"Even I've calmed down a bit."

'*Well, she certainly has.*' Abel thought behind her gentle smile.\
Alto's talents earned her praise as a genius from a young age.\
Meanwhile, Abel always served the Wedge Tower from the shadows, never deigning to take center stage.\
The two of them could not be more different.

(…You know, I've always been jealous of you.)

While Abel whispered in her head, Alto brought out the plate of sweets.

"By the way, what's happened between you and Director Schacht?"

"Oh? Gossip now, is it?"

"Do you have a problem with that? I thought it would be nice to talk about this kind of gossip with you."

General Affairs Office Director Schacht had confessed to her a long time ago.\
By then, Abel was already pregnant, so she was unable to accept Schacht's feelings.\
Although decades had passed since then, Schacht remained single, working dutifully as both the Director of the General Affairs Office and as Abel's right hand man.

"I'm an old granny these days."

"Schacht isn't all that young either. Couldn't you … at least try to get to know him a little better…?"

"Hm, we'll see."

Abel scoffed and took a sip of her tea while Alto pursed her lips and frowned. Both of them were too old to be doing this kind of thing.\
Then, she heard singing from outside the window. The song of a Harpy.\
Alto closed her eyes and smiled.

"A lovely song, isn't it?"

"Indeed it is. I see why Fiene loved it so much."

She, who had sacrificed everything her whole life to protect humanity from Monsters now found herself willfully listening to a Monster's song.\
How ironic.\
She had been beside herself when 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower' was lost; she had thought there was no future for her at her age; yet now she found herself taking the first step to something new.

"…Yes, and it was all thanks to you."

"What is?"

Rather than respond, Abel only gave her a gentle smile.\
Alto's exasperated "Cut it out already!" never failed to entertain her.

***

"Oliver! I'm ready!"

"Roger that!"

Tia shouted, wearing the Magical Flying Device on her back, when Oliver descended from above with spear in hand. Today as always, Oliver was practicing his flight magic.\
Not long ago, he had been limited to that incredibly bothersome flight strategy of soaring straight up into the air, then rocketing off horizontally with a second spell. Lately, however, he'd apparently acquired a knack for flying diagonally too. That was a huge step forward. She wished Wren was here so she could tell him all about it.

"I don't see Frederik anywhere. Is he still sleeping?"

"I thought he would have been sleeping soundly after hearing your song last night?"

"Yeah, he was. That's why I thought he should have woken up by now, but…"

Both of them shared a glance, then headed for the dorms.\
Just when they got there, they ran into Rikard Oks as he stepped out of the door.

"Hello Rikard! Poppo!"

"Hello Tia! Poppo!"

Rikard's booming voice answered Tia's cheerful greeting in kind. This was part of Rikard's voice training. Whenever Tia called out "Poppo!" Rikard would do so as well. It was good practice when you had to speak up in public without feeling embarrassed about it.\
Tia had been practicing singing and dancing with Rikard quite a bit lately. While they were still far from Hütter's flawless Poppo Dance, they were starting to shape up bit by bit.\
Tia was going to be so proud when the day came that Rikard could put on a Poppo Showtime of his own.

"Hey Rikard, is Frederik up yet?"

"He's still asleep. At this rate I think it's crossed the line from a good night's sleep to lazing around, though."

"OHHHH! BROTHERRRR! GET UP!! IT'S ALMOST LUNCHTIME, BROTHERRRR!!"

Oliver dashed into the dorm, and less than a minute later went flying out one of the windows.\
In his place, Frederik strolled out the dorm's front door with a yawn.

"Good morning, little rival. Is it time to head out already?"

"Piyopp! Good morning, Frederik! I'm a little ahead of schedule, but I came here as soon as I was ready! It's okay, you can take your time getting ready."

When Frederik and Tia casually exchanged their greetings, Rikard felt obligated to interject.

"Tia, you can't just tell Frederik to take his time. This guy's concept of time is totally broken."

"How does one even break a concept? You're exaggerating."

"You were just about to go back to sleep when you heard you could take your time, weren't you?"

Frederik averted his eyes.\
Tia suddenly recalled something.

"Pevuvu… Wait, don't tell me that tree that fell down near the dorm was…"

"Helena was pissed off. He wouldn't even wake up to the sound of Eurydice's explosions, so…"

"BROTHERRR! GOING BACK TO SLEEP DISRUPTS YOUR NATURAL RHYTHMS! YOU NEED TO OPEN YOUR CURTAINS AND LET THE SUNLIGHT IN, BROTHERRRR!"

Oliver ran up to join them, where Frederik casually yet forcefully punted him away.

***

Tia's agenda for the day: Patrolling where the Western Wall used to be to make sure there aren't any Monsters around. Then, searching for information on Hütter.\
Usually, Frederik, Rikard, and Helena would accompany Oliver and Tia on these trips. When it came to a battle, nothing was a match for the Extermination Office trio, and with Oliver and Tia there relaying intel was a piece of cake.\
Most importantly, there always had to be someone keeping an eye on Tia whenever she ventured out from the Wedge Tower. Frederik volunteered for that role himself.

"*If you ever become a threat to humans, I'll kill you with my own two hands.*"

"*Yeah, and if it's you Frederik, I know you'll actually do it. I'm relieved.*"

That was the promise Tia and Frederik had made with each other.\
If Tia ever became the kind of Monster that posed a danger to humans, she didn't want to force Sevil to take the responsibility for killing her.\
When forced to take the life of a loved one, Sevil was the kind of person who could actually do it. That was precisely why Tia didn't want Sevil to be the one to kill her.\
It had to be a human who knew how scary Monsters could be, and who also had the skills to kill Tia properly — There was no one better suited than Frederik.

"*I'll do my best to make sure you won't have to kill me, Frederik.*"

When Tia said that, the tension released from Frederik's face. His look of utter relief left a lasting impression on her.

***

Today, Helena was helping out at the graveyard and Rikard had other duties to attend to, so the patrol would only consist of Frederik, Oliver, and Tia.\
When she landed at a good vantage point, Tia pulled a small notepad and pen from her bag. Frederik shot her a quizzical glance.

"What are you writing?"

"Pefufu, you see, it's my book of treasures."

"…Your book of treasures?"

Frederik looked over at the words on Tia's notepad.

"*Beneath a boulder that looks like a bird flapping its wings, there's a live oak tree with tasty berries.*"

"*Follow the river past a round spring and a little spring, there's a cliff with a great view and a nice breeze.*"

"*Go north from a place with a whole lot of white rocks and there's a lake that sparkles. Especially pretty early in the morning.*"

With the same expression as his brother, Oliver asked:

"Tia, what's this?"

"It's a record of the pretty sights I've found!"

When they flew, Harpies were excellent at finding places with gorgeous views.\
So she decided to write down all the places she wanted to show Wren when he eventually came back.

"There's some words here that don't have anything to do with sights though. Are you trying to solve a riddle or something?"

When Frederik flipped the page, Tia rushed to grab the notepad back from him.

"Don't look at that! …You can't…"

"What, is it one of your evil Monster schemes?"

Frederik narrowed his eyes with a devious smile.\
Tia's lips twisted as she whispered under her breath.

"I'm… making a song."

Both Frederik and Oliver's eyes widened just a little bit. Both brothers made the exact same face when they were surprised.\
Tia fidgeted uncharacteristically. She never felt embarrassed to try something new, but to a Harpy who was so particular about her songs, she couldn't bear to show anyone an incomplete song.

"Monsters aren't good at creating things… But you never know if you really can't do something without trying it first… I think."

When she first came to the Wedge Tower, there were all kinds of things that she assumed she, as a Monster, had no need for.\
Yet when the time came, she did need them, and at that point she wanted it more than anything else.\
There were things Monsters could not create; cultures and technologies that were the product of the collective efforts of human history. Human thoughts gave those things meaning.

"I have plenty of time now, so… I want to have something to look forward to in the future. That's why I was thinking of trying out some new things."

In a similar situation to Tia, Finn said he was going to read books. Reading loads of books, then teaching other illiterate kids how to read them as well had become Finn's new goal — his obsession.\
Likewise, she would find all kinds of pretty things, and write a song to tell the people she liked how much she liked them.\
She wanted to try loads of other things too. Rather than just take fun things from humans, she would try making them herself. She had a long life ahead of her, and she wanted to make sure she wasn't spending that life merely living like a watered-down soup drained of all its flavor.\
Maybe it was hard for Monsters. Even so, Tia had confidence that she could do it.

(After all, Mr. Hütter taught me that even I can do these things.)

Frederik eased his devious look and smiled kindly.

"I see, then how about you show me once you're done. If it's a good song, I'll fall asleep for sure."

"Piroro… How am I supposed to know if it's good or not if you're asleep?"

Tia cast a look of doubt, but Oliver considered it with a much more serious expression. Oliver was always serious, though.

"No, he does have a point. Brother never falls asleep to my lullabies…"

"If my little brother is singing a lullaby to me at this age, I can only imagine he's trying to make fun of me. Try it again and I'm crushing your windpipe."

Those extra words he whispered at the end were scary.\
Frederik glared once again at his brother, then picked up his spear and turned to the sky.

"Well, why don't we go take a look around the sky for a bit, my rival?"

Their little flying competitions were part of the fun of their patrols.\
They would compete on speed, how well they could glide on wind currents, and even play tag. As of now, Tia had lost every time. Aside from the magic battle where she had the full force of the Apprentices behind her, Tia had yet to win against Frederik even once.

"Piyopp! Today's gonna be the day I win!"

"I don't plan on losing though?"

As the two rivals playfully stared each other down, Oliver laughed with confidence.

"Hmph… You can't just forget that I'll be participating as well."

"Why can't I? It's not like it'll change anything you useless brat."

"Pirororo…"

***

Frederik started his incantation. On cue, Tia activated her magical flying device and white metal wings spread out from her back.\
A soothing breeze lifted Tia's body ever higher into the sky.\
The sky was at her fingertips and the wind was in her hair. Today was a good day.\
Tia's amber eyes arched in delight and she began to sing. Full of enjoyment, she sang loud and proud.

This was a song dedicated to the dwindling Monsters, a song to praise life until its last moment.
