## [V12C4] Lovable Idiots

At present, the Wedge Tower was a chaotic mix of tower mages, reinforcement soldiers, and nearby villagers who came to pitch in, so it was only natural to run into people you didn't recognize. Nevertheless, reports of a suspicious individual reached the desk of Headmaster Sevil.\
"*I don't know who he is, but he sure is something.*" "*No matter how you cut it, that's no normal man.*" "*Could it be a Monster disguised as a human?*"\
Based on these reports, Sevil had to consider the possibility that a Greater Monster had infiltrated the Tower, so she ordered Assistant Headmaster Julius to apprehend the intruder.\
Later, Agniol would say the following after arriving on the scene with Julius.

"The young master's not good with exercise but he ran as fast as he could! He was totally ready to defend the Tower's peace and everything! But he never thought the intruder would be his friend all clean-shaven like that… The young master was shocked! Shocked, I say!"

***

It was a young man so beautiful one wondered if he was perhaps a rose garden taken human form.\
He looked to be just past twenty years of age. Long lashes surrounded eyes the color of a budding spring forest, while his brilliant curly hair reminded one of a crimson rose. His well-proportioned face was so beautiful it could only have been made by magic.\
When Wren first saw this man's appearance, the image of that beautiful Witch crossed his mind. Wren had never seen the Witch's unveiled face, but she and him looked strangely alike — He felt like they were the same kind of person.\
That man stood at the front gate, surrounded by Apprentices. Rukiye and Roswitha knitted their brows suspiciously, while Ella smiled bitterly. Sevil and Julius had clearly displeased expressions.\
The non-humans — Tia, Finn, and Agniol — whispered about something between each other.

"Pirorororon… The fuzziness was so nice though… now there's nothing to fluff, why would you do this…"

"I was just thinking about how nice it would be to have a proper beard like that…"

"He's got no mane! Why!? We were matching!"

After hearing the non-humans' ramblings, Wren quickly intuited the identity of this handsome man.\
He understood who he was, but his emotions had trouble catching up, so Wren hesitantly asked:

"…Rose?"

"That's me."

The devilishly handsome young man nodded with a warm, relaxed smile.\
If he imagined a beard around that mouth with the same color as his hair… Yep, that was Rose alright. Definitely Rose.

"You're so *young*! You're about the same age as Oliver, aren't you!?"

Wren shouted, his eyes nearly popping out of his head. Oliver and Gerald reacted to this with an attitude of '*Is it really that surprising?*'

"Wren, look. From his neck down, it's the same Rose as always."

"His stance and posture haven't changed."

"Guys, look at his face! Wait, hold on, are you guys looking at this pretty boy the same way? Huh?"

While yelling at Oliver and Gerald, Wren took a closer look at Rose.\
That muscular and tastefully suntanned body was the Rose he knew well. Yet with the face of a tremendously beautiful young man riding atop that body, his mind was thrown for a loop. His long, unruly hair was tied neatly behind his head, and it was simply unfair just how much that little change changed his vibe.

"I've been scammed."

"Totally a scam."

Roswitha and Rukiye grumbled. Usually Ella would step in to talk them down, but she only smiled nervously.\
Sophie, meanwhile, wailed with distress written all over her face.

"Geez, if you'd showed me that face earlier… If you had that face when you came to save me, my heart would have skipped a beat! Like '*My prince!♡*', you know? But now…"

Sophie looked up at Rose and her expression totally fell apart.

"It's way too late now~~~~! Without the fuzzy, Rose isn't Rose anymore~~~! At least show your face when you reveal your identity~!"

"Well… it wasn't really the time…"

"Timing is critical! It's too late for my heart to skip a beat~~! Geez! But even so, we'll always be friends, okay! The Rose in my head will eternally be an honorary fuzzy!"

Roswitha and Ella muttered to each other.

"Wait, what's honorary about that?"

"It's Sophie's way of complimenting him… I think…"

Wren definitely sympathized with Sophie's laments. The Rose Wren knew would always be the fuzzy lackadaisical guy covered in mud all the time.\
Rose was at a loss as Rukiye asked him with a serious expression.

"…More importantly, why did you only now decide to shave your beard?"

"This beard was a disguise. I had a 'curse that makes you fuzzy' placed on me."

"Your country's Shamans must be fun at parties."

"I'm sure my friend would be thrilled if you told him that! I'll tell him for you!"

'*No he won't, anyone can tell that was sarcasm,*' Wren thought.\
Once the curse that made Rose fuzzy was lifted, he could have shaved his beard at any point, but he was occupied with other things and must have totally forgotten about it. After all, the Wedge Tower was taken over immediately after his curse was lifted. No wonder it slipped his mind.

"But then recently I got a letter from my friend back home…"

Rose rummaged through his bag and took out a letter.\
With orderly letters, it outlined critical advice for his return home.\
"*Make sure to double check before you head out in case you forgot something*" "*Don't keep all your cash in one place*" "*If your clothes or boots are damaged, don't just stick it out, go repair them.*" "*Don't crumple important paperwork into a ball.*" "*Comb your hair.*" "*Pay attention to how you're dressed before you show up.*"… and so on. Whoever wrote this made sure to include every basic tip they could think of.

"So I was thinking I should at least shave my beard while I have the chance. Plus, if any of you come over to Ridill, or if I ever come back to the Wedge Tower, I don't want people to be like '*Who's this!?*'… That would be sad…"

Rose gave lukewarm excuses, but Sevil and Julius' gazes were ice-cold. Both of them had been tearing their hair out since morning with all the reports of an intruder.\
Sevil spat in dismay:

"What a bother. If word gets out we treated one of the Seven Sages as a suspicious individual, there would be an international incident."

"All I did was shave my beard… Why does it feel like everyone is mad at me?"

"Ignorance of your own beauty is a sin. You should behave as I do."

Wren nodded along to Sevil's words. She was absolutely correct. He should have some self-awareness. He could take a hint or two from this pretty boy.

(Wait, this means I'm going to be traveling with this face? … It'll be a pretty boy and a pretty man walking together! We're gonna stick out like crazy!)

Wren steeled himself. He was going to have to bring his A-game, or else they'd get wrapped up in all sorts of trouble he'd rather avoid.

"Now Rose, if you have the opportunity to speak with my brother, let him know that I am alive and well."

"Sure! I'll tell him that Sevil is doing great!"

Rose had ties with the Black Lion Emperor himself. He had come here in the first place with the Emperor's support in order to recover the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Chromatic Garden.'\
However, Rose left the 'Chromatic Garden' in Tia's hands. As he put it: "*Just make it look like I wasn't ever able to find the 'Chromatic Garden' at all!*"\
To be frank, were it not for his status as one of Ridill's Seven Sages, he was in a position where he may well have been assassinated by now, whether it be by the Black Lion Emperor or by Sevil. Wren tried to hide is nervousness, but Sevil saw right through him.

"It's nothing for you to worry about."

(Gyah! … It's like she can read my mind…)

Wren shrunk a little as Sevil smiled with a glint in her eyes.

"This is an order from your Headmaster, Wren. Go see the world! Go find Mr. Hütter! And, once you're done, you must return to my side!"

"I don't have a choice in it, do I?"

"You're my favorite. I wouldn't let go of you that easily."

Wren took one step away from the Wedge Tower and turned to catch the whole Tower — and his friends — in his view.\
Before he left, he wanted to make sure this image was burned into his mind. Then, he said:

"Tia, you know, there's something I always wanted to tell you."

"Piyo? What?"

"Well… You know how you don't like the word 'friend'? I was thinking… I know there's no point in coming up with names for every relationship but… y'know…"

Wren still hadn't quite recovered from the shock when Tia screamed "*I don't need friends!*" By then, he had assumed that they were already friends.\
Since then, it has always weighed on Wren's mind.

"To me, Wren is 'A very special person I like a whole lot!'"

"Hehe, that's pretty good. I was thinking something pretty similar."

Wren wanted to put a name to his relationships. He wanted to proudly proclaim "*This is what these people mean to me!*"\
He wanted to tell stories about his Wedge Tower companions to his brother back home, and to all the people he would meet from here on out.\
When the time came, how would he refer to them? — One phrase fit perfectly in Wren's mind, and so he shouted:

"…See you later! My precious companions… My '*lovable idiots!*' Love you guys!"

***

He could hear Tia singing. It was a song to bless Wren's coming voyage.\
With that song in his ears, Wren walked alongside Rose.

"Once we make it to Ridill and everything calms down, I was thinking of introducing you to a colleague of mine named Mary. I think she'll be willing to give you a job and help you with your studies."

"…Uh-huh."

"Oh, and you were interested in the 'Transport King' Anderson, weren't you? I've got a friend of a friend who runs a trading company out there, so I can ask if she needs any help."

"…Uh-huh."

"Oh, also… um…"

Rose hesitated a little bit as he walked.

"Do you need a handkerchief?"

"I dooo…"

"Here."

Wren wiped his teary face with the handkerchief Rose handed him.\
He wasn't making a very pretty boy face at the moment, but he'd done his duty as a pretty boy well until the moment he set off.\
It hurt to part with his lovable idiots. He already missed them. Truthfully, he had mountains of worries about heading to an unfamiliar country. Would he be okay over there? Would he be able to find Hütter? The more he thought about it, the more worries continued to pile up.\
While Wren sniffled into the handkerchief, Rose made a face like he had just realized something.

"Oh right! Wren, could you hold out your hand for a second?"

"…? Like this?"

"Both hands would be better. Now make sure you catch this~… Here!"

Rose took a few steps away from Wren, then swung his arm underhanded to toss a small box at him.\
When it fell into Wren's hands, he saw it was the card game box from earlier.

"It's my gift to you, Wren, as you set off to do your best in new lands!"

"…Why did you throw it?"

"Because that's what friends do!"

"What's that supposed to mean…?"

As he grumbled about it, Wren examined the box in his hands. The elegant lettering and designs reminded him of the tension and excitement he felt when he first held it.

"I thought this was special to you, Rose… Didn't you say your friend gave it to you? Is it really okay for me to have it?"

"Come on, it's fine. I know my friend would say the same thing."

Rose nodded and smiled. Like a grown-up doting on his children. 

"I just hope those cards will help you make new friends in new places wherever you go."

Every time he looked at these cards, he knew he would be reminded of the times he and his friends at the Wedge Tower sat around playing with them.\
Finn would be sitting on Rose's lap and studying the rules while Oliver organized the acorns into piles to explain how points were calculated.\
When Julius and Roswitha would be at each other's throats, Ella would casually win the round and the whole room would go wild as Rukiye spent more time marveling at how the cards were printed than paying attention to the game.\
The rules were all in Ridillian, and it was Gerald who taught Wren how to read some of the tougher words.\
Sevil and Sophie would be munching on snacks, while Tia went *piyo-piyo* and sang to her heart's content… Oh, no. Now he was crying again.

"Hehe, hehehehe… Thanks, Rose."

He could still hear Tia singing.\
If he could hear her voice, surely Tia would be able to hear his as well.\
So, just when the song faded into the distance, he shouted to the skies.

"TIA! Look forward to a new and improved pretty boy when I come back!"

***

As she flew around on the Magical Flying Device, tears poured from Tia's eyes.\
No good. She couldn't sing as well if she was crying.

(I feel lonely now. I'll really miss you.)

But waiting for Wren's return was one of Tia's new obsessions.\
Demon Queen Folulutia would live on, eagerly awaiting the day she can be the one to welcome him back home.

"Bye-bye, Wren… I'll be waiting for you!"

By the time Wren made it back, he would probably be old enough to no longer be a mere 'boy.'\
Regardless, Wren will announce himself by saying "*Your pretty boy has returned!" So, Tia will respond:

" —*Piyopp! Welcome back, pretty boy!*"
