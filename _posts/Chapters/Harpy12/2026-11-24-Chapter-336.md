## Afterword

Thank you all so much for following White-Winged Harpy all this way.\
Since the previous work was the story of a protagonist learning to live in the human world as a human, this time I wanted to write a tale about living in the human world as a monster. What I started writing ended up as this story you see here. I've been able to write a whole lot for this, and it's been a blast.

Since White-Winged Harpy featured so many rambunctious siblings like the Black Lion siblings, the Lange brothers, and the Blanke brothers, I felt like I wanted to try writing about a pair of brothers that actually get along well next time, and that became The Forgotten Curse Knight, which is in serialization right now.\
I would be delighted if you could follow this next one as well.

Isora Matsuri
