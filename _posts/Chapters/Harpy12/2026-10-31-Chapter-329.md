## [V12C2] Joint Offensive of Love

There was an open space near the dorms where Roswitha and the others made their stand against the Primordial Beast.\
Now, that place bore witness to a recreation of the same battle. On the human side stood Henrik Blanke, commander of the reinforcements; alongside Ella, Roswitha, and Sophie.\
Of course, not everything could be exactly as it was the last time. The only reason they had been able to trap the Primordial Beast in that ball of water the first time around was because Ella and Roswitha carefully laid a plan and caught him by surprise.\
In addition, the Primordial Beast did not have the recovery speed he had back then. The Wedge Tower's mana levels had already dropped to a level much more suitable for humans than Monsters.\
Knowing all that, the Primordial Beast requested this rematch, and Henrik accepted.\
The battle between humans and the Primordial Beast went on for more than half of the day. They couldn't use the same ball of water plan that they'd used last time either. Instead, Ella and Roswitha put together a plan to use walls of water to restrict the Primordial Beast's movements to a certain path. In essence: a maze. They shared their plans for the maze with Henrik and Sophie beforehand as well.\
Roswitha kept an eye on the unfolding battle through her watery reconnaissance fish, then directed Ella to reposition the walls in a way that benefitted Henrik. Any left-over openings she would fill with more watery fish.\
When the Primordial Beast got fed up and tried to jump over the walls, Roswitha's fish would attack all at once to knock the Beast off balance. In that opening, Henrik's sword pierced through the Primordial Beast's neck.

"The Shaman Sophie Schwarzenberg commands you. 'Despina, the Chain of Fools,' seal that beast in this tome!"

After waiting all day for this moment, Sophie activated the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Despina, the Chain of Fools.' Glowing chains ensnared the Primordial Beast, and his body started to break apart and turn into words.\
As this happened, Henrik spoke regretfully, still grasping to his sword.

"Sorry… I feel bad about this. We had too much of an advantage…"

"Hey, don't say something so lame. You're the winner."

The Primordial Beast growled disappointedly, and Henrik only responded with a weak "Hah…" Nevertheless, he still held firmly to his sword, watching the Beast's every move like a hawk.\
Roswitha and Ella put everything they knew into weaving this strategy, but it would have been impossible to pull off without Henrik's help.\
Bleeding from all over and slowly turning into words, the Primordial Beast roared.

"Stand proud, humans! Your talents have triumphed!"

The moment words filled the blank pages of Sophie's book, the Primordial Beast's form disappeared. The sealing was complete.\
Roswitha held tightly to one of her wands as she savored the moment.\
The Beast that Roswitha's mother once fought, the one that scarred her mother's face, had now been sealed. Now when she thought of the Beast, no longer did resentment well up within her.

(Mama… I won. *We* won.)

For ages, she stubbornly screamed to everyone she met that she would go after the Primordial Beast and fight her all by herself.\
Yet only by working hand-in-hand with the cooperation of the people around her did she accomplish her goal. Now, Roswitha could finally turn the page on that chapter of her life.\
With this, she could now take the first step towards her next goal, whatever that may be.\
While Roswitha made a face like she was finally freed from a ghost that had been possessing her, Sophie beside her spun around and struck a pose for no good reason.

"Did you see that? Little ol' Sophie here saved the day! …Ow, ouch… Aw my legs hurt after standing all day…"

"…You know I've been casting magic all day, right?"

"Now, now, Roswi. Sophie did finish the job after all."

Surrounding Roswitha, Ella, and Sophie were a crowd of mages and soldiers who had been waiting on standby incase anything happened. All of them looked awfully relieved.\
One person pushed through that crowd to approach Roswitha and co.: Henrik. 

"I owe you all my deepest gratitude for your assistance in sealing this Monster. By the way…"

For a split-second, his strangely unreliable face hardened sharply.

"…Where could I find my little brother?"

Tia and Wren, who had been watching this fight, dashed off somewhere in a tremendous hurry.

***

"Run, Gerald!"

"Pyofu! Pyofu! A scary creature is coming!"

Gerald was sitting in a relatively undamaged room in the First Spire: White Smoke, but when Wren and Tia burst in he set down his quill with an expression of steely resolve.

"So my brother… He beat the Primordial Beast, then?"

Tia shuddered.\
Beneath those long bangs covering his eyes, Gerald's expression was that of a man who had come to peace with his own death. It hurt to see.

Then, the door swung open to reveal a tall, lanky man with a sword on his hip. The commander of the reinforcements, Margrave Henrik Blanke of Warmberg — Gerald's older brother.

"Stand, Gerhard."

The man who fought toe-to-toe with the Primordial Beast and forced a Vampire, a Greater Monster, to surrender to him now unsheathed his blade and ordered Gerald.\
Gerald's brother Henrik radiated a deadly aura that made the room feel like standing on an active battlefield. His grey eyes narrowed sharply and it was clear at a glance that the man was incensed.

"I heard all about it from Sir Hegelich. It sounds like you lied about your identity to hide here as an Apprentice — Without even a word of apology to the house you were supposed to be serving."

Gerald said nothing. Not even an excuse.

"We're going to go apologize to them right now. Atone with your head, Gerhard."

"Wa-wa-wa-wait a second, mister Gerald's brother!"

Before he could think, Wren leapt to action.\
Perhaps Wren was only able to speak up like that because, unlike Tia or Gerald, he did not truly understand how powerful Henrik was.\
She had to sing something or they'd all be toast. She could sing a song to make everyone get along… no, she had a feeling even singing couldn't save this. That deadly aura radiating from him was enough to crush even a Harpy's will. He was just way too scary!\
The new Demon Queen cried "Pefupefu…" with tears in her eyes as she tugged on Wren's clothes, but heedless of her, Wren raised his voice.

"Hey, can't you at least hear Gerald out for once!?"

"Then let me hear it. Gerhard, if you have an excuse, say it now."

Gerald slumped over to the sound of Henrik's words. With a halting and barely-audible voice, he mumbled:

"…I don't have one."

"Idiot! Of course you have an excuse! Come on, say it! Tell him you don't want to fight! Tell him you're not cut out for that lifestyle!!"

Despite Wren's pleas, Gerald just bit his lip and stayed quiet.\
Henrik glanced between Wren and Gerald before speaking again.

"His Majesty has entrusted our family with the defense of the Warmberg borderlands. I will not permit you to abandon that mission, much less to cause trouble to the house you were meant to serve. You have no choice but to atone with your life."

"Now hold on a second! 'Atone with your life' is way too extreme! If anything, the guy you're apologizing to would just be freaked out! Why don't you just talk it out a little and…"

"I'm sorry, if you have nothing to do with this matter, I would like you to be quiet."

His mercilessness cut through the air like a well-honed blade. He left no openings to grab on to.\
However, before Henrik could grab Gerald by the wrist, another voice came entered the door.

"Then what if it was from someone who did have something to do with it?"

"Euuugghhhh," Henrik let out a groan. Drops of sweat streamed down his face.\
Henrik spun around to find himself face-to-face with a beauty with lightly waving black hair — Sevil.

"You wouldn't call me an interloper, would you?"

"…You're totally an interloper, though."

Wren muttered under his breath, but Sevil only puffed out her chest and insisted.

"I'm Lord Henrik's Fiancée, aren't I?"

Henrik shook his head furiously.\
Even Tia, inhuman though she may have been, could tell there was no interpreting his expression as any sort of bashfulness.

"Princess Adelheid, Hegelich has told me a great deal about you as well. I beseech you to return to His Majesty's side and…"

"No can do. After all, I have officially been appointed as the Wedge Tower's Headmaster!"

"Piyo!" Tia chirped up. Wren also sounded surprised.

"Oh right, you did say that. Just declared yourself the next Headmaster and all…"

"Indeed, and just now it's been made official. I am the Headmaster, Lady Abel and Julius are both Assistant Headmasters, and Director Hegelich is moving to replace Abel as the Spiremaster of the First Spire: White Smoke."

"Piyo! It's official! Wow!"

"Heheh, it was trivial. The trick is to never compromise and make sure to let everyone know you won't give them an inch!"

"That's just intimidation…"

Disregarding Wren's critiques, Sevil strode up to Henrik. She was uncompromising, making sure he knew she was not giving him an inch.

"Say, Lord Henrik. What say you and I take this Empire by the reins?"

"Take the Empire!? What possessed you to say that!?"

"I control the Wedge Tower, and with it the Empire's East. You rule Warmberg, to the West. Everything's in place to execute a pincer attack on my Brother and the First Imperial Capital!"

Wren went bug-eyed while Tia pursed her lips in a diamond shape and trilled.

"That was your plan this whole time!?"

"Pirorororoon…"

Tia imagined a map of the Empire in her head.\
The Empire's territory was vast, but it was longer than it was tall. At the Western end sat Warmberg, while the Eastern end was the Ihrsen Autonomous Territory. The most influential force in that Autonomous Territory (so the most important part!) was the Wedge Tower. No wonder she was confident, Henrik and Sevil really controlled both ends of the Empire.

"Come, my darling. Let us take the entire Empire into our hands. It will be a joint offensive of love!"

*A joi-nt of-fen-sive of love,* Tia sounded out, then glanced at Wren.

"Humans do the weirdest things, huh?"

"Don't be stupid, Sevil's a special exception. Don't let her speak for all of humanity."

The whole time this exchange was going on, Gerald just hung his head in silence. His stance looked like he was prepared to offer his head at any moment. Tia had offered her head to a human in much the same way before, so she understood.

(What can I do…?)

Tia's eyes darted around before catching a glimpse of the paper in front of Gerald. This was it.\
Tia grabbed the page and called out to Henrik.

"Um, excuse me, mister Gerald's brother!"

With Sevil all up in his face, Henrik looked to Tia hoping for her to save him.\
Tia ignored his gaze and held out the paper she just picked up.

"You see, Gerald is making all the food for the Monsters!"

Written on the page were a series of research notes pertaining to cooking and preservation techniques to provide meals for Monsters.\
Even before all this, Gerald had been helping Rose out with his experiments in imbuing plants with mana.\
By imbuing plants with mana, Rose had been attempting to design disease-resistant crops that could grow even in poor soil to help prevent famines and food insecurity.\
However, while the mana-infused plants did grow spectacularly, they contained mana levels that were clearly unsafe for human consumption. This caused Gerald and Rose many a headache.

"What if we used these mana-dense crops as Monster food?"

Gerald had been in the process of researching a method for detoxifying those crops to get the mana out of them after harvesting.\
If one served them as they were, it could be food for Monsters, while if one extracted the mana, then humans could eat it as well. They'd found a use for their research and made vegetables that were disease-resistant, easy to grow, and grew even in poor soil.

"And now, Gerald has to process the substitute meat that I made using the 'Chromatic Garden' and carry it to the Crystal Territory. And it's substitute meat, but it's still kinda human meat, you know? A lot of people really don't want to cook that stuff, but Gerald volunteered to do it himself."

Tia had asked Gerald "*Aren't you a little uncomfortable processing human meat?*" not long ago.\
In response, Gerald answered gravely.

"*I would be lying if I said I wasn't at least a little hesitant about it. But even so… I would feel even more uncomfortable if all these Monsters starved to death because I couldn't do it.*"

Because Gerald was willing to say such a thing to her, because he was willing to fight the Monsters' extinction even if only for a short while, Tia knew she had to bow her head on Gerald's behalf.

"Mister brother, let me have Gerald. Folulutia, the King of Monsters, needs this cook."

Tia was a Monster who had surrendered to humanity. She had no right to give a human an order. This was only a request.\
Henrik slowly exhaled as he took his hand off of his sword's hilt.

"I shall take it that Warmberg's Gerhard Blanke is dead. The one standing before me now is simply Gerald Anker, mage of the Wedge Tower."

Gerald lifted his face with a start.\
If this was their last moment together as brothers, that itself was a sad occasion. Tia was speechless, but Henrik gave a weak smile.

"Even so, why not show your face back at home once in a while? Everyone's worried about you."

"……Okay."

"Also, you should come to your sister's wedding party. We're going to have a celebration at our castle before she heads abroad for the ceremony."

"Okay."

Henrik's expression tensed up a little bit as he turned to readdress Sevil.\
Sevil had her arms crossed and a faint smile on her face. She was confident.

"I will be taking my leave now, but I believe it would be best to report to His Majesty about your matter, Princess Adelheid. I trust him to make a wise decision."

"True, we certainly do need to send a report. Then I shall send my brother a report of my own."

At this point, Henrik Blanke was oblivious.\
By the time he visited the Black Lion Emperor to deliver his report, Sevil's '*Engagement-Announcement-slash-Coup-Declaration*' had already arrived. A future on the run from the Emperor's dogged pursuers awaited him.
