## [V11C47] Remembering How to Feel Anger

The moment Tia pulled Fiene away from the Font of the Abyss, Frederik made his move from overhead.\
The Font of the Abyss was lost. No more new Greater Monsters would come shambling out from its depths. All that was left was to finish off the King of Monsters… and yet his speartip missed its mark.

(My attacks just won't hit…!)

Frederik thrusted, but his spear wandered away from the King of Monsters, piercing through nothing but empty air.\
As soon as Frederik saw the King of Monsters' silver eyes, a pain ran through his heart like someone pricked him with a thousand needles. He could hardly even remember how to breathe. He needed to run away. To get out of here. To seek the escape of death.\
Tia's song continued in her attempts to counteract the fear. In all likelihood, were it not for that song, every human around him would have already thrown themselves into the Font of the Abyss.

(…Damn.)

Abilities that fanned the flames of his fear worked especially well on Frederik.\
Be it years ago or now, Frederik Lange has always, always been paralyzed by a fear of Monsters. A fear he could never escape.

"…The Font of the Abyss is buried now. But if we dig it up, would the Font return to life?"

The King of Monsters gazed at the burning Spire and muttered to itself. Just hearing that voice was enough to cause all the hairs on his body to stand on end.\
Yet simultaneously, Frederik's ears picked up on a gentle tune.

"*Teylfia larna lihr sheyt (So you are not taken away by the night) (I hold on to your hand)*"

A song that banished fear itself. That alone supported Frederik's mind as all his instincts told him to run away.

—'*Why? Even though she's a Monster… No, it's because she's a Monster.*'

Tia spun around in midair, making an attempt on the King of Monsters' head with her talons. Frederik likewise wreathed his spear in wind and aimed for the King's abdomen.\
But just before it worked, just before the attacks connected, they drifted off target.\
How must this look to an outside observer? The King of Monsters wasn't doing anything, and yet both Frederik and Tia missed the easiest shots ever for no discernible reason.\
Truly, the King of Monsters wasn't doing anything. Simply the gentle breeze across its jet-black robe was enough to offer a brief glimpse into what lied beneath.\
Therein lied a deep, palpable blackness far darker than the jet-black robe that covered it. Grains of that darkness dripped from the robe like sand.\
Feeling it for himself, Frederik knew at once what these were. Those black grains brought fear itself — they were parts of the King of Monsters. A terrible, terrible fear so powerful it made one wish for death seeped into the ground.

(Where are those black grains going?)

He had a very bad feeling about this. Unable to piece together any more concrete ideas despite the urgency, Frederik gritted his teeth and waited.

"Though you may believe in different gods, you humans all have a god to guide you in the afterlife, do you not? But who then will guide us Monsters? Where will Monsters go after we die? A King alone is insufficient to lead us. We need a God to guide us."

The King of Monsters mumbled, as if speaking only to itself, when it glanced at Tia.

"Oh Harpy of Breakneck Gorge, where will you go when you die?"

Tia's whole body shuddered under the weight of that terrifying silver gaze, yet she kept her cool and answered as if it was only natural.

"Once we've sung enough and flown enough, we Harpies go to die at the bottom of Breakneck Gorge. We need nothing more."

What would he do? Frederik thought amidst his fear.\
Could he hold his head high and go to Heaven? Until just yesterday, he had believed there was nothing wrong with him being a Monster-killer.\
Surely, the God who judges good and evil must be on the side of humans.\
Now that Frederik has protected a Monster, would the gates of Heaven truly let him pass?

(…Ironic, isn't it?)

He never could have imagined he would feel so jealous of a Harpy's simple outlook on life and death that gave no consideration to good or evil.\
Tia, still maintaining her song to banish the fear, raised her voice.

"You're looking for a place to go after you die? Then just pick one! Just think up whatever afterlife you want!"

"As a Monster yourself you must surely understand that we Monsters can do no such thing. Thus, we need a human's assistance."

All of a sudden, the ground shook ferociously. These vibrations all centered on the burning Third Spire: Water Bubble.\
A mature woman's voice echoed out from deep inside.

"*Ahh, so hot… so hot… Where is my daughter? … My precious, adorable daughter…*"

***

Otto, having caught Fiene from Tia's toss, desperately called out to the girl.

"Fiene, are you okay!? Everything's gonna be fine, so don't…"

No matter what Otto said, however, Fiene wouldn't respond.\
With a soulless, doll-like lack of expression, she stared at the burning Third Spire — at 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower' buried beneath it, to be exact.\
Her delicate lips softly whispered.

"I'm right here, Mother Chariclea."

Black smoke rose from the raging bonfire that was once the Third Spire, yet there was another black substance pouring out that looked nothing like smoke.\
Rose's defensive barrier surrounded the burning Spire, containing the heat, flames, and smoke from leaking out… but that defensive barrier did not extend all the way underground.\
Black particles spread deep below the surface, staining the ground an inky black.\
By the time Otto realized what was happening, the whole ground was already jet-black. At the same time, every muscle in his body gave out like his whole body had simply given up on living.

"If injecting Chariclea with crystals causes crystal contamination… Then would you perhaps call this — staining her with the fear of the Abyss — Abyss contamination?"

"Fiene! …Fie… ne…!"

He could hardly even breathe, yet Otto used every breath to call his daughter's name.\
Fiene, however, never even spared Otto a glance.

(Like hell I'd give up on her… I'm done with giving up!)

Otto clawed against the black-stained earth and pushed himself up just enough to grab the hem of Fiene's clothes.\
Fiene stopped and looked down at him. Her eyes carried no feelings toward Otto one way or another.\
In this dark world, assaulted by fear and the Abyss, lives petered out. The sounds of life disappeared one by one. Amid this hopeless world, Fiene alone stood out beautifully. The monochrome land around her only served to accentuate her godliness.

"All will return to the Font of the Abyss. It will meld together, and from it, new life will be born. Thus, the wheel of life will turn once more."

The inhuman and almost artificial-looking girl smiled mercifully. Then, all of a sudden…

A screeching shout echoed from above.

"WHOOOAAAA!! BROTHHEEEERRR!!!"

***

His mind blank with fear, Frederik lied collapsed in the jet-black dirt. Then he heard that headache-inducing voice.

"Oliver, the Red Rain, at your service!"

'*Serve me by getting the hell out of here.*' — His mind, once paralyzed with fear, kicked into action before he knew it.\
Dropping on the King of Monsters from directly overhead was none other than Oliver, wielding a spear.\
No attacks could hit the King of Monsters. One's body, so consumed by fear, would simply refuse to land an attack on it. Yet nonetheless Oliver's attack did not waver. He flew almost foolishly straight at it, and the tip of his spear headed straight for the King's head… but the King of Monsters simply lifted a hand and stopped the spear cold.\
Those sickly pale fingertips grabbed the tip of Oliver's spear, then hurled it along with its wielder back in Frederik's direction.

"Oughff!"

Oliver tumbled across the ground, but just as quickly leapt back to his feet and brandished his spear.

"Brother! Now that I'm here, everything's gonna be just fine!"

Yep, this was definitely the same headache-inducing Oliver as always.\
Frederik's mind had been so terribly cowed by the fear, but he was finally remembering how to feel anger.\
It pissed Frederik off so much he was half worried the veins in his head might burst.

(What's just fine about any of this, you thick-headed muscle-brained stupid little brother! There's no way you can kill a Greater Monster when you're not even using magic in your attacks!)

Oliver lacked so severely in magical talents that flight magic was essentially all he could use. He couldn't even imbue his spear with mana.

—'*Ah, this is pissing me off. I can't take this. Nothing pisses me off more than myself having to rely on this stupid brother of mine!*'

Frederik contained his impulse to blow his lid at him, and instead stood up and chanted an incantation that imbued his own spear with mana. Winds gathered at the tip of Frederik's spear, but not his usual spiraling winds. That would cause the tip to wander off target unless you had practiced with it — meaning Oliver couldn't make use of it.\
Instead, he imbued as much mana as he could into the speartip, then pressed the shaft into Oliver's hand.

"I'll take care of the winds. You just need to **make sure it hits,** Oliver!"

"Roger that!"

Now that he remembered how to feel anger, he also remembered how to move his body.\
Frederik made a quick incantation to activate flight magic, lifting himself, the spear, and Oliver into the air all at once. Then, he **closed his eyes** and sent them flying towards the King of Monsters with his wind at the ready.\
Frederik flew straight as an arrow, focusing on nothing except maintaining the mana imbued in his spear.\
Then, he left the rest to Oliver, who locked eyes with the King of Monsters and aimed.

"I am Oliver Lange, the man who knows no fear!!"

To Oliver, whose sense of fear was eaten by a Monster long ago, the King of Monster's overwhelming fear was meaningless.\
Their spear, foolishly direct and telegraphed a mile away as it was, pierced straight through the King of Monsters' chest.
