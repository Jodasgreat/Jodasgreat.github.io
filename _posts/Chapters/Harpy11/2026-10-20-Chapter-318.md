## [V11C43] Begging for Life

Tears ran down Tia's cheeks as she listened to her sister's voice fading into the distance.\
Where had she gone to seek her final rest after being inflicted with an incurable wound by 'Ignatius, the Severer'? As a Harpy herself, Tia knew all too well.

—A bird who could no longer fly belonged in the bottom of Breakneck Gorge.

Her sister definitely used the last of her strength to return to Breakneck Gorge.\
As Tia stood frozen in place, she heard footsteps behind her. It was Frederik. Dammer had stabbed him with a knife earlier, but it looked like he tore off a sleeve from his clothes to do some rudimentary first aid on his wounded shoulder. Frederik kept his guard up with his spear at the ready as he glared at Tia with darkness in his eyes.

"…Don't move."

Frederik said with a hoarse voice. If Tia made any suspicious movements, his expression made it clear that he would run her through with that spear of his.\
Tia knew why Frederik hated Monsters so much. She thought it was only natural that such a person would despise Monsters.\
So why had he covered for her in spite of that? That part she didn't get.\
A single look at his eyes told that he knew exactly what Tia was.

"Frederik."

Tia bowed her head. When apologizing or begging to a human, the first thing you needed to do was bow your head like this.\
Tia understood that doing so essentially meant offering her own head on a platter.

"The last Harpy of Breakneck Gorge, Queen Folulutia surrenders to humans."

'*I'm leaving myself vulnerable, so please forgive me, okay? Acknowledge my gesture and show some mercy, please…*' Tia stuck her neck out, and begged for her life.\
Frederik spat, his voice filled with anger and hostility.

"…I can't imagine you know what it means for a Monster to surrender."

"I acknowledge my defeat, and submit to the victor. I am prepared to accept whatever treatment humanity deems fit to own a creature like me."

Having been owned as a pet by Fiene before, that word was a grave insult to Tia. Even as the loser, it still made her seethe.\
But regardless, Harpies had lost the contest of survival to humans.

"But with that, I beg of you. Frederik, I need your help to end this battle. Help me. Please."

"…I thought I told you to get out of my sight."

"If you think I'm harmful to humanity, you can kill me any time you want, Frederik."

Since she had her head bowed, Tia couldn't see Frederik's expression. Despite that, she sensed a faint hesitation from him.\
This was something she couldn't ask from Hütter, Wren, nor Sevil.\
Only Frederik, who knew the terror that Monsters brought and who could kill Monsters with ease, could do this.

"I don't want to be harmful to people like Mr. Hütter, Wren, or Sevil. So, Frederik, I want you to kill me if I ever become harmful like that."

After a series of shallow, conflicted breaths, he answered in a low voice.

"…If you hurt someone, I'll kill you."

"Uh-huh."

"…If you control someone with your songs, I'll kill you. If you rape someone, I'll kill you. If you eat someone, I'll kill you."

"Uh-huh."

"…If I ever decide that you're a danger to people, I'll kill you."

He growled a list of grievances.\
Yet somehow, Tia heard a different message:\
—'*Please, don't be like that.*'\
Tia lifted her head and looked up at Frederik. By now, the sight of this human's face in pain no longer looked delectable as it once did.

"Pefu… Yeah, that's fine with me… Thank you, Frederik."

Even after losing Charonlala, the last of her family, Tia was resolved to follow through with Hütter's plan all the way to the end.\
The plan that would be most convenient for humans; the plan that may or may not let Monsters go extinct.

***

The moment Dammer and 'Ignatius, the Severer' fell into the Font of the Abyss, Fiene finally had everything she needed.\
The Font of the Abyss was made from the combination of negative human emotions and mana. There was more than enough mana to begin with, but there weren't quite enough negative emotions. Fortunately, that man who just fell in had just the right amount to cover the deficit.\
Now, the Font of the Abyss expanded to its ideal shape, melding flawlessly with the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower' that formed its base.\
Fiene did not originally have the aptitude necessary to manipulate the Abyss herself. That was a skill that only those of very special bloodlines were capable of.\
So instead, Fiene used her status as contractor to an Ancient Magical Artifact to her advantage. Through her connection to 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower,' she interfered with the Font and by extension controlled the power of the Abyss.\
Through her submerged hand, she could hear the voice of the man who just fell in to the Font.

—*Why me? I'm not doing anything wrong! I'll kill the Monster! I'll kill the white-haired girl! I'll have my glory!*

"Allow me to save you as well."

Gradually, the man's angry voice grew weaker. What once was a man's body melted into the Abyss. Then, the man's dissipated consciousness intertwined with mana, ultimately creating a new Monster.\
Fiene brought this Monster closer just as a mother would pick up her newborn child.\
In time, a black mass crawled out from the Font of the Abyss. It looked like the silhouette of a large human, but with no details like eyes, nose, or mouth, making it look like someone made a rough outline of a person with clay.\
The humanoid Monster's 'face' wiggled.

"I see, so you are a Monster obsessed with glory then."

She lifted the Monster once again and shared her will with it. At last, Fiene had achieved total control over the Abyss.\
The King of Monsters had been waiting for this moment for ages, but now for some reason it stared aimlessly up at the sky.

"…What's wrong?"

Fiene spoke to it, and the King of Monsters whispered, its eyes locked on the northern sky.

"That Harpy did not desire a peaceful rest in the Abyss."

That heavily wounded and bleeding orange Harpy.\
The King of Monsters must have wanted to mourn her by sinking her body into the Abyss.\
But the Harpy refused its offer. That fact must have weighed heavily on the King's heart.\
Fiene's eyes drooped sadly as she said:

"That Harpy couldn't accept the Abyss' salvation, hm… What a pity."

"…Did it perhaps lack persuasiveness?"

"Just now, it has acquired the power it needs to be persuasive. So long as we cast our pain into the Abyss, peace will be all that remains. Come, let us begin the march of death!"

The King of Monsters lightly nodded along with Fiene's words.

"You're right. I need only give one last push."

Once it said that, the King of Monsters gently raised one hand.\
In an instant, true fear dominated the surroundings.\
The darkness inside the King's robes fell upon the earth, staining the ground black.\
The humans and Monsters who fought over that ground stopped at once.\
Some fell to their knees in tears. Some lost their minds, staring upwards with hollow eyes. Others gave up on living entirely and stopped breathing of their own volition.\
When both humans and Monsters cowered in fear, Fiene spoke with a sweet and gentle voice.

"I shall grant you the respite brought about by the Abyss. There, you will find true peace."

Those who heard her voice, even as they cried and groveled, made their way towards the Font of the Abyss with their own feet.\
Like sinners seeking paradise, they moved with desperation and determination. '*Ah, how lovely.*'\
Fiene smiled softly as if she were watching her child's first steps.

"*This is a charm of the night. (Where is the guardian?) (Await the dawn)*\
*A modest token for those who await the dawn (Modest) (Modest)*\
*Teylfia larna lihr sheyt (Yet you still go) (Chasing the sun's light)*"

A singing voice resounded.\
Other voices sang along with the soothing voice, creating a wonderful harmony.\
Those who were about to toss themselves into the Font of the Abyss stopped in their tracks.

"*The curtain shall hide the traveler from sight (The guardian is here) (Illuminating the night)*\
*Granting him but a moment of calm (Now sleep) (Now sleep)*\
*Teylfia larna lihr sheyt (Until the sun rises again) (I am here for you)*"

Three copies of the same voice worked in perfect concert to weave a complete composition. Or perhaps it would be better to say that they were, through their music, creating an atmosphere.\
The singer of this song rode on the back of a flying, spear-wielding mage.\
White hair, amber eyes, and white wings that enveloped the girl's arms. Her feet were bird's feet, covered in scales.

"*With this tiny charm in hand (The guardian sings) (Thinking of you)*\
*A modest token to those who await the dawn (Dawn will come) (Dawn will come)*\
*Teylfia larna lihr sheyt (So you are not taken away by the night) (I hold on to your hand)*"

Carried on a human's back, the white-winged Harpy sang at the top of all three of her voices.\
Her song of rest painted over the King of Monsters' fear.

"*For it will disappear come dawn (It will disappear) (It will disappear)*"
