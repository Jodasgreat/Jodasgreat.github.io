## [V11C45] Rukiye's Request

Two nights earlier, while the three from Hütter's class were off secretly practicing for Poppo Showtime, Rukiye ran to where the other Apprentices were gathered and quietly woke them all up for an urgent message.

"I think Tia might be a Monster."

Rose, Julius, Ella, Roswitha, and Sophie — all six (or perhaps seven, counting Agniol) of them reacted with expressions of utter shock. Sophie in particular was especially shaken.\
Her face, on the verge of tears, screamed '*Please tell me that's a lie!*'

"W– What are you saying all of a sudden, Rukiye? We just went through this whole thing with Finn already…"

"If Finn isn't a Monster, that makes the possibility all the more worth considering, doesn't it?"

Rukiye's retort put Sophie at a loss for words.\
Rose, who was relatively unshaken by the revelation, sat cross-legged and folded his arms.

"Hmm… So if you're saying Tia is a Monster, would that mean Wren and Sevil already knew?"

"Kuku, Mr. Hütter wouldn't have a clue, but I bet Wren and Sevil knew the whole time. When you think about it, those two have been constantly coming up with excuses to cover for Tia's oddities."

Agniol, in her little lion form, followed up Julius' statement with a cheerful "Wow! I didn't realize at all!"\
Everyone was shocked in some way, but it seemed like many of them could see the logic. Reluctantly, Gerald piped up.

"Um, does that mean Tia also has a crystal in her foot?"

"Who knows? It's not like I've checked myself. Besides, she clearly isn't the same species as Finn."

To begin with, Rukiye hadn't even known that Monsters still existed before coming to the Wedge Tower. She barely knew the first thing about Monsters.\
In her place, the Apprentice who knew the most about Monsters, Roswitha, gave it some thought.

"With how much she likes singing… She would have to be either a Harpy or a Lorelei… No, she's gotta be a Harpy. Tia loves flying, too."

Roswitha turned to Rukiye and carefully asked:

"…So are you trying to tell us that Tia is a traitor and we should go smoke her out or something?"

"If so, I would have told the adults first."

Rukiye lifted the Magical Flying Device she'd had with her and stroked it with her fingertips.\
She had taken it off of Tia's hands under the pretense of doing some last-minute adjustments.

"It's not that I trust Tia herself. But considering how trustworthy Sevil and Wren are, I've decided to place my trust in their little group. So, I was thinking of making some changes to Tia's Magical Flying Device."

Julius, quick on the uptake, whispered under his breath.

"You mean making it absorb the user's mana?"

"Exactly. If you pull the lever out of the socket, it will change to a mode where it burns the user's mana. Modern magical devices usually malfunction when Monsters use them, but Tia probably has some limits on her mana output when she's in human form. There haven't been any real malfunctions up till now. However, I'm worried there would definitely be some issues if she used it in Monster form, so I was thinking of enhancing the mana resistance…"

Sophie interjected while making a tense face.

"Uh, uhmm… Which means?"

"It means I'm turning this into a magical device that works on a Monster's mana and designed to withstand a Monster's mana. In short, a magical device tailor made for Monsters."

Normally, it was strictly forbidden to make magical devices that consumed the user's mana unless it was designed to treat patients with either mana hyper-absorption or mana poisoning.\
Thus, this was a taboo no self-respecting artisan would commit.\
Even without that, making secret modifications to a magical device without telling the user was a grave offense to Rukiye's pride as an artisan.

"I don't really like it when people pry into my life, so I don't make a habit of prying into others either. So it's not my place to comment on who or what Tia is. That said…"

When Tia returned from her scouting mission, she seemed awfully down. She said she couldn't be the person who understands her sister.

"*You were sad when your family rejected you, weren't you?*"

Tia had been rejected. That was why she was here, wearing human skin.\
It was not her place to judge. Yet, as one of her fellow Apprentices, and as an artisan, she wanted to do something to help Tia.\
So Rukiye bowed her head and pleaded to her peers:

"Please. I want you to believe in Tia… And when the time is right, I want you to let her know how this magical device is used."

***

With the wings of the Magical Flying Device spread behind her, Tia grabbed a candy out of her pocket and tossed it in her mouth.\
The skin on her girlish arms tore open and white wings sprouted from within. Tia's wings weren't good for much more than gliding these days, but the Magical Flying Device had that part covered.\
More importantly, her original form let her sing with three voices at once.

"*This is a charm of the night. (Where is the guardian?) (Await the dawn)*\
*A modest token for those who await the dawn (Modest) (Modest)*\
*Teylfia larna lihr sheyt (Yet you still go) (Chasing the sun's light)*"

With a song that could banish fear and instill hope, she suppressed the terror of the King of Monsters.\
Then, as she cast her song across the battlefield, Tia closed in with the Monster that was once Dammer.\
In terms of raw strength between Monsters, her opponent born freshly from the Font of the Abyss would probably rank above her. However, with her new and improved Magical Flying Device, Tia had an edge over him in aerial combat.\
The Monster that was once Dammer stretched his tentacles from above to target Tia. Tia dodged them all, spinning as she climbed ever higher. The Monster chased after her, but behind him stood Frederik.

"Begone."

Frederik's wind-wreathed spear grazed the Monster's shoulder. While the Monster was knocked off balance, Tia closed the distance and scratched his face with her talons.\
The Monster was now caught in a pincer with Frederik above and Tia below.\
Regardless, the resilient Monster would not go down so easily. With the high mana density around them, he recovered almost instantly.

(So Dammer really did turn into a Monster.)

Harpies used their songs to capture humans, but they never killed humans directly through song. If they had to, they would either rip them apart with talons or drop them into Breakneck Gorge.\
But against Monsters… A Harpy Queen would not hesitate to sing Monsters to death in order to protect her flock.

"*With this tiny charm in hand (The guardian sings) (Thinking of you)*\
*A modest token to those who await the dawn (Dawn will come) (Dawn will come)*\
*Teylfia larna lihr sheyt (So you are not taken away by the night) (I hold on to your hand)*"

As her fear-banishing song drew to an end, she pivoted to an interlude.

"*La, laalalalaala, lalala… Luuua, laalalalala—aaa—aaa—*"

One of her voices kept singing the interlude. Tia dedicated the remaining two voices to a different song.

"*On the day of tears, you will learn your worthlessness. (I will tear your skin one piece at a time)*\
*In the mud, no one will witness your flower. (I will slice your flesh one chunk at a time)*\
*You will rot, melt, and dissipate. (I will crush your bones one at a time)*"

***

Only one person present could fully appreciate just how impressive this incomprehensible and yet terrifyingly well-executed arrangement was. That being Spiremaster Alto, resident choral magic expert.

(What in the world…)

At first, Tia sang '*This is a Charm of the Night*' as a means of banishing the fear.\
Yet once she sang that song all the way through, she devoted one voice to singing an interlude while the other two voices sang a completely different song on top of that.\
In essence, Tia was trying to keep her fear-banishing song going while also using the effects of a separate song.\
One would expect two different songs played over each other to clash discordantly, but as if she had arranged both songs into a single composition, the combination didn't feel odd or discordant at all.

(No wonder she picked up choral magic so fast…)

The hairs all over Spiremaster Alto's body tingled.\
This was more than just talent; she had the musical sense to arrange these songs together on the spot as she was singing them. That there was a creature born to sing.

***

Tia sang at the Monster that was once Dammer.\
It was a song of demise, meant especially for Monsters.

"*Your demise is evident, you shall not see tomorrow. (I will tear, and slice, and crush you)*\
*Regardless of how much value you wear. (I will tear, and slice you)*\
*That value is not your own. (I will crush, and melt you)*"

Tia had the ability to imbue her songs with directionality that guided their effects.\
Whereas her fear-banishing '*Charm of the Night*' was spread over as wide an area as she could make it, she worked to direct her Monster-killing '*Song of Demise*' solely towards the Monster right in front of her.\
After all, Tia did not want all Monsters to perish.

"*On the final day, you may laugh at your worthlessness. (I will burn your skin)*\
*You will become a buried corpse without a gravestone (I will char your flesh)*\
*To whom will you offer your prayers? (I will turn your bones to ash)*"

Tia's song caused the body of what was once Dammer to crumble into pieces like how a lump of dried dirt would crumble in one's hands.\
Then, Frederik delivered the finishing blow.\
The instant his wind-wreathed speartip gouged into his body, what was once Dammer exploded into a million pieces.\
Tia cast a gaze down to the surface — to Fiene, whose hand was still sunk into the Font of the Abyss.\
Fiene gazed up at Tia sadly. That puny human still hadn't given up on Tia.\
Even though she knew Harpies did not want the Abyss' so-called salvation. Even though the Breakneck Gorge was more than enough for them.\
At that moment, a tiny, watery fish entered the corner of Tia's vision. That had to be Roswitha's magic.

(That's right, the other Apprentices are all…)

Just a minute ago, Tia had heard their voices from all over the place. They had all split up, but all of them were nearby.\
The watery fish swam in circles around the Third Spire: Water Bubble. As if to say that this was a signal.\
Then, her Harpy ears picked up a voice. Wren's voice.

"Tia!! It's time for the Super Mega Pretty Boy Cannon!"
