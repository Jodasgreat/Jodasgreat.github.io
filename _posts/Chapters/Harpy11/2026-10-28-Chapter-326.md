## [V11C51] Designated Talker

"Thus, the male Harpy was saved by the Great Witch, and to show that Witch the wider world, he devoted himself to tearing down the Western Wall. The end."

Kai straightened his hat and in the process covered the flower ornament on his hat with his hand. Covering the ears of the one he loved.

"The humans and Monsters can all just die. All I need is my Witch."

'*Such deep hatred in his voice,*' Tia thought.\
As if it were a great low-pitched bell ringing '*dong, dong*' from deep, deep underground, the surface rumbled with the echoes of his rage. All of that had been steaming within Kai all this time.

"…Kai, how many years have you lived?"

"Who knows? I've been using the Chromatic Garden's power to stay alive by burning through the color the Roseberg Family had built up… but I stopped counting after a hundred."

Kai must have been mingling among humans and cavorting with Monsters for many many long years to prepare for this day. All in service to his plan to make both humans and Monsters suffer, and along the way bringing down the Western Wall.\
At the root of his hatred for both humans and Monsters laid an intense loathing specifically for female Harpies.

(I can't argue with him at all.)

Male Harpies were useless. They didn't have the food to spare feeding the useless ones. Therefore, female Harpies had always killed the males.\
They had done it out of necessity to raise the odds of their race's survival, but it planted the seeds of resentment in the one male Harpy who miraculously survived. In the end, that very practice led to the extinction they had tried to avoid.

"Why are male Harpies killed? *Because they cannot fly and cannot reproduce* … So, Tia…"

A voice filled with malice asked her.

"How is that any different from yourself today?"

"……!"

Now she understood why Kai had broached that deal when Tia lost her flight feathers.\
It was all so he could point at Tia, having lost both her flight and her fertility, and rub her face in the cold reality.\
This was his way of revenge. His retribution against Harpies as a species.

"Honestly, I hate you so much I would love to just kill you right now, Tia. But when I see your sorry mug having lost everything you've ever loved, I think I could live with this. As far as I'm concerned, I'd rather you live a long life and suffer every day of it."

Kai's lips started to whisper something she couldn't make out. Whatever it was, its flowing song-like verses sounded a lot like Roswitha's incantations. Classical Magecraft.

"If you just stand there and watch me go, then I won't do anything. But…"

Kai pulled tiny seeds out from his pocket and dropped them on the ground. They sprouted in the blink of an eye, separating Tia from Kai.\
They were rose vines, but each one was several times wider around than her arm.

(It's just like the ones Rose used…!)

The vines wiggled a little and white flowers bloomed out of them. The delicate-looking flowers let off a gentle fragrance, and yet something about them felt so menacing.

"If you try to stop me, I'll wring out your blood and leave here you bleeding out all alone."

Tia stared at the vines before her.\
Until this point, Tia had never seen Kai openly using magecraft in her presence. Ever so cautious, Kai must have been playing his cards close to his chest.

(I know I'm not good at thinking about complex things, but…)

If she broke things down into smaller chunks she could understand, then bit by bit the whole picture would come into view.

"*Mr. Hütter, what do you mean by 'break it down'? How is that different from just reading it?*"

"*You have to do more than just reading or hearing the words. You've got to ask 'Why?' 'How?' until you've searched through every question you can ask.*"

Every time Tia came to Hütter's Imperial history lessons with questions like "*Why?*" and "*How?*" he would respond "*Oh, good question. Questions like that are the first step toward understanding.*"\
And the answer to each question always started with prompting her to make her best guess first.

(First, work out what I do understand. I need to make sure I've got everything in order.)

The first thing she knew: Kai had just used an Ancient Magical Artifact right in front of him.

"I didn't know you had an Ancient Magical Artifact, Kai. You were hiding it, weren't you? … Is that the Witch's power?"

"……"

"Does one of those powers involve mental interference?"

"……"

"Were you the one who used mental interference magic on the nun lady… Assistant Headmaster Miriam… and made her think of Fiene as the Daughter of God?"

"……"

"Kai, were you the one who made the Wedge Tower cut ties with the former Emperor? That blond man who pretended to be the Emperor's messenger and delivered the false letter, was that you, Kai?"

Bit by bit, the web unwound as she worked out her thoughts.\
Tia pressed with confidence, and Kai just curled his lips. He held something in his hand: a crystal shard… no, a *rivet.*

"The humans and Monsters just took what I told them and took it in whatever way was most convenient for them."

His leering smile was exactly that of a Harpy staring down their prey.

"…Funny, isn't it?"

"Kai."

Tia sent mana into her Magical Flying Device. The artificial wings absorbed Tia's mana and glowed white.\
Standing before her was a victim who had suffered at the hands of female Harpies like her. Someone who was trampled upon time and again by both humans and Monsters.\
Even so, his naked hostility and tricks in the course of enacting his judgement on both humans and Monsters made him a clear threat to both. Neither humans nor Monsters could afford to leave him alive any longer.\
…In that case…

"…I acknowledge your grievances. Someone must take accountability for the suffering Harpies have wrought upon you as a male Harpy. I, the last Queen, Folulutia, accept responsibility for everything."

The rose vines shot at her all at once, forcing Tia to fly higher to dodge them. Then, she switched her wings to flying mode and did a barrel roll.\
With the face of a Harpy gunning for her prey, Tia screeched.

"But still, I must kill you! As a threat to both humans and Monsters alike!"

"I see, then you'll have to die."

As Tia dodged the rose vines and focused on the wind's heading, Kai stretched an arm out in her direction.\
Then, he spoke a song-like verse of an incatation.

"*O groveling dead, o prideful king, let all be consumed equally by ash–*"

Before he could finish, Kai let out a sharp gasp. Because he inhaled the pungent fumes drifting towards him from upwind. Seemingly unable to control his mana, the rose vines halted their rampage.\
Standing upwind of Kai with a handkerchief over his mouth was a certain pretty boy — Wren.

"I don't care how cool your magic is. It won't do jack if you can't chant! That's a weakness of the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Chromatic Garden,' isn't it?"

Kai covered his face with his hands and coughed as he tried to wave away the smoke around him. There was no way he could chant anything in this state.\
Upwind of him, Wren continued to needle him with words.

"You talked all that game, but I know you refused to say any of the parts that would hurt your story, didn't you?"

Kai glared suspiciously at Wren. '*What's with this kid?*' His gaze asked, to which Wren held his head up and declared:

"The Ancient Magical Artifact 'Rebecca, the Chromatic Garden'… Or should I say, the First Thorn Witch, Rebecca Roseberg?"

Instantly, Kai's face went red with rage.\
Even in the middle of all this noxious smoke, Kai's eyes were wide open, glowering furiously at Wren.

"You…! I see, that was you! The one… who snuck into… the Witch's house…!"

"Oh, I wouldn't put it like that. I was invited. I'm a pretty boy, after all."

Kai coughed as he reasserted control over the rose vines. But before they could do any damage, Tia came swooping down to pick Wren up with her feet.\
Dangling from Tia's feet, Wren continued his monologue.

"'Rebecca, the Chromatic Garden' — it's an Artifact that replicates all the magecraft of the First Thorn Witch, Rebecca Roseberg. You, meanwhile…"

Wren looked down on Kai with a look of pity, and told him:

"You killed the Harpy that was its previous contractor and made a contract with it yourself, am I wrong?"

Kai's face contorted from anger to a whole different kind of discomposure. Between strands of his blond hair, his eyes gleamed with a murderous glint as he glared at Wren.

"The 'Chromatic Garden' is made from the soul of Rebecca Roseberg. But only half of it. The other half was extracted from the 'Chromatic Garden' to be implanted in the blood of the Roseberg Family. The 'Chromatic Garden,' what you call the 'Witch,' isn't at its full strength."

"Shut up…"

"You lied to that Witch. Didn't you? At the very least, you had to make sure that nothing she didn't want to hear reached her ears, right? You couldn't afford to have your beloved Witch hating you, could you!?"

Perhaps on instinct, Kai's hand drifted to the flower ornament on his hat.

"Who… are you…? Why… why…?"

Tia couldn't help herself from whistling. "Piroro!" Wren was amazing. Tia had really tried her best to stand up to Kai, but Wren made the whole thing look easy.\
Tia looked down in amazement as Wren gave a quick wink and whispered to her.

"Tia, leave the talking to your favorite brainy pretty boy. Besides, that guy really pisses me off. He made you go through all that shit for his own gratification!"

"Piyo… But this is my responsibility as a female Harpy."

"Even so, I can't stand that asshole!"

"Pefu…" Tia huffed.\
She wasn't about to relinquish her responsibility for this. She didn't want to take the easy route and bail. Even so, Wren's words delighted her.\
Wren removed the handkerchief from his mouth and snorted at Kai down below.

"You didn't know who I was at all, did you? That's just proof the Witch doesn't trust you."

As he said that, Wren offered him a glimpse of the little vial of candies the Witch had given him.\
Kai's eyes immediately shot wide open. It was unmistakably one of the Witch's candy bottles, but it wasn't one Kai had given to Tia. The Witch gave this to Wren directly.\
If the Witch was truly just an Artifact that followed Kai's orders, obviously she would have told Kai all about Wren's visit in the time he was away. But the Witch never said a word about Wren at all.\
Kai tore the hat off his head and spoke to the flower ornament that adorned it.

"Oh Great Witch… Oh Witch… Why…? Why…!?"

***

Once upon a time,\
In the Witch's garden, between verses of her song, a Harpy spoke.

"You know, I don't just have sisters. I had a little brother, too. The moment the egg cracked, I knew the chick was a male. Did you know? Harpies…"

"When a male is born, you drop it to the bottom of Breakneck Gorge."

"…Yeah. That. But Mom was so distracted keeping the eggs warm I don't think anyone noticed. So I secretly took my brother and dropped him off at a human village. All I told everyone was '*It was a male, so I got rid of it.*'"

The Witch said nothing. Knowing what she did about Harpy biology, she could neither condone nor condemn those actions.\
The Harpy, the Witch's friend, stared into the distant sky.

"If you ever see my brother, I'm sure he will like you as much as I do."

To make sure her young brother wasn't crushed, she wrapped him in leaves and tree bark before picking up the bundle in her talons and singing him off with a gentle song. "*O Rose, O Rose,*" The song of love to the Witch of Roses.

"My brother is a Harpy too. I know he will be fond of you."

***

The Harpy still had the appearance of a young girl, but in human terms she was already pushing thirty. Exceedingly long-lived by Harpy standards.\
But her time would be up soon. Long distance flight had become a struggle for her.\
The Harpy perched on a branch to rest her wings. Then, her fingertips caressed the crimson flower ornament adorning her hair.\
Once she made it back to Breakneck Gorge, she would pass this ornament — the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Rebecca, the Chromatic Garden' — off to someone else. Without a contractor, the Witch wouldn't be able to maintain her garden.\
She'd already said her goodbyes to the Witch. All that was left was to hand this off… As she was thinking of that, she felt a shock to the back of her head. Someone had thrown a rock at her.\
She toppled off the branch and sank to the ground, and a young blond fellow ran up to her, swinging a large stone.

"Die, Harpy! You… you bitch! … Why did I have to suffer all this… It's all your fault, Harpy! You damned…! You bastards!!"

Cursing her out every way he knew how, the male Harpy repeatedly battered her head with the stone.\
Over, and over, and over, and over again. Once for every time he'd been abused.

"…You creatures should just go extinct!"

Hissing with a voice filled with rage, the man picked a crimson rose flower ornament off of the ground.\
Not knowing that this Harpy had been the first one to ever love him.

When the Harpy flew off, Kai entered the Witch's garden.\
He didn't lie.\
When the Harpy flew off, Kai killed that Harpy and stole the Artifact off her corpse. He used it to recreate the Witch's garden, *then* Kai entered the Witch's garden.
