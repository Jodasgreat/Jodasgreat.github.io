## [V11C44] White-Winged Harpy

The Wedge Tower's mages looked to the sky and found themselves at a loss for words.\
The King of Monsters radiated madness-inducing fear, but it was mitigated by a shrill song.\
The singer of that song was no human. It took the form of a bird Monster with white wings.\
Hütter may have claimed it was the work of his 'chantless illusion magic,' but there was no hiding that singing voice. It carried potency and mana that far outclassed even the most advanced choral magic. That was the siren song of a Harpy.\
A Harpy with the face of Apprentice Mage Tia Vogel, carried on the back of Extermination Office mage Frederik Lange, sang her monstrous tune. Just how many people there fully comprehended what they were seeing?\
Just one of them. Sevil used her curved sword to prop herself up, shaking away the lingering terror as she looked up at Tia and smiled.

(I see, so Tia has finally brought Frederik Lange to her side.)

Her duty as a commander compelled her to whip her comrades back into action, but she wasn't so sure about that. Tia's song was just too magnificent; it would be rude to start barking orders over her.

***

Riding on Frederik's back and singing, Tia gazed down toward the ground.\
The chaotic battlefield where humans and Monsters mixed together smelled of death and fear. And in the center of it all: the King of Monsters, Fiene, and the Font of the Abyss.

—*The King of Monsters or the Font of the Abyss. Taking out one or both of them is the key to getting out of this mess.*

Without the King of Monsters, the Monsters would have no one left to guide them unlike the Humans who had a whole organizational structure to guide them.\
Alternatively, without the Font of the Abyss, the Monsters would be unable to maintain their mana density and have no choice but to return to the Crystal Territory.

(The battle has already begun. Blood will spill…)

If the Monsters somehow won this battle, the Empire's armies would surely eradicate them all.\
For the people she knew at the Wedge Tower to die *and* for Monsters to go extinct… That would be the worst possible outcome.\
So, even if it made her a traitor, Tia chose to side with the humans and take down the King of Monsters.\
Frederik spoke rapidly.

"I'm speeding up. I won't be able to support you from here on, so hold on tight."

"Piyopp!"

Up to this point, Frederik had been flying with one hand on his spear and the other keeping Tia steady on his back. But he couldn't fight to his fullest like that.\
So Tia wrapped her fluffy feathered arms tightly around Frederik and, just to be sure, squeezed her legs around his stomach.

"*Teylfia larna lihr sheyt (Yet you still go) (Chasing the sun's light)*"

As Tia sang her song to counteract the fear, Frederik accelerated with his flight magic. He intended to run his spear and the whirling winds around it straight through the King of Monsters.\
However, just before he could make it, a dark shape blocked the way. It was a black mass with the outline of an adult man — most likely a newly born Monster. Its body writhed violently, then suddenly huge wings sprouted from its back. Almost like the wings Tia herself had right now.\
The Monster gargled, and with a shaky, growling voice, whispered something.

—*I'll have my glory. I'll kill. Kill the bird girl… Give me more glory. More praise.*

Upon hearing that voice, Tia instantly understood.

"That… That's Dammer…"

"That thing?"

When Frederik took a swing at him, the winged Monster spun out of the way. Then, he tried to maneuver its way on top of Frederik. Not one to give in, Frederik sped up and climbed as well. Both knew the altitude advantage often decided the winner of these aerial dogfights.\
The Monster that was once Dammer flapped his wings with extremely precise form. Frederik asked Tia as her heart skipped a beat:

"So you're saying Dammer was reborn as a Harpy?"

"No, that's not a Harpy. His wings are a lot like a Harpy's… but he's a new primordial Monster."

She could clearly tell it wasn't a Harpy because this Monster cared nothing for music. He spoke of nothing but a desire for glory.\
After all, male Harpies were worthless things that could neither reproduce nor fly. When one was born, it would be killed immediately.\
What stood in their way now was a whole new breed of Monster, just one that happened to look a lot like a Harpy.\
Newborn as he might be, any Monster born directly from the Abyss was due to be quite powerful.\
On the other hand, while Frederik might have been a master of flight magic, he was both injured and carrying Tia on his back.

"Frederik, set me down for a second."

"I'd love to if I could, but…"

The sound of tightly compressed air bursting at once boomed towards them. A long black whip extended from the body of the Monster that was once Dammer, and it flew straight towards Frederik, intending to **impale** him.\
Dammer frequently used his whips to ensnare his opponents' arms and legs, but this Monster did not merely swing his whip. It could fly straight at its target at high velocity with enough force to pierce through human flesh.\
It was, in essence, no different from a gun.\
With a '*Crack!*' and a '*Boom!*' the tentacle emerging from Dammer's body rocketed towards Frederik and Tia.\
There would be no time for him to set Tia down. If they did try to descend for a little bit to let her get off, the Monster would take advantage of that moment for sure.\
Frederik swiftly swung his spear around, deflecting the attack. Despite it moving at the speed of a bullet, he knocked it away with his spear. This was the work of a true master, but in that brief span of time he focused on that, the Monster that was once Dammer spun around and climbed even higher. Now he had the altitude advantage.

"Frederik, I can glide by myself–"

While her Magical Flying Device was out of mana, she could still glide in her Harpy form as long as she borrowed a little help from some Wind Spirits.\
But she could not remain a Harpy for much longer. Tia took a candy from her pocket and moved to bring it to her mouth… but as if aiming for that exact moment, the Monster that was once Dammer launched an all-out attack.\
Frederik brilliantly guided his spear to block the tentacles that shot towards them like bullets. He couldn't catch all of them, so he used wind to blow some of them away then knocked down the rest with his spear. The sheer impact of it all caused the candy to drop from Tia's hand.

(Crap…)

Before Tia could pull out a new candy, the Monster that was once Dammer slammed into Frederik.\
Frederik's spear grazed the Monster's shoulder, but the Monster was undeterred. With a tremendous '*thud*,' Frederik's body shook off balance just as the effects of her candies wore out.\
Her Harpy wings and talons folded in to human arms and legs. Pain, and then, weightlessness.\
Due to the force of the impact, Tia's body tumbled off of Frederik's back into freefall.\
The intense pain rendered her speechless and no songs would come out. Her arms stretched wide, but they were not wings. This was but a weak, suffocating human body. Even her Magical Flying Device had long since lost its mana.

(I'm falling.)

No matter how resilient Tia may have been, she would not be able to just walk away after falling from this height.\
Would this be how she died? She hadn't even stopped the fighting yet. Her sister sacrificed everything to save her life; how could she let that life go to waste so quickly? '*Think, think, think! Use your head, listen to your surroundings! Harpies have three weapons: songs, wings, and excellent ears, after all.*'\
Countless emotions and thoughts raced through Tia's head at a dizzying pace when, all of a sudden, her ears picked up on a certain voice.

"Tia!"

The most dependable of the Apprentices. The kind human girl who taught Tia the meaning of tools.\
But it wasn't just Ella. She heard her fellow Apprentices' voices from **all over**.

"Tiaaaa! Lever! The lever!"

She heard that voice from down below. It was Sophie.\
Next to Sophie, she heard Gerald's voice as well.

"Tia! Pull the lever!"

'*The lever?*' As if answering Tia's unspoken question, Julius and Roswitha's voices called from slightly higher up.

"Tia! The Flying Device!"

"Pull the lever now! Pull it all the way out! Now!"

And, finally, Ella's voice again.

"Tia! Believe in Rukiye!"

Tia's body moved before she could think about it.\
She reached for the lever on the Magical Flying Device. Then, she pulled it out of it's socket with all her might.\
For a brief moment, she felt a faintly hollow sensation. Tia's mana was decreasing. The Magical Flying Device was draining Tia's own mana.

"Hmph!"

Tia immediately stretched out both arms. Mana enveloped the metal wings behind her, making them glow white.\
The Harpy who had once lost her flight feathers now spread her artificial white wings and once again took to the skies.
