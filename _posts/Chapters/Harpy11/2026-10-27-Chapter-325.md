## [V11C50] Behind the Curtain

Sunset came early in the winter. A bloody red stained the western sky, casting a dark shadow on those who walked below.\
The colors of night painted the road as one man walked, trailing a long shadow behind him.\
This man was covered all over in bandages but wore fashionable clothes and a hat. The colors of dusk reflected against his glossy blond hair, making it shine the color of polished copper.\
The man suddenly came to a stop and narrowed his eyes toward the tower looming in the East.\
The Wedge Tower, known as a gathering place for mages and their knowledge, was originally a prison tower that held the worst criminals. Criminals were subjected to forced labor, and their children would be used as sacrifices to 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower.'\
Once upon a time, the man was himself held prisoner here. However, he could not have children of his own, nor was he even truly human in the first place, so he escaped before the humans could get around to killing him off.\
Thanks to that experience, the man was very familiar with the Wedge Tower's underlying structure.\
The Ancient Magical Artifact 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower' was a cradle hidden beneath what was now known as the Third Spire: Water Bubble, around the edge where the later expansions began. This tiny cradle, barely large enough for a single baby to sleep inside, formed the basis around which the Three Spires were built to protect it.\
Then, to make use of the excess mana surging from the Artifact, a Mana Forge was built. Considering the Mana Forge's presence and its location in the remote countryside, it became a refuge for mages with nowhere else to go. In time, the Wedge Tower came to be known as a mage tower, not a prison… although that was all ancient history by this point.

(I expected a lot more humans and Monsters to die than that.)

Both humans and Monsters should just die, filled with hatred and regrets. They should be covered in mud and tears, then swallowed whole by the Font of the Abyss. Kai had waited ages to witness this beautiful tragedy play out.\
But then, the Monsters surrendered to the humans, and the humans accepted that.

(Oh boy, a happy ending where everyone holds hands and sings. It makes me want to puke. Just kill each other already.)

And of all things, it was all thanks to that damned Folulutia.\
She should have been wailing in despair, distraught as she realized her own powerlessness. Who would have imagined that instead, she would kill the King who controlled fear itself and declare herself the King of Monsters? There's unexpected, and then there's just ridiculous.

(Ahh, but even so…)

The man's lips curled into a smile. Like a weight had finally lifted from his shoulders.

(The Harpies are now functionally extinct. I drove them to extinction.)

His desires finally came to fruition, but the man would not say so out loud. After all, it was in direct opposition to the wishes of his beloved Witch.\
He had worked tirelessly on this plan for many long years, and in the grand scheme of things, one could call it a success. Many humans and Monsters killed each other, the Harpies were now extinct, and the Western Wall had fallen.\
If he had one regret, it was the fate of that weak Werewolf boy — Finn.\
He was a weak, cowardly child that constantly suffered the abuses of his packmates. The man granted him the Crystal Rivet because he had expected this weak little Werewolf might be able to accomplish great things. "*Use this to take back your pride. Make your packmates regret it,*" he whispered.

—The man wanted to watch Finn, after so much abuse from his pack, return to them with a venegance.

However, Finn grew too attached to the humans. In the end, he suffered grievous wounds and the humans sealed him away. What a boring end to his story.

"Kai!!"

A cheerful voice rang out from overhead.\
The man, Kai, stopped and turned his gaze to the sky.\
A white haired girl slowly descended from the skies with a Magical Flying Device — Tia. Her limbs were human, and her feet were bare.\
The girl's bare feet softly landed on the dirt.

"Hey, Tia. Long time no see."

"Yep, it's been a very long time. Um, Kai, I have a favor to ask."

"You need some candy?"

"Yeah, candy. I used 'em all up… Now I can't turn back to Harpy form."

As she spoke, Tia waddled up to him.\
Kai paid close attention to Tia's hands. She wasn't carrying any weapons. Nor was she reaching for the lever on her Magical Flying Device.\
However, the next moment, Tia swung one of her legs at him with a sharpened rock grasped between her toes.\
Kai immediately stepped back out of the way, but the tip of the knife-like rock ripped apart Kai's shirt.\
Kai laughed bitterly.

"After all that human-play you've done, you've got pretty good at lying, haven't you, Tia?"

"I knew it…"

The pupils in her amber eyes narrowed to thin slits.\
Looking like she was evaluating her next prey, the female Harpy growled at him.

"…Navelless."

***

A Harpy is a Monster with a girl's face and body but a bird's wings and feet… but on rare occasions, a male may be born as well.\
Male Harpies are unable to reproduce, and have no wings. They lack a navel, but in all other respects their bodies are indistinguishable from humans. Thus, female Harpies refer to their male counterparts as 'Navelless.'\
Since Harpies lived in caves along the face of a sheer cliff, flightless beings had no way of living in their society. Because they couldn't reproduce as well, there was no reason to leave these males alive.\
Therefore, if a male was born, he would be summarily tossed to his death at the bottom of Breakneck Gorge.

"This is the first time I've seen a Navelless that survived. Actually, I've never even seen a male Harpy get born at Breakneck Gorge before either."

"That's right. Aside from my missing belly-button, we're no different from your average human in lifespan or physicality."

Kai pulled the bandages off that covered half of his body. Underneath, she could see the scars of what must have been the many times he was attacked by Monsters.\
A normal Harpy would recover from those wounds in no time. In fact, if they were in somewhere with enough mana density, it might just heal in the blink of an eye. Kai, however, was not so fortunate.

"…Kai, you can live just fine in both low mana density and high, right? But you can't cross the Western Wall, can you?"

"Oh, what a surprise. A little cleverer now, aren't you, Tia? And here I thought all female Harpies' minds were no different from a common bird's."

Each word out of his mouth dripped with pure hatred for female Harpies. It was only natural.\
His birth was not celebrated. From the moment he hatched, he was due to be killed by female Harpies. No wonder he hated Tia as well.

(I just happened to be born female somewhere where females were in control. And Kai was born male.)

Some time back, Tia had said some words to Rukiye's father that now came back to haunt her.

"*Mister, aren't you glad you were born in a place where the males were on top?*"

When she said that, she must have been making quite a horrid expression.\
Tia bit her lip, and Kai laughed coldly at her.

"You sure are asking a lot of questions, Tia. I thought you didn't care who I was?"

"…That's right."

Tia wasn't interested in who Kai was, so she had never thought to ask.\
Even now, she was not asking out of simple curiosity.

"There's some things I need to know, even if I don't want to. As Queen Folulutia of the Harpies, this is something I can't shy away from."

Kai chuckled. His laugh was cold and dry.\
He had a habit of laughing when he wasn't even happy. Just like humans did.

"I see, then listen well, Folulutia. Listen to the tale of a male Harpy you birds tried so hard to stamp out."

***

"I was picked up by family of farmers, and raised as a human, but the fact that I had no navel earned me no shortage of grief."

Humans knew very little about Monsters. Besides, male Harpies were usually killed just as they hatched anyway.\
The humans that took Kai in did not know that a Navelless child meant that he was a male Harpy. Regardless, they understood that Kai was not normal.

"As time went on, the humans started to call me the 'Monster Boy.' '*Hey Monster Boy, I bet you won't die even if we don't feed you.*' '*This won't kill you right, Monster Boy?*' '*You're a Monster Boy and all, so…*' … Well, that was over a century ago at this point, so there might be some things I don't remember. Nevertheless, it's a fact that I have almost no memory of ever being treated as a human."

Those around him all looked at him with suspicion and targeted him with unjust violence day in and day out.\
When Kai was a little past the age of ten, a Greater Monster appeared near his village. It was a man-eating Monster with a humanoid form that sported bulging muscles and horns on its head.\
The humans searched for a sacrifice to offer this Monster, and they chose the Navelless Kai.

"This Monster, the one they called the Ogre, was more than thrilled to bite down on me… but he just as quickly spat me back out. It seems like I wasn't very edible to them."

The Ogre told Kai: "*Your flesh tastes like Monsters.*"\
Then, after examining Kai's body more thoroughly, said:

—"*Oh, so you're a Navelless?*"

"That Monster taught me everything I needed to know about the race of Monsters known as Harpies, and how those Harpies killed their males at birth."

Driven out of the human flock, he was not strong enough to survive among the Monsters. Worse, his own race saw male Harpies like him only as things to be killed.\
He found himself unable to live among either humans or Monsters.\
His life from then on was a waking nightmare. Even he could not remember how he managed to survive through it all. At one point he was arrested as a criminal. He'd snuck into a human village to steal food, and when a stray dog bit him, he bit that dog back until it died. He lived alone, cursing humans, Monsters, and everything in the world around him.

"And then one day, I caught sight of a Harpy."

The first time he laid eyes on the Harpy, Kai immediately tried to hide himself. He knew Harpies would try to kill him.\
But then, after seeing her for a few days in a row, he slowly became curious.\
Obviously he ought to have stayed away from something that could kill him, but he just wanted to know. He wanted to learn about the Harpy… Maybe it was because Kai himself was a Harpy?\
Unable to fit in among human towns or Monster herds, Kai chased after the Harpy in pursuit of his roots.\
Then, he saw it: that beautiful, dreamlike garden. And the Witch who lived within it.

***

The Harpy looked to be nearing the end of her life. She wobbled as she flew, bidding farewell to the Witch.\

"*Do you wish for a longer life?*"

"*I don't need that. After all, I've lived plenty already. I got to fly a whole lot, sing, and lay my eggs.*"

The moribund Harpy smiled brightly, and said:

"*More importantly, if you ever meet any other Harpies like me, I want you to get along with them, okay? I'll leave a song about my favorite friend, too. So that the Harpies will all come to like you.*"

When the Harpy flew off, Kai entered the Witch's garden.

"*I– I'm a Harpy too! Will you like me too! Please… please!!"
