## [V11C40] A Corrosive Voice

Blood dripped from the face of Dammer, Extermination Office mage, as he dragged the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Ignatius, the Severer' behind him.\
His eyes darted every which way, searching for his sword's next prey.

"Damn you… damn you… I'll kill you all… damn Monsters."

He swung the sword at an approaching Kobold. Its tip only barely scratched the surface, leaving only a shallow cut, but it did not matter.\
Any Monster cut by 'Ignatius, the Severer' would never be able to recover from their wounds. Even the tiniest cut could be fatal. Ignatius made sure of it.\
While 'Ignatius, the Severer' drank the Monster's blood, its voice echoed in Dammer's head.

"*KILLKILLKILLKILLTHEMONSTERSKILLTHEMALLKILLKILLKILL*"

From the moment he picked up the blade, the echoes of that voice never ceased in Dammer's mind.

***

At first, Dammer made his way to the center of campus alone, determined to get his revenge on the black Werewolf that humiliated him earlier on the north end of the Wedge Tower.\
What awaited him was the sight of that Werewolf he was planning to kill getting sealed away. Why were those Apprentice brats wasting there time with that nonsense? They pissed him off. Dammer would have been just fine beating that thing to death on his own.\
But before Dammer could boil over with rage, that *thing* appeared.\
The King of Monsters spread fear everywhere it went. Just being nearby, just looking at it, was enough for this thing to kill a man with fear. The moment he laid eyes on it, Dammer's feet stopped cold. It wasn't just his feet, either. Everything from the top of his head to his fingertips was overcome with an inescapable fear so intense that even breathing felt like too tall a task.\
The terror was so overwhelming that his body longed for death to escape the fear.\
And yet all that fear was blown away by that air-headed white-haired little girl — Tia — singing a stupid little song. She was supposed to be back at the village with all the other Apprentices, but for whatever reason these Apprentices just kept popping up everywhere he looked.\
He had no idea what their deal was, but it worked in Dammer's favor.\
Tia had already realized that the scar on Dammer's face was not the Primordial Beast's mark. She likely saw through his whole ruse — how he scarred himself to regain his honor after leaving his whole team for dead.\
So he figured he could take advantage of the fog of war and take Tia out when no one was looking.\
When the King of Monsters left for the Third Spire with Fiene, Daughter of God, Tia followed after them. Dammer was about to chase after her as well, but then he noticed the longsword lying on the ground — the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Ignatius, the Severer.'

(If I had this, I could kill any Monster!)

And it just so happened that the aforementioned Primordial Beast was right there.\
The last time he encountered this beast, it cut through his Extermination Office colleagues in the blink of an eye, wiping out his squad before he knew it.\
It had a tough hide, abnormal strength, and overwhelmingly keen battle instincts. There was just no beating it. Every aspect of this creature was designed to kill humans.\
So of course Dammer ran. Running for his life was only natural. He used his wounded comrades as shields and ran as fast as he could.\
Behind him, he heard the Primordial Beast's disappointed voice.

"*What a disgrace. You're not even worth killing.*"

Regardless, Dammer survived and, by carving that X-shaped scar on his own face, obtained the distinction of a man acknowledged by the Primordial Beast.

(I can kill it… With 'Ignatius, the Severer,' I can kill it now!)

The Primordial Beast was curled up in a ball right in front of him, appearing to be asleep. It would be a piece of cake to just walk right up and kill the bastard.\
Using the cover of the trees and bushes to his advantage, Dammer circled around and approached the Primordial Beast. So long as he got a cut in the Beast's back, no matter how small the wound was, he would win. He alone would have the glory of killing the infamous Primordial Beast!\
The moment he grabbed Ignatius' hilt, a voice echoed in Dammer's head. A deep, hushed man's voice.

"*KILLTHEMONSTERSKILLTHEMALLKILLKILLKILLTHEMKILLKILLKILL*"

The voice unsettled Dammer the same way a bug flying in his ear would. Thanks to this noise, he had a much harder time hearing his surroundings. Truly a nuisance.\
Someone had told him before that Ancient Magical Artifacts had a personality and could talk to you on occasion.

(So this… is 'Ignatius, the Severer's voice!)

He'd heard that 'Ignatius, the Severer' wasn't picky about its wielders unlike other Artifacts, but he didn't know it could be so pushy.\
Was Headmaster Möbius hearing this voice all the time as he cut through hordes of Monsters? That had to take some serious mental fortitude.

(Damn, this voice is pissing me off — KILLKILL — Yeah, yeah I get it — KILLKILL — Just shut up already. I'm gonna kill them anywKILLKILLKILL)

His own thoughts slowly melded with those of 'Ignatius, the Severer.'\
Dammer never knew that its previous wielder, Headmaster Möbius, had been able to use it flawlessly while keeping Ignatius' will cleanly separated from his own. It was something that was only possible because he was Headmaster Möbius, the genius swordsman.

(Kill… the Primordial Beast. I'm gonna killKILLKILL…)

"Yo."

The sleeping beast spoke, as if talking to himself.

"Are you going to do it or not?"

Beads of sweat dripped down his palms.\
The Primordial Beast knew. It knew that Dammer was hiding in these bushes.

(No, it's fine. It shouldn't know that I have 'Ignatius, the Severer' with me…)

"Just one stab with **that** and you can kill me, right? Well, you better make sure you don't miss — before I eat your face off, that is."

His whole body screamed at him: '*You can't win!*'\
He couldn't win. He could never win against this creature. The moment Dammer lifted this sword, the Monster's fangs would bite his arm clean off.\
A vision flashed before his eyes of his sword-arm landing unceremoniously on the dirt.\
Dammer forced his shaky legs to move, leaving this place. That's right, he left. Even though this was his one big chance to take down one of the most infamous Monsters.\
The voices in his ears grew louder. 'Ignatius, the Severer' was irate. 

"*KILLTHEMONSTERSKILLKILLKILL*"

(Shut up, okay? I got it, KILLKILL, I just need to KillKILL the King of Monsters and I'll —KILL — be a hero — KILLKILLKILL)

There was a feature to the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Ignatius, the Severer' that not many knew about.\
This sword, which was forged for the express purpose of killing Monsters, could interfere with its wielder's mind if that wielder tried to avoid a battle with a Monster.\
Since it had until now been in the possession of Headmaster Möbius, who was eager to fight any Monster he saw, Ignatius had been relatively calm.\
But now Dammer just ran away from a battle with the formidable Primordial Beast.\
From that point forward, the Monster-killing sword went on a quiet rampage.

***

'*I can't stand this damn voice. I've got to kill all the Monsters or it won't go away.*' Dammer struck at every Monster he could see. Kobolds, Werewolves, insects or trees — anything he spotted he would cut until the pieces stopped moving.\
Dammer wasn't exactly skilled with longswords. He could use one in a pinch, but he much preferred knives or whips.\
Fortunately, 'Ignatius, the Severer' could help him with that. By working its way into Dammer's mind, it subtly helped guide his movements.\
As a result, Dammer cut through one Monster after another with the form of a master. It wasn't long before it started going to his head. He never knew that even his sense of confidence was Ignatius' doing.\
He killed, kill, and killed again. Then, delighted by the death he had wrought, Dammer laid eyes on the Third Spire: Water Bubble.

There, he saw it. The white-winged Harpy revealing her true form.

"Nothing to worry about, everyone! Did you forget who I am!?"

Hütter, dangling beneath Tia's feet, projected his voice.

"This is, of course, chantless illusion magic."

"*NO!*" 'Ignatius, the Severer' screamed at him. "*THAT IS A HARPY. A MONSTER. KILL IT.*"\
Upon hearing that voice, the corners of Dammer's mouth lifted into a smile.

He'd always hated that girl. But now the girl he'd planned to kill anyway turned out to be a Monster the whole time.\
If she was a Monster, then there was nothing to worry about. He was justified. Attacking her was the *right* thing to do.

(KILLkillKILLkillKILLkill…)

He was in the right. He was about to do the right thing.\
Dammer wandered about the gardens before finally catching sight of Tia.\
Hütter, Wren, and Tia were whispering about something, but then they split up and ran in separate ways with purpose. This was his chance.\
Dammer's body, now little more than a man-shaped ball of murderous rage, moved with ideal form on Ignatius' command. Dammer's feet carried him faster than he ever thought possible, and his arms lifted the longsword with strength far greater than the limits of his muscles.

"Die, Monster."
