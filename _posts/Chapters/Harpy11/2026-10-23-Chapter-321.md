## [V11C46] Super Mega Pretty Boy Cannon

"Tia!! It's time for the Super Mega Pretty Boy Cannon!"

Roswitha heard Wren's shout as she stood on the roof of the First Spire: White Smoke.

"Geez, somebody's getting ahead of himself. There's nothing 'pretty boy' about this."

Even so, considering all the work Wren had done running around and coordinating between the Apprentices, it might as well be a Pretty Boy Cannon after all.\
Roswitha closed her eyes to see through the watery fish she sent flying earlier.

"…Good, Tia's noticed the fish."

Beside Roswitha, Julius started chanting. His was a long, long incantation. Long enough that his face was drenched in sweat.\
Roswitha pulled out three sticks she'd taken from the White Smoke's preparation room.\
Both Julius and Roswitha — the Apprentice's resident titans of Modern and Classical Magecraft respectively — voiced their incantations side by side, though Roswitha was first to finish.

"*Thy irrational devotion, the rain that dwells within thee, o fish who lost thine arms… burrow forth!*"

She tossed the three sticks ahead of her. Each stick was soon enveloped in a cocoon of water, taking the form of a fish. Those three fish then clumped together as tight as they could.\
Then, a tiny red lion, Flame Spirit Agniol, jumped atop the fish.

"We'll do it with a bang, Young master Julius! Little Roswitha!"

In place of responding, since Julius was still in the middle of his incantation, he just lifted the corners of his mouth in a smile.\
Then, he lifted the staff he held and thrust it forwards.

"In the name of Julius Löwenich, open the gate!"

Red particles gathered before Julius, taking the form of a large gate.\
With a *Whoosh*, a blast of hot wind blew from the gate, rustling the pair's clothes and hair. Atop the watery fish, Agniol puffed out her cheeks in delight.

"Emerge wreathed in the fires of ceremony, O Spirit King of Flame, Flemme Brem!"

The most powerful spell in Modern Magecraft: Spirit King Summoning. It was Julius' trump card, but one with extremely limited use cases.\
First of all, the incantation took far too much time. Then, even if he did manage to open the gate, he would have a hard time actually hitting the target with the Spirit King's power he summoned.\
While he could hardly say he had perfect control over this magecraft, there was one way to ensure he hit the target without fail.\
The mana emanating from the gate took the form of a gargantuan fireball. At the same time, Roswitha shouted.

"I'm sending you out! Try not to fall off, Agniol!"

"You got it! This way, Spirit King~!"

Controlling her watery fish, Roswitha sent Agniol flying rapidly towards the Third Spire: Water Bubble.\
Then Agniol, riding atop those fish, used her own mana to draw in the flames of the Spirit King Summoning.\
The giant fireball chased after the fish, hot on Agniol's heels. Eventually, both came to a stop around the newer parts of the Third Spire, particularly near the Management Office, where the Mana Forge was located.\
The moment Agniol and the fireball touched, Agniol's tiny lion body started to expand. Her legs grew longer and her mane billowed in the heat as she growled loud enough to shake the entire campus.\
—Long ago, there was once a Great Spirit so feared it was spoken of alongside dragons as a living natural disaster.\
Drenched in the Spirit King's flames, the Flame Spirit Agniol's overwhelming power was now returned to its original glory.

"Wait! The Water Bubble contains countless priceless tomes that…"

Director Hegelich's screams of desperation would not ruin Agniol's good mood.

"Here we go~! Take that!"

Spirit King Summoning involved using the gate to call forth a tiny fraction of the Spirit King's power, then shaping that power into the mage's desired form.\
When doing so, there was an extra option available only to mages who had contracted a Greater Spirit: strengthening one's contracted spirit by granting them the Spirit King's mana.\
A Greater Spirit, with power and precision the likes of which no human could match, could utilize the Spirit King's unfathomable mana to it's full extent — but of course, this was only if the mage trusted their contracted spirit implicitly. Julius now put that power to the test for the first time in his life.\
In tandem with Agniol's delighted shouts, a blazing inferno rocketed out, scorching the Font of the Abyss and the Mana Forge at its center.

***

Just as Agniol came flying towards the Third Spire atop the watery fish, Tia made her move.\
Still singing her song to banish away the fear, she dove toward Fiene who stood beside the Font with arm plunged into it, and grabbed the girl's shoulders with her talons. If Tia wanted to, Harpies like her had enough strength in their talons to fly off with a whole calf.

"—Tia!"

Fiene yelped as Tia came to her in a very different way than she was imagining.\
Her talons alone would have been plenty powerful enough to crush Fiene's little body right here. However, Tia suppressed that impulse and flew away from the Third Spire.\
Then, once she got close enough to where Otto was standing, she tossed Fiene at him.

"Kya–!"

Fiene let out the briefest scream before Otto's quick reflexes caught her in an embrace.\
In Otto's arms, Fiene reached a hand out towards Tia. Tia, instead, turned her back on Fiene and said:

"You can't save anyone. You're the one who needs saving."

***

At the heart of a raging inferno, a huge lion with a red mane roared with glee.

"I'm not holding back this time! I'll boil every little bit of this stupid Abyss!"

The main reason Julius hadn't used Spirit King Summoning up to this point was because it was just so powerful that it would level everything in the vicinity to a heap of smoldering ashes. Besides, there was a decent chance he'd get caught up in the flames himself and burn to death.\
However, this time he had someone who could restrain Agniol's full power.

"Rose, now!"

On Wren's cue, Rose maintained his Rose Fortress while activating a different sort of magecraft.\
It was nothing more than an ordinary defensive barrier. Only it was built with orders of magnitude more mana than usual.\
The transparent wall wrapped all the way around the Third Spire, preventing the flames from spreading out of control.\
This man was one of the Seven Sages, one of his country's greatest mages, but even among them he was regarded as an anomaly for his mana reserves. In addition to his own mana, he deployed this defensive barrier with mana he'd absorbed through his Rose Fortress, creating a barricade that could contain both the spreading Abyss and Agniol's flames.\
Inside of Rose's barrier, the inferno went wild with all the mana it had been fed. The raging flames focused particularly on burning up the Font of the Abyss and the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower.'

"Wow…"

Wren stared with bulging eyes as Rose gave a slightly cocky smile.

"If I'm fighting to protect people, no one's gonna beat me!"

***

Hütter stood in watched with a certain level of exasperation as the Third Spire: Water Bubble crumbled within that ultra-thick barrier and cage of rose vines.

(I know it was my idea and all, but…)

Hütter stopped his glasses from sliding off his nose and, while he was at it, clutched at his stomach.

(Who said to take it this far!!?)

The plan Hütter gave to Tia and Wren was supposed to be just a rough, big-picture kind of thing.\
To be honest, he didn't even think it was possible to destroy the Font of the Abyss or 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower.'

(Julius can use Spirit King Summoning? Damn, that kid really is a genius, huh… And I'm surprised to see Julius and Roswitha working together, too… There's also Rose but… man that guy's so inhuman at this point I don't even know what to say…)

And moving all of these geniuses into place was that little Wren.\
In such a short time, Wren was able to meet up with the other Apprentices, put together a plan, and communicate the precise details to all of them. No wonder he called it the 'pretty boy cannon.' Even though he had no hand in the attack itself, this whole grand finishing blow was ultimately Wren's achievement.\
Meanwhile, countless tomes that recorded the ancient knowledge of the Wedge Tower were burned in the fires and crushed by falling rubble alongside the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower.' The mages who'd supported the Wedge Tower all this way raised wails of lament. Director Hegelich was particularly inconsolable.

(Director Hegelich… Uh… Sorry, dude.)

He did feel a little bad about it, but this did tip the scales of battle in the humans' favor.\
The Font of the Abyss was buried in rubble, while 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower' was destroyed. With this, Fiene, Daughter of God, found herself powerless and all out of options.\
Now they just needed to wrap this up.\
Having deposited the Daughter of God safely in Otto's arms, Tia spread her artificial wings and rocketed straight for the King of Monsters.

Tia would slay the King of Monsters. That would be the last step towards ending this battle once and for all.

***

*Post-script:*

This battle technique of giving the power of Spirit King Summoning to one's contracted spirit can, by harnessing power and precision no human could possibly muster, potentially be an incredibly effective tactic that ends battles in one fell swoop. \
However, because it rests entirely on the contracted spirit, only those with a deep and trusting relationship with their contracted spirit would ever do this.\
That last part is the reason why, despite there being a certain person who fulfilled the requirements of '*using Spirit King Summoning*' and '*having a contract with a Greater Spirit*,' no one used this tactic in the previous series.
