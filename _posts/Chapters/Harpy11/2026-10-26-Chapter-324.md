## [V11C49] Paradise Made to Order

Once, Tia herself had been a Monster kept in a human cage. That episode left Tia with rage and shame that would never leave her as long as she lived… And now she was ordering all Monsters to submit to humans in much the same fashion. It was nothing if not heartless.\
She had all sorts of complaints bubbling up to the surface, but Tia forced those thoughts back down to the pits of her stomach. She was fully prepared to accept the hatred and resentment of all Monsters.\
The Monsters did not take up the white flags immediately. After all, none of them even knew what it meant to wave the white flag.\
At that moment, two pigeons came flapping up to her. in their beaks, they held a white handkerchief.\
After saying a quick "Thanks," Tia grabbed the handkerchief with the human-like hand on the tip of her wings.

"Like this! Wave the white cloth! That means you surrender!"

Teaching wasn't easy. You had to be persistent and repeat your message over and over. The more she did it, it dawned on her just how impressive Mr. Hütter was.\
In her time living among humans, one thing she noticed was that a great many humans feared death and had an obsession with living. They would do anything just to live a little longer. Just to extend their life expectancy even the slightest bit. They were, bluntly, addicted to life.\
Meanwhile, death was a much more familiar concept to Monsters than it was to humans. Short-lived Lesser Monsters in particular lived while fully accepting death at any point. It wasn't so much 'giving up' as it was 'just the way things were' in their minds.\
Monsters were not addicted to life in the same way humans were. Thus, there would surely be those who refused to surrender.\
What would they do with the rest of their life after surrendering? Was there any meaning in a life under human control? Was it not enough to lose and to die? — There would certainly be those who thought along those lines. Tia, a Monster herself, could not bring herself to argue against those points.\
Nonetheless, she shouted.

"Those of you who want to delay your extinction by just a little bit longer, wave the white flag!"

There had to be some out there. Monsters like Finn who hated fighting. Monsters like Charonlala who wanted to avoid extinction.\
A couple Kobolds copied Tia and waved the white cloths, but their faces made it clear they had no real clue what it meant. After them, some relatively more intelligent Lamias, Monsters with the upper body of a woman and the lower body of a snake, held up white cloths and waved them around as well.\
One by one, white flags popped up here and there throughout the crowd.\
Many of these Monsters were less intelligent than Tia. It would take some time before they understood the true meaning of surrender.\
There would likely be some crafty ones who feigned surrender, then attacked the humans when their guard was down. To nip that problem in the bud, Tia shouted:

"If you want to live, then wave that white flag! However, I won't stand for deception! If you pretend to surrender then harm humans anyway, I, Queen Folulutia, will rip your head off with my talons! Just like I did to this old King here!!"

Tia lifted the severed head in her talons to emphasize her point as she flew around.\
She felt the humans staring daggers at her. There was no hiding her true identity from them any longer.

(I think it's about time.)

Tia tossed another candy in her mouth to maintain her Harpy form, then turned to address the humans on the ground.

"I demand to speak with the humans' leader! Who here can speak on behalf of the Wedge Tower!?"

It went without saying who responded to Tia's call.

"Tis I! I am the Acting Headmaster of the Wedge Tower, Adelheid Sevil Lamea Krewing!"

Sevil projected her voice upwards, surreptitiously promoting herself from 'Next Headmaster' to 'Acting Headmaster' in the process.\
Tia gracefully glided down in front of Sevil, holding the King of Monsters' head.\
Naturally, she also held that white handkerchief.

"I Folulutia, the new King of Monsters, offer my surrender to humanity. I ask that you accept me and those Monsters who surrender with me. I leave my fate and the fate of my fellow Monsters in your hands as the new Headmaster."

"Very well, I shall accept your surrender. … Attention all Monsters! Those who wish to surrender, wave the white flag! If you do not have one, kneel with your hands in the air!"

***

"Wait, aren't those two just classmates?" Someone wondered aloud.\
Hütter, who was busy passing out white cloths along with the Apprentices, whispered voicelessly.

(That's right, you're right on the money.)

Essentially, Hütter made Tia do the same thing Sevil had already done.\
Sevil usurped the top spot on the human side without anyone asking.\
And now Tia usurped the top spot on the Monsters' side.\
Once these two are accomplished, the rest is just a race condition. Tia would declare a surrender on the Monsters' behalf, and Sevil would be the one to accept it. He hadn't had enough time to run this plan by Sevil, but Hütter was confident that Sevil would play along as soon as the situation called for it.\
Naturally, there would be Monsters who did not go along with the surrender. They couldn't save everyone. That was the gist of this plan: a resolution that worked out nicely for the humans while also letting some Monsters maybe not go extinct.\
But to make this plan come to fruition, there was one who absolutely had to die. That being the King of Monsters.\
When Hütter finished passing out the white cloths, he walked up to Tia and stared in shock at the King of Monsters' head she held in her wings.\
The King of Monsters' white eyelashes were blinking up and down. Despite being reduced to just a head, it was still alive.\
Sevil must have noticed as well, as she whispered:

"To still be alive even as just a head… What terrifying resilience."

"Even so… As a fellow Monster I can kinda tell. It won't last much longer."

The white-winged Harpy lifted the King of Monsters' head with the girlish hands on the ends of her wings. Hütter silently mused that it looked like a beautiful painting of a haunting scene.\
Just as a human would treat a priceless heirloom, Tia handled the head delicately as she turned it around to speak with it.

"Do you have any last wishes?"

"…To hear Fiene's words again."

As if answering the King of Monsters' summons, a young girl walked up to them.\
With straight black hair and pale skin that had never seen the light of day, the girl acted every bit the graceful and holy Daughter of God she professed to be as she stood before Tia.\
Tia kept her expression neutral as she turned the King's head to face Fiene.

"I ask the Daughter of God: Now that the Font of the Abyss is gone, where will we go after death?"

Fiene reached out a hand to the King of Monsters and stroked its bloodstained cheeks.

"A paradise just for Monsters. Go there, and your suffering will disappear."

"What sort of place is this paradise? If I went there, could I… could I find the Crystal Dragon I was never able to mourn?"

Hütter believed he was the only one who noticed that Fiene was at a loss for words.\
Considering everything the King of Monsters had said so far, the third-rate con man who excelled at picking up on hints and not much else pondered on the King's goals… or perhaps he should say 'prayers.'

(The King of Monsters wants an afterlife… Really, it wants a *story* to make that afterlife feel real. A story it can believe in. All this stuff about religion and gods was just to give that story some backing.)

Monsters lacked the ability to make things themselves. So they needed a human to build an 'afterlife' that fit their desires. That human was Fiene.\
If the Font of the Abyss was still around, perhaps Fiene's tale of a paradise might have had some persuasiveness to it.\
But now the Font of the Abyss was gone. The instant the King of Monsters was decapitated, all of the black fear it had spread ceased and 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower' fell completely silent.\
This was a huge blow to the 'story' Fiene was trying to tell.

(Your improv skills are lacking, rookie con woman.)

In contrast, this here third-rate con man was an expert at making up bullshit on the spot, if nothing else.\
The King of Monsters' death was a critical piece of his plan to have the Monsters surrender. This King had to die for the sake of Hütter's plan.\
So offering some comfort was the least he could do.

"In the paradise for Monsters, there is a great big road. If you wait along the side of that road, something important to you might just pass by."

He spoke with the stern candor of a priest while the depths of his voice carried a gentle grace as Hütter asked the dying King a question.

"The Crystal Dragon was important to you, right?"

"…Yes."

"Did that dragon ever say what they found beautiful?"

"…Spring flowers. They enjoyed those little yellow flowers that bloomed by the pond."

"Well those same flowers are blooming in paradise. You should pick some of those flowers and wait for the one you want to meet. In paradise, there's always a pleasant breeze and the streams always run with the water you enjoy most."

There was no guaranteeing that a Monster had the same concept of a pleasant breeze or tasty water as humans did.\
So the con man took care to choose his words carefully as he built the image of a paradise for Monsters in the dying King's head.\
Hütter glanced at Fiene.\
This girl, raised as the Daughter of God at the whims of adults, stared at Hütter in shock.

(Just shut up and watch, missy.)

Hütter placed a finger on his lips to tell her to "Shh," then continued.

"Do you like music?"

"Music… Yes, I listened to songs the humans made. The Crystal Dragon and I… We sang O Rose together…"

Hütter shot Tia a quick glance.\
Tia nodded and, ever so quietly, started to sing.

"*O rose, O rose, with you, I will go anywhere. O rose, O rose, with you, I will be forever.*"

The King of Monsters' white eyelids gradually closed, as if savoring the beautiful melody.\
Behind those eyelids, what sort of paradise was the dying King's mind drawing up?

"Can you see the spring flowers blooming in paradise? Can you see that long, wide road?"

"…Yes."

"I'm sure the one you lost will be there soon enough."

Long ago, he pretended to be a priest to an old, dying man he met in the course of his travels.\
He listened to the old man's regrets and nodded along with the appropriate words of sympathy. "*God will forgive everything. For you, who has confessed so honestly to everything, the gates of heaven will surely open.*" He told the man in a soft voice, then shortly after the old man passed away.\
As he watched that gentle dying face, he wondered to himself '*What the hell am I doing?*'\
Yet nonetheless, he was now repeating the same thing he did back then. He truly was a rotten con man to his core.

"Rest in peace, King of Monsters."

The head held in Tia's arms would not open its eyes any longer.
