## [V11C38] Teacher, Help Me

While Tia coasted on the wind currents with Hütter dangling beneath her, she wasn't flapping her wings at all.\
The singing he heard from time to time must have been her way to generate wind forces. Each time Tia sang, tiny particles of light would glimmer, and with it the wind would shift ever so slightly.

(Oh, so those must be Lesser Wind Spirits then…)

Monsters and Spirits typically had a hostile relationship, but things were different in the specific case of Harpies and Wind Spirits. It was said that Wind Spirits were willing to lend their power to Harpies that offered them their songs.\
Which meant that Tia was in a condition where she wouldn't even be able to glide properly were it not for the support of these Wind Spirits.\
In the time that Hütter spent thinking back on Tia's actions thus far and putting the pieces together, the ground quickly approached.\
Just as Hütter's feet made contact with solid ground, Tia landed unceremoniously on her stomach.\
Unlike an angel's wings, a Harpy's wings did not grow out of her back. Her arms were, in and of themselves, her wings.\
As Hütter stared at Tia lying face first on the ground, she suddenly curled up in pain, her arms and legs convulsing violently.

"Agyuuu! Uuu… Uuh… uu…"

"What's wrong? Are you hurt!?"

Hütter's concerned shouts received no response. He could only watch as the girl writhed on the ground, her frail arms and the wings attached to them transforming before his eyes.\
As if watching a flower bloom in reverse, the brilliant white feathers folded in on themselves, collapsing into her slender arms. The talons on her feet likewise scrunched up and a layer of pale skin stretched over them until they eventually resembled human feet.\
This process of transforming a Harpy's wings and feet by forcing them into a human form seemed to involve quite a lot of pain. Tia's face was drenched in sweat and her breaths were ragged as she turned to face Hütter once more.

"…Mr. Hütter…"

Hütter saw his own face reflected in her amber eyes.

"Did you know… what I am?"

Hütter closed his eyes and slowly exhaled through his nose.

"I had considered it as a possibility. If Finn is a Monster, then it would be a plausible guess."

This wasn't a lie, but it was hardly the truth either.\
In the first place, the closest thing that crossed Hütter's mind was when the possibility of Finn being a monster came up. He simply thought '*Well, I guess anyone could turn out to be a Monster at this rate, huh? I wouldn't want to be caught off guard if someone randomly revealed they were a Monster the whole time.*' At that point he never would have even dreamed that Tia could have been one of them.\
But when he saw the way Fiene and Tia looked at each other — when he saw the unbridled hatred Tia displayed towards Fiene — He realized there had to be something more to it.

"Mr. Hütter… You knew, and you didn't tell anyone?"

"I didn't get the feeling that you were a spy or anything. Besides… Wren and Sevil already knew, didn't they?"

Hütter asked with confidence, to which Tia gave a tiny nod.\
Of course there were tons of things that Hütter didn't know. It might be best to clear some things up now while he had the chance.

"So, what's your connection to Otto's daughter… Fiene, I mean."

"…Möbius cut off my flight feathers, and gave me to Fiene as her songbird."

"Ah…" Hütter lightly vocalized.\
A lot of pieces started to fit together in his mind. Why would the famously cautious Möbius and Miriam bring Tia in to such a place? Hütter had a reasonably strong guess.\
Fiene's mother, Satie, was said to be a talented singer.\
Miriam must have wanted Fiene to grow up with the gift of song too. But her throat was in no condition to sing herself. So she made an exception. She let her have a songbird. The only one that could survive in such thick mana density: a Harpy.

"…And I take it Headmaster Möbius used 'Ignatius, the Severer' to clip those flight feathers?"

"Wow, how did you know that?"

"If it's not regenerating, that's why."

She was a Harpy who had lost her flight feathers and, thus, her means of flight. That was why Tia had been so obsessed with learning how to fly.\
Now that he knew that, he could understand why Tia was at the Wedge Tower. For a Monster like Tia who couldn't go beyond the Western Wall, the Wedge Tower would have been her only hope of learning another way to fly.\
As Hütter worked it all out in his mind, Tia (still lying on the ground) turned her eyes up nervously towards him.

"Mr. Hütter…"

Tia usually never hesitated to say what was on her mind, but now her face twisted uncomfortably when she asked weakly:

"…Are you afraid of me?"

"For what it's worth, I don't feel like I'm in any danger being with you right now. After all, you came flying to save me, didn't you?"

Sure, Hütter couldn't claim to understand all of Tia's situation or what she was thinking.\
But in that moment, it was a fact that Tia was willing to expose her identity in order to save Hütter from that newborn Monster's attack.\
It wasn't just that one time either. When he was in the clutches of the Vampire Jill, Tia did everything she could to rescue him.\
If Tia truly was on the Monsters' side, there would have been no reason she couldn't have left Hütter to his fate.

"Thanks for saving me, by the way."

"Pefu…" …A little sound escaped Tia's throat.\
Her eyebrows shot up, and the corners of her eyes turned a little red.

"Mr. Hütter… I…"

Her amber eyes wavered, unable to focus on anything.\
Tia searched for the words, trying to find the right way to express her dilemma. It looked like her search wasn't going very well.\
Hütter had a guess as to why.

"Tia, I won't ask whether you're on the side of the humans or the Monsters. It's fine whichever you choose. You could even choose neither. "

"…Really?"

(It goes for me too, after all.)

Hütter was nominally on the humans' side, but he didn't really have anything to do with the Wedge Tower in the first place. He had no qualms begging for his life when Jill showed up.\
If someone asked him which side he was on, he would have a hard time saying he was anything more than a con man who happened to pass by.

(I bet Tia is conflicted on what side she should be on.)

Then, Hütter thought it would be best not to force her to answer that question.

"It's not my place to criticize your choices. Instead, I just have one thing to ask you: when you came here, what was it that you wanted to accomplish?"

"I… wanted to stop the fighting."

As long as she just stayed put and acted like a good Apprentice Mage, Tia wouldn't have been in any danger. And yet she came here anyway.\
To stop the battle between humans and Monsters.\
Tears welled up in Tia's eyes.

"Mr. Hütter… I don't get it… I thought real hard about it, but I still don't get it…"

"What don't you get?"

Hütter asked just like he would in their usual lessons.\
Tia spoke carefully and awkwardly, yet with desperation.

"The Monsters can't beat humanity. Even if the Monsters win today, an even bigger force of humans is going to come and kill them. Their best choice is to go back to the Crystal Territory… but Sis and the others just won't listen."

He was impressed. She'd grown.\
He'd only known her for a short while, but Tia had put in the effort to get a much deeper understanding of humans.

"Sis is angry at humanity. Because humans killed all the other Harpies. But Frederik is angry too. A lot of people Frederik knew were killed. Frederik himself went through some scary things as well."

"Uh-huh."

"I… feel like I should hate Frederik for killing all those Harpies? But… I don't know… I just can't hate him like that… even though I'm a Harpy. But still, I understand how Sis feels. I'm a Harpy too, after all. Of course we'd want to take revenge for killing so many of us… but I care too much about humans now… and not just because I need them to reproduce…"

With the same difficulty one would expect if she was coughing up blood, Tia expounded on her dilemma.\
Tia could understand how humans think, but she was decidedly not human herself. Nor did she ever want to be human.\
And that, precisely, was the source of her pain.

"You told me before, Mr. Hütter, that whenever thinking wasn't enough, I needed to do some research. But I've done my research. I got all the information I could find, but the more I learned about everyone, the harder it got… Everyone has a reason to fight. Everyone has a reason to be angry."

Tia learned all of this while still being a Harpy at heart. She learned all about the humans' schemes and the emotions they carried.\
As a Harpy, she had grown attached to humans. When he thought about the way Tia looked when she was with Sevil and Wren, he could tell that she wasn't just acting.

"I don't want Lala to die… but I don't want Frederik to die. I don't want you or the Apprentices to die either…"

If Tia said she wanted to be a human, this conversation would have been much easier.\
He could have just said "*You're already plenty human enough, let's work together! If you want to save your sister, let's just take her and sneak out of here!*" or something like that.\
But that would not work here.

"What do I do? I don't know… I just don't know…"

The ground beneath her was sopping wet with her tears.\
Tia's whole body shook and, with the faintest voice, she said:

"Help me, Mr. Hütter…"

Hütter pressed his hand against his forehead and stared at the sky.

(Ahhh—……)

The third-rate con man Smoke Fox was… how should he put it… weak to this sort of thing. Really, really weak. He just couldn't help the urge to be the hero. That's what made him a third-rate.\
Hütter took his hand off of his forehead and addressed Tia.

"Tia, why do you think humans go to war?"

"…Because there's something they want?"

"Oh, good answer. Sounds like you've been thinking about this. But if you just want something, it would be easier to get some money or valuables together and convince the other guy to let you have it, right?"

"…So why go to war then?"

His answer to that was a little tricky, but it was the truth. Reality.

"The answer is that sometimes talking it out won't fix things. If it won't stop until blood spills, then sometimes you have to spill blood until it stops. With that in mind, all you can do is try to save as many people as you can."
