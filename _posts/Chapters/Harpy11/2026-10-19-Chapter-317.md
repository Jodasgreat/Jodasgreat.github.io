## [V11C42] Farewell Song for an Angel

Charonlala had good ears, so when she stopped singing for a second, she could hear anything and everything.\
Folulutia the traitor concealed her identity and lived among the humans, but nonetheless, Folulutia was still a Harpy. She never once wanted to be human.\
And then there was a human who accepted Folulutia even when they knew that she was a Harpy. That man who sang with Folulutia back at the village, the one they called Hütter.\
In that moment, Hütter gave Charonlala and Folulutia a chance to sing together one last time.

(…So long as that man is around, Folulutia will be just fine.)

She was confident in that. Thus, Charonlala resolved to do everything she could to ensure her little sister made it out of this alive.\
Along those lines, the reason she introduced herself as "the last Harpy" to the Monster Hunter was in order to keep Folulutia safe.\
That Monster Hunter despised Harpies. Surely, if he realized Folulutia's true nature, he would not hesitate to kill her. So Charonlala felt she had to insist that she alone was the last remaining Harpy… Or so she assumed, but that Monster Hunter then dove in to protect Tia. Even though, in all likelihood, he already knew what she was.

(That Monster Hunter turned his back on me, his enemy, to go save Folulutia…)

When she saw that, Charonlala's heart wavered.

(Tia… has formed bonds with humans.)

Charonlala was in love with a human man. Yet she never revealed her identity to him. She could never.\
So instead, she chose to cut ties with Kamil, her lover, and live on as the last remaining Harpy.\
But seeing Tia be so loved even though they knew she was a Harpy — seeing Tia accomplish what she herself could not — Charonlala knew she could entrust her dreams to Tia.\
The man with a scar on his face raised his sword against Folulutia. It was that human weapon they called an Ancient Magical Artifact. One cut from that and there would be no saving her.\
Charonlala rolled into a dive as fast as she could, grabbing the man's shoulders with her talons.\
'Ignatius, the Severer's blade sliced across Charonlala's torso from her shoulder to her stomach.

"Luu… Aah…"

With a little song, she called to the Wind Spirits. Then, Charonlala spoke to her sister and the spear-wielding Monster Hunter behind her.

"There's no saving me now. Folulutia can't reproduce. As of this moment, Harpies are as good as extinct."

"S– Sis…"

Tia called to her with a trembling voice. Charonlala simply looked over her shoulder and smiled.\
Then, in a songlike cadence, she told her:

"Farewell, Folulutia. My adorable little sister, you are now truly the last of the Harpies."

Charonlala's talons grabbed the cursing, rambling man by the face and she took off.\
The man flailed wildly with 'Ignatius, the Severer,' slicing at Charonlala as much as he could. His hand maintained a firm grip on the Artifact sword with no signs of letting go any time soon — perfect.\
While Harpies preferred to drop their prey into Breakneck Gorge, that was far from here.\
So instead, Charonlala approached the Font of the Abyss and unceremoniously dropped the man inside.

"—Ah."

The man made a weak noise as his body disappeared into the Abyss along with the Monster-killing sword in his hand.\
Charonlala dripped with blood from all over as the King of Monsters looked up at her.

"O Harpy, do you wish for a peaceful rest in the Font of the Abyss?"

Charonlala knew what the King of Monsters was asking.\
Those words were the King's way of mercy. The King of Monsters wanted to grant Charonlala with the most sincere honors.\
Nonetheless, Charonlala shook her head.

"…Forgive me, O King. That is not the place where I must rest."

Charonlala spread her wings wide and, catching a wind current, flew ever higher.

"Luuuuaaaa—… Aahh—"

Harpies could make three voices at once.\
Charonlala used two of those voices to sing, while her third bid farewell to her little sister.\
She did so secretly, in a register only Harpies could hear.

—*Farewell, my adorable little sister. Farewell, live life to the fullest. Farewell, farewell, I have always loved you.*

Bleeding all the way, Charonlala flew northwards. Winter winds blew from the north to impede her flight. Even so, Charonlala sang to the Wind Spirits, borrowing their wind to fly further into the north.

***

On that day, just as always, the blind pianist Kamil sat playing his piano. Today's song was a concerto composed by a genius musician from a neighboring country.\
While some out there would have called Kamil a genius of his own right, Kamil understood that true geniuses were on a whole different level.\
He'd seen what a true genius could do, and it broke his heart. Even so, he loved the piano. He loved music. It had been his lover, Lala, who made him remember those things.

(If only Lala were here…)

Kamil tapped the keys with his lover on his mind when he heard a faint rustling out of his window that made his hand pause above the piano.\
That sound meant she was here — the sound of her **wings**.\
Kamil pretended not to notice that she was really an angel in disguise. He was worried that if she knew that he knew, his angel would leave him.

"Lala."

Kamil walked up to the window, sensing her presence.

"Kamil."

Kamil rested his hand on the windowsill, and she placed her hand on top of his.

"I came to say goodbye."

Kamil swallowed his breath.

(Why? …Oh, yes, she must have realized that I know she's an angel.)

Lala's voice shook with sadness. Her voice made it abundantly clear that she didn't suddenly hate Kamil.

"I love you, Kamil. I love you, your music, and everything that makes you"

"Me too, Lala. I love you, your singing, and every part of you."

Something soft pressed against his lips.\
Then he noticed the smell of blood in the air.

"Lala, wait, don't tell me you're hurt…"

"Kamil, would you let me hear your piano one last time…… Please, send me off like you always do."

Kamil told her "I love you" one last time, then sat at his piano.\
With all the pain in his heart traveling through to his fingertips, he tapped the keys.

*Because you are so beautiful, I taught you the color of the sky.*\
*I wanted you to feel the spring breeze wafting over my wings.*\
*Let us see the world outside the window.*\
*O rose, O rose, with you, I will go anywhere.*\
*O rose, O rose, with you, I will be forever.*

—*I will steal you, and we will go anywhere.*

His angel's voice grew ever more distant.\
Kamil shed tears as he played the same song over and over again.

***

"*O rose, O rose, with you, I will go anywhere. O rose, O rose, with you, I will be forever.*"

The bloodied Harpy sang as she charted a course towards Breakneck Gorge.\
The sounds of the piano had long since faded into the distance. Yet nonetheless, the notes echoed in Charonlala's chest until her last breath.

"*I will steal you, and we will go anywhere…*"

When she finished singing the last line, Charonlala closed her eyes.\
However much she loved a human, Charonlala was still a Harpy.\
And there was only one place for a Harpy to find her final rest.

A bird who could no longer fly belonged in the bottom of Breakneck Gorge.

(Farewell, Folulutia. Farewell, Kamil. Farewell, farewell…)

Her fellow Harpies tossed their dead kin into Breakneck Gorge while singing songs of lament.\
Should Folulutia someday make her way back to Breakneck Gorge, would she sing a sad song for her?\
Thinking of that day, the bloodied Harpy fell into the gorge.

—And thus, Harpies were now functionally extinct.
