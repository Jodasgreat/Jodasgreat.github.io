## [V11C37] The Weak, Foolish Harpy

Total chaos reigned around the Third Spire: Water Bubble.\
A crowd of Monsters big and small gathered around Fiene and the King of Monsters, each one lashing out with teeth and claws. In particular, the five Greater Monsters born from the Font of the Abyss changed from simple black masses, gradually taking more lifelike forms as their tentacles and claws attacked humans.\
The mages of the Wedge Tower gradually closed the distance and attacked in formation, using Rose's rose vines to absorb the excess mana.\
However, their attacks could not reach the King of Monsters or Fiene. That they were standing in the Abyss' exceptionally dense mana, combined with the fear radiating from the King of Monsters, meant no attack could get close to hitting them.\
And in the skies above, battles between bird Monsters and Extermination Office mages raged on.\
The most intense of those was the battle between Charonlala and Frederik.\
Frederik had the advantage in both flight speed and maneuverability. Charonlala did a barrel roll, aiming to get on top of Frederik, but Frederik disregarded the wind completely as used magic to accelerate upwards.\
Frederik's spear shot towards Charonlala's wings, wreathed in swirling winds.\
But Charonlala would not be defeated so easily. She sang to the wind spirits, willing them to interfere with Frederik's flight magic. Just as Frederik lost his balance, she delivered a strong kick with her talons. Frederik deflected her kick with the shaft of his spear, then counterattacked with another lunge. The tip of his spear clipped off a tuft of Charonlala's hair, strands of orange scattering into the breeze.

"…Sis, please… Run… Get out of there…"

Tia stood still and whispered in a voice only her sister could hear. Her sister, however, would not run. After all, there was no longer anywhere for a Monster like her to go.

…And there could only have been one person who orchestrated this terrible situation.

(Where is he? He's gotta be watching this, but from where!? ……Kai!!)

Tia's eyes darted around, desperately searching every hiding spot in sight. Yet, as far as she could see, there was no sign of Kai anywhere.\
Even if she did catch sight of Kai and dragged him out for the world to see, would that put a stop to this conflict? …No.

"Uu… aa…"

Tia's lips, that never had an issue singing without a care in the world, now let out a groan of despair.\
As a Harpy, Tia could understand how Charonlala was feeling. Of course she would loathe and despise the man who killed her kin.\
But at the same time, Tia knew where Frederik was coming from. She had seen it first hand.\
Imagine watching your father be eaten before your eyes, then having the seeds of terror planted in you in order to make you better prey. It was Monsters themselves who made Frederik fear Monsters so much. On top of that, while Tia didn't know the specifics, how many of his Extermination Office comrades had he watched lose their life?

(I thought there was something I could do…)

Tia had, in her own way, done her research. She had thought and pondered all she could… and the answer she arrived at was:

—There was no stopping this fight. Blood would spill, no matter what she did.

Frederik's spear found Charonlala's skin. As she was trapped in the maelstrom around the spear tip, this attack was due to tear her wing off and send chunks of her body flying along with it.\
Before her thoughts could catch up with her, Tia activated her Magical Leaping Device at full throttle. *Faster, faster faster—* Then, she reached out towards Frederik. For the briefest moment, her fingertips brushed against Frederik's arm. In aerial combat, even that tiny nudge would make the difference between an attack hitting or missing.\
The tip of Frederik's spear drifted ever so slightly off target, ultimately doing little more than slicing through a couple of Charonlala's feathers.

"Tia!?"

Frederik looked at Tia in shock. He was alarmed, but he didn't make a scary face.\
He must have assumed that Tia had come to assist him, and that she had only bumped in to him because she'd misjudged her jump. He never suspected for a second that Tia might have intentionally thrown off his attack.

"Don't worry, I've got this. You should get somewhere safe, my rival. You might get hurt."

Frederik's gentle words made Tia's chest ache with guilt.\
Out of the corner of her eye, she saw Charonlala regain her balance and lift up her talons. Tia's arm reached out towards her.

"Stop… please…"

*CLANK* Tia felt her body shudder. No, wait. What shuddered was the Magical Flying Device on her back.\
She had pushed the Flying Device to its limits, and with that last sudden acceleration, she had expended all of its mana.\
Tia's body leaned over, then fell to the earth.

"Tia!"

"Folulutia!"

Both Frederik and Charonlala shouted at once.\
Frederik reached out with his hand, Charonlala with her talon.\
But just as either of them were about to reach her, Frederik was weary of Charonlala, while Charonlala was weary of Frederik. In that brief moment when their attention was focused on each other, Tia's body slipped out between their fingers.\
As she fell, the voice of that loathsome girl reached her ears.

"Tia, look at me."

Fiene was smiling, her hand still immersed in the Font of the Abyss. Like an angel.\
A newborn Monster in the shape of a bird emerged from the black surface covering the Spire. As soon as it was born, it flew straight for the bush where Hütter was hiding.\
Fear overcame Tia's whole body. Fiene had been waiting for this: just the right moment when there was no way Tia could make it in time.

—To kill Hütter.

"Mr. Hüttttteeerr!!"

In freefall, Tia reached to her pocket. Then…

***

When he saw that black bird-shaped Monster flying straight at him, Hütter made his peace.

(Ah shit, I'm dead.)

That beak would go straight through his body. Sevil was up on the front lines taking charge, while Wren stepped away from Hütter to better throw tubes of inscription magic towards the Spire.\
The Monster was clearly targeting Hütter.\
This middle-aged man's hips didn't work like they used to. It would take him a few seconds just to stand up. There was no way he could dodge this.\
As he stared down a hopeless future, Hütter spotted something white in the corner of his vision.\
Coming up from behind the black Monster, a large bird with white wings flew towards him.

(No, wait, that's not a bird! That's…!)

Starch-white hair and amber eyes. The face of a girl that always laughed and sang — the face of his student.\
And yet, her outstretched arms were covered with white feathers. her shoes were torn to shreds, with a bird's talons sticking out of the tears.

"Luuaaaahhh!!"

When Tia sang, tiny grains of light gathered around her, bringing along with them a gust of wind.\
Tia accelerated, overtaking the black bird Monster. Not content to simply overtake it, she lashed out with her sharp talons, cut through it, then accelerated again.\
The moment Tia's feet — the Harpy's talons — latched on to Hütter's shoulders, her Magical Flying Device let out a single powerful burst of wind. That must have been one of its safety features activating. When the Magical Flying Device ran out of mana, it was designed to store up a tiny bit of reserve mana that it used to stop its user from falling to their death.\
Using that wind to her advantage, Tia soared high into the sky, avoiding the Monster's counterattack.\
As he dangled from Tia's talons, Hütter gazed at the increasingly distant ground.\
Everyone stared in shock at Tia — at the white-winged Harpy.\
Everyone, with two exceptions: Wren and Sevil.

(Those rascals… Of course they knew.)

Their faces sent a clear message: '*Help us out, Mr. Hütter.*'\
So, the third-rate con man faced the people down below and projected his voice.

"Nothing to worry about, everyone! Did you forget who I am!?"

Realization spread across the mages' confused faces.\
With a confident attitude that insisted there was nothing wrong, he held his index and middle finger two his forehead.

"This is, of course, chantless illusion magic."

In essence, his excuse was: '*Tia may look like a Harpy right now, but it was merely camouflage to deceive the Monsters. You don't have to come rescue me.*'\
Tia tilted her posture downwards. Looking up, he noticed that a couple sections of the Harpy's wings were unnaturally short. She was missing her flight feathers.

(I see… so *that's* why.)

Tia sang her song to control the wind as she descended towards the gardens.
