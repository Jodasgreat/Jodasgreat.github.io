## [V11C39] The Whereabouts of 'Ignatius, the Severer'

—*If it won't stop until blood spills, then sometimes you have to spill blood until it stops.*

The first of the Apprentices to reach that conclusion was doubtlessly Sevil.\
She had, on too many occasions, witnessed the harsh reality where bloodshed was the only option. So naturally, she would take up the sword and take responsibility herself so that the weak need not spill their blood. Such was the Barbarian Sword Princess' way of life.

(…Talk won't solve this. That's just reality.)

Any other time, he would have wanted to dig deeper on this subject, but now they probably didn't have the time.\
What could they do to prevent a war from breaking out? The answer was obvious: *It's already started, dumbass.*

"Next question: Do you know what the biggest difference is between the human side and the Monsters?"

"Um, there's a lot of humans, but they all work together. The Monsters are all over the place."

"Correct. You've been paying attention. To be more precise, the Monsters don't have enough leaders."

The Monsters' side was led by the King of Monsters. Aside from that, they relied on Fiene and the Font of the Abyss, and that was about everything holding them together. They didn't have anything a human would recognize as a commander.\
The Primordial Beast and the Vampire Jill might have been in a position to take command, but the former had completely abandoned the battle. Jill, meanwhile, was being kept at bay by the humans' strongest fighter.\
Even with the Headmaster absent, the humans could fall back on the three Spiremasters. And if the Spiremasters were defeated, the Directors of each office could step up. Wherever the Directors weren't able to take charge, their subordinates organized themselves into smaller teams to coordinate their operations. This organizational structure set the humans apart from the Monsters.\
Humans' strength lied in their organizations, something Monsters did not have.

"Tia! Mr. Hütter!"

Dashing through the gaps in the trees, Wren ran up to them. He must have seen Tia flying away with Hütter in her Harpy form and come bolting here in a huge hurry.

(Sevil's still on the battlefield, huh? …Of course she is. There's no way she could just slip out of there after declaring herself the next Headmaster like that.)

Wren glanced at Hütter with an incredibly uncomfortable expression. So Hütter made sure to address him with the same tone as always.

"Tia filled me in on her circumstances. Wren, you mentioned something a little bit earlier, but I want to make sure we're on the same page. What's the status of the other Apprentices?"

"Um, uh… Rukiye stayed back. Then, uh, I think everyone else, aside from Oliver and Rose, is here on campus. Finn did get sealed away though; he's in the book Sophie's holding now."

'*So, excluding those from my class, there are only five left who can move around feely: Julius, Gerald, Sophie, Roswitha, and Ella. I might as well count Agniol, who should be with Julius as well.*' Hütter quickly ran through the numbers, then told the two of them:

"Alright, listen up. I'm going to suggest a plan to solve things, but I'm a human, so it's an idea that's convenient for humans. I want you to keep that in mind, and make your own decisions, okay?"

His plan would mean things would work out in a convenient way for the humans, while also leaving the possibility that the Monsters *might not* get completely eradicated.\
But not everyone would like it. With his biases laid established, Hütter turned to face Tia.

"It goes without saying, but this plan is going to be hardest on you, Tia. Do you still want to go through with it?"

Tia nodded to his question.

"I'll do it. I will."

The continued survival of Monsters as a race weighed on this question. Tia had to bear all that weight herself.\
Even so, Tia did not hesitate to nod. When Wren saw that, a bright new confidence entered his voice.

"…Well, shucks. Sounds like you're gonna need some pretty boy support too!"

Now that Tia and Wren had made their decision, Sevil would definitely pitch in as well. Everything else relied on his follow-through.\
'*Alright, it's time for the biggest con in human and Monster history.*'

"Great, then I hope you're ready for Mr. Hütter's special lesson! There's just one detail you need to keep in mind: *don't die.* —That's all!"

"You can't say anything more specific!?"

Hütter returned Wren's shout with a scolding.

"Do you really think there's anything more important in this situation!? If anything happened to you, I would be responsible! For crying out loud, don't die on me!"

"Piyo…"

"You grown-ups are the worst!"

Wren gave his usual snippy response, to which Hütter smiled like the bad man he was.

"That's right. I've taught you well, haven't I?"

***

In front of the Third Spire: Water Bubble, the situation approached unprecedented levels of chaos.\
The humans were only able to maintain their morale — and only barely — thanks to Sevil's presence.\
She might have sounded ridiculous when she called herself the next Headmaster, but in battle her orders were succinct and accurate. Her aura of never taking no for an answer, alongside her status as a Princess and a Krewing, stirred the disorganized people into action.

"Take on the Greaters in groups! The enemy has no coordination! Hit them from behind!"

The Extermination Office mages deployed to take on the enemy. When they had the target cornered, heavier hitters like Rikard swung in to deliver a devastating blow.\
The other, non-Greater, Monsters obviously posed a threat as well, but Saint Helena's 'Eurydice, the Cackling Foam' mercilessly shredded the weaker Monsters to bits.\
However, while Sevil had ordered the destruction of the mana forge, that would be easier said than done. A great deal of attack mana was expended in that direction, but all of that magecraft ended up absorbed into the Font of the Abyss.\
Looking down on all of this from above, Frederik Lange swung his spear.\
The wind-wreathed spear lanced out at the orange Harpy. The tip of the spear, however, drifted ever so slightly off target, hitting nothing. The enemy Harpy's attacks likewise suffered from some slight accuracy issues. Frederik supposed both he and the enemy Harpy had the same thing on their minds.\
He couldn't shake that image of his little rival in the back of his mind — the Harpy with her white wings spread wide.\
When Tia picked up Hütter, the man claimed that it was his chantless illusion magic, and with how much the majority of people trusted him, they all took him at his word.\
But Frederik had seen the whole thing. He saw Tia put something in her mouth as she fell. He saw the moment the girl's skin tore open and white wings unfurled from within.\
And then, Tia demonstrated a method of flying entirely unlike flight magic or the Magical Flying Device. Having killed countless Harpies himself, Frederik of all people could say with confidience:

—That was how a Harpy flew.

(Don't.)

The blood drained from his head; he could barely form a complete thought.\
He thought of the spider Monsters that attacked the Lange Hamlet. Of the Arachne in human skin who died to protect Oliver.\
That foolish Monster that pretended to be human now overlapped in his mind with his cheery little rival.

(Stop it.)

His father was killed before his very eyes. They forced his father's flesh into his parched mouth, planting the seeds for an incurable fear. He could never eat a proper meal again. He could never sleep properly at night. So, he turned to his anger to withstand the fear.\
For a Monster to act as a human was nothing short of disgusting! Revolting! He had to kill every monster! Kill them all! If he couldn't kill all of them, he would never be able to sleep in peace!

(Cut it out!!)

That gentle singing voice seeped into his frozen heart.\
Thanks to that girl, he slept a full night without a single nightmare for the first time in ages.\
Thanks to that girl, he started to think of flying as fun.\
Frederik had never once doubted his belief that, in the future when all of the Monsters were dead, that little rival of his would be there with him.

(If she's really a Harpy…)

He was the one who killed the Harpies of Breakneck Gorge.\
He did not regret it. Harpies were pests that abducted, raped, and murdered humans.\
But how must Frederik the Harpy-killer look in Tia's eyes?

(Why…)

The orange Harpy approached with her talons.\
She too seemed to hesitate in her attack. Even so, she was much quicker to recenter herself than he was.\
The orange Harpy declared with ferocious eyes:

"*I* am the last Harpy. Now's your chance to finish the job, Monster Hunter."

That orange Harpy had long since made her decision.\
Frederik, on the other hand, could not snap out of it. He had forgotten how to focus on his anger like he always had. Unless he stoked the flames of that anger with hatred, Frederik could not be strong.\
Just barely dodging out of the way of the talons rocketing at him, Frederik spotted something out of the corner of his eye. About halfway between the Third Spire and the gardens stood a familiar face.\
It was the Extermination Office mage with an X-shaped scar on his face, Dammer. He was wondering where that guy had run off to. '*So that's where he was?*'\
Whatever he was doing, he must have had his hands full. With blood all over his body, he held a magnificent longsword. Even though Dammer always preferred whips and daggers.

(Wait, that sword…)

It was a little hard to make it out with the distance, but the silhouette of that sword gave Frederik a very bad feeling.\
The sword Dammer was holding looked an awful lot like the Ancient Magical Artifact that leaves irreversible wounds on Monsters: 'Ignatius, the Severer.'
