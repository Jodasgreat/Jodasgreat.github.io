## [V11C48] King of Monsters

(It's Oliver! Oliver's here!)

Tia kept the fear-banishing song going as she calmly surveyed the area.\
The Ancient Magical Artifact 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower' had absorbed the King of Monsters' power, spreading pure fear from the Abyss over the whole area.\
But the moment Oliver's spear pierced through the King of Monsters, that inky substance that stained the ground disappeared in the blink of an eye.\
…No, it just weakened. It hadn't disappeared. The King of Monsters still had some strength left.\
As a Monster herself, Tia knew that in a place with mana density as high as this, simply putting a hole in a Greater Monster would not be enough to kill them. Then, what would? Tia already knew the answer.

"Oliver! Jump! Like a wild hare!"

Instead of answering, Oliver started chanting. He activated his own flight magic.\
His flight magic only let him fly straight up into the air. Still holding on to his brother Frederik and his spear, he soared into the air, dragging along the King of Monsters who was impaled on that spear with him.\
Rose's defensive barrier covered 'Chariclea's main body. Thus, it was absorbing the King of Monsters' power through the earth, and through that same earth releasing its power. Therefore, the mana density increased as one got closer to the ground.\
So if they were to distance themselves from the ground, the mana density would thin out… and Monsters would weaken.

"WHOOOAAA! I am Oliver, the Red Rain! I'll die before I let go of this spear!!"

Oliver howled as he soared ever higher. Frederik kept his attention on the mana in the spear.\
Fresh blood poured upon the land like falling rain from the gaping hole in the King of Monsters' body, courtesy of the Lange Brothers — a red rain.\
Tia bit down on another candy as she soared upwards after the Lange Brothers.

"Children of man, that spear alone will not be enough to kill me."

The impaled King of Monsters spoke.\
Tia, singing with two of her voices, responded with her remaining voice.

"Then I will sing to you with all my heart."

—*You will become a buried corpse without a gravestone (I will char your flesh)*
—*To whom will you offer your prayers? (I will turn your bones to ash)*

While she sang a Monster-killing song with two voices, she used the Magical Flying Device to accelerate.\
While flying with both Harpy wings and the Magical Flying Device's wings at once got trickier especially when flying this high, Tia got used to it immediately.\
The Magical Flying Device absorbed some of Tia's mana and sped up. Quickly. Accelerating faster than Harpy wings alone could ever manage, Tia rocketed to close the distance.

(Thank you, Rukiye.)

A Harpy's strength and a human's ingenuity. With their powers combined, Tia flew right up to the King of Monsters.\
Tia could not hit the King of Monsters directly. The fear was simply too strong and her attacks would just slip away. Instead, she just had to focus on Oliver, rather than the King of Monsters.\
Her target: the highest altitude Oliver's flight magic could reach.

(—Here!)

After all the time they had spent practicing flight magic together, she knew exactly where and when Oliver's flight magic would reach it's peak. Tia approached that height and swiped with her talons.\
Far away from the dense mana of the surface and weakened by the Monster-killing song, the King of Monsters' neck tore under the force of the Harpy's talons.\
Blood stained its white hair as its head rolled off kilter.\
Then, Tia dug her talons in, separating the head from its torso.

***

To the King of Monsters, its eyes mattered little. The King of Monsters could see everything its fear could reach.\
And yet now, the King of Monsters focused its attention on the scene reflected in those silver eyes.\
The Monster-Killing Langes and their bloody rain… Indeed, their family was known to use flight magic as a means of pulling Monsters away from the ground's mana density and finishing them off in midair. Thus they cause a rain of blood to fall from the sky, earning them the moniker 'Red Rain.'\
The King of Monsters had witnessed countless Monsters becoming such a bloody rain. It had wondered if someday it too might meet the same fate, but…

(Both man and Monster joined hands to slay me?)

That was most unexpected. That possibility had never even crossed its mind. It was just absurd. Even though the King itself had joined hands with a human in much the same way.\
Humans were weak creatures, yet they had so, so many things that Monsters did not.\
The King of Monsters looked down at its fallen body, now separated from its head. Specifically, those silver eyes turned to its wrist and the flame-colored woven bracelet adorning it. That, the product of human creativity, was a treasure to a Monster like him. Of course Monsters would be obsessed with humans.\
The silver-eyed Monster desired *something* to help it face death.\
Yet now, having been unable to attain that *something,* its long life was drawing to a close.

(If only I could make my own, just like that girl did.)

It recalled the sight of that young artisan dutifully sketching ideas for various projects.\
How different would things have been had it pleaded to her: '*I'd like to make one of these bracelets myself. Could you teach me?*'

(I will probably die soon.)

Cutting off its head wasn't immediately fatal to it.\
Nonetheless, there was no avoiding that impending death.\
In these scant few moments it had left, it still had the chance to come up with a way to face death. More than anything, the King of Monsters' curiosity was piqued.\
How would this Harpy, who flew on two sets of wings — her own and a manmade pair — bring an end to this conflict?\
The Harpy flew high in the air with a song in her voice and the King of Monsters' head grasped in her talons as she shouted to those below.

"*Hear me!* The Silver King who spread fear on these lands is dead! From now on, I — Folulutia of Breakneck Gorge — am the new King of Monsters!"

All humans and Monsters on the ground turned to look up at the Harpy flying with a human device.\
Confusion, bewilderment, shock, and more such emotions dominated the crowd as the Harpy spoke forcefully.

"All Monsters, surrender to the humans! The Font of the Abyss we have fought for is gone! This battle is now meaningless! Rather than sit there and wait for your death, beg for your lives! If you want to live, even if it means you may be merely a pet to them…"

A flash of white filled its vision. White, white, white, and more white cloths. Human children were running around passing them out like candy.

"If you still want to live, then wave the white flag!!"

***

"*Do Monsters know what it means to surrender?*"

When she pleaded for Hütter's help, that was the first thing he wanted to know.\
Tia shook her head.\
Most Monsters had know idea what surrender meant. Even if they did know the word, they wouldn't understand it. Even Tia herself had only recently learned the meaning behind waving a white flag.\
Hütter nodded at Tia's honest response, then said:

"*In that case, you're going to have to teach them yourself. Show the other Monsters exactly what it means to wave the white flag.*"

***

Sophie hefted a huge basket in both hands and wobbled as she ran. Inside the basket was a collection of random white cloths she had gathered from all around.\
Tablecloths, bedsheets, pillowcases, handkerchiefs, even any relatively clean rags she could find.

—"*Once Tia slays the King of Monsters, she's going to declare a surrender, so you're gonna have to pass these out.*"

That was Wren's message.

(Quickly, quickly, I need to start passing these out!)

But with all this many large cloths, the basket was just too heavy and unwieldy for her. Ella and Gerald had already started passing out cloths. She had to get there and help too…\
All of a sudden, her bag vibrated. No, it was a book in that bag that was shaking.

"…Finn?"

Sophie instantly touched the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Despina, the Chain of Fools' on her wrist.\
Thankfully, Sophie had paid close attention when they were teaching her how to use this precious Ancient Magical Artifact and its power to seal away Monsters.

"*You wish for release. Obey me, O Monster who dwells in this tome — Werewolf Finn.*"

As she held the book open and spoke her command, letters started to lift off of the pages.\
Those letters formed a pattern in the air, eventually taking the form of a black wolf.\
This black wolf landed on all fours, hunched over, and growled.

"…Are you telling me to get on?"

Rather than respond, he growled again.\
Sophie, looking like she was about to break into tears at any moment, wrapped her arms around the wolf's head.

"Say somethiiiing… Finn…"

As Sophie sniffled, the black wolf spoke in a tiny, almost imperceptible voice.

"…It feels awkward…"

That weak voice was exactly the Finn that Sophie knew.\
Sophie's face flushed red as she rustled the fur on the black wolf's head.

"Geez! Don't do that to me! You can talk after all! …So talk to me! Everyone's happier that way! Totally!"

"But I…"

Finn's nose twitched awkwardly as always when Sophie thrust the basket of white cloths in front of him.\
Then, through her tears, she smiled.

"Let's do it together! It's Mr. Hütter's special extracurricular lesson! We're all Apprentice Mages, aren't we!"
