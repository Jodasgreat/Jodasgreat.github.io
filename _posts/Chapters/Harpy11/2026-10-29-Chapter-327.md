## [V11C52] The Soul's Whereabouts, The Witch's Gift

"Oh Great Witch… Oh Witch… Why…? Why…!?"

As Kai broke down, Wren sneered at him.

"The Witch's soul is still incomplete, so I bet she can't use all the power she's supposed to have. No wonder you were able to pull off all this sneaky business behind her back, huh? You don't seriously think the Witch would approve of your plan to drive Harpies to extinction, do you?"

"Shut up…"

"You're jealous towards the Witch, aren't you? All of this stuff you've done just looks like lashing out to me."

"Shut up!!"

"I'm not shutting up, dumbass! You've got no reason to hate Tia personally. Tia just happened to be a perfect target for your lashing out. You knew Tia wouldn't be able to say anything back, so you could say whatever nasty words you wanted! You bastard!"

Wren's tirade silenced Kai.\
Tia's mouth let out a little "Pyohee…"\
The brainy pretty boy was already good with words to start with, but he'd refined his craft under Hütter's tutelage and powered up to a whole new level.\
Kai's dim, hate-filled eyes stared up at him through the gaps in his blond hair. Once they flew to a certain height, his rose vines could no longer reach them. However, that was not all there was to 'Rebecca, the Chromatic Garden's power.\
The noxious smoke had thinned out. Kai muttered something under his breath.

"*O groveling dead, o prideful king, let all be consumed equally by ash.*"

The hairs on the back of Tia's neck tingled. That sounded like bad news.\
Kai held the flower ornament to his chest with one hand as his other hand stretched out towards Tia and Wren.

"*Sway, then with thy touch cleanse and consume all things. — O black flame, bring us the end.*" 

His incantation flowed with a distinct rhythm as black flames snaked up from his fingertips.\
Tia's whole body shuddered at the mana radiating from those flames. It felt just like 'Ignatius, the Severer.' One touch and that would be it — a power that meant certain death.

"The First Thorn Witch Rebecca Roseberg had two iconic magics. One was the Man-Eating Rose Fortress. The other was these Black Flames that burn through anything and everything…"

As he spoke, Kai surrounded himself with these black flames. The flames slithered up, changing shape to resemble flying snakes. The heads of those snakes aimed at Tia and Wren.

"Now die, burnt to a crisp by the Witch's flames."

Just when he was about to unleash those black flames on them, rose vines suddenly surrounded Kai.\
Kai's eyes went wide. He was not the one controlling those vines.

"Thanks for filling me in on everything, Wren."

Despite everything, this voice was as laidback as ever.\
With muddied gardening boots, a bearded man with fuzzy red hair stomped towards them — Rose.\
No, perhaps it would be more accurate to call him the Fifth Thorn Witch.\
When he voiced a quick incantation, the ground around Kai swelled up, a cage of thorny branches emerging all around him.\
Then, rose vines crawled between the thorny branches around Kai, smothering the black flames in the air.

"You're a Roseberg, aren't you!? … It's no use! These black flames burn through anything and everything!"

"I know. It was my ancestor's specialty magecraft. But you don't have my ancestor's full power, do you?"

With a gentle wave of Rose's hand, the rose vines sprouting from the ground multiplied. The thicker, sturdier vines enveloped the black flames.\
Without a doubt, the black flames did burn through Rose's vines. However, it took a few seconds to reduce each one to ash. In that time, more and more vines erupted to fill the space.\
The black flames were not snuffed out, but Rose's vines multiplied just as fast as it could burn through them.

"Witch… Great Witch…!! Give me more… Give me more power…"

Unable to move, Kai clung to the flower ornament. As he did, he poured all of his mana into the black flames. Rose's vines, however, did not falter.\
The petals of Rose's white roses turned black and fell apart. Each rose consumed mana from the black flames, turning it into fuel for the vines, allowing it to flourish ceaselessly.\
A gust of wind rustled Rose's hair. His curly, unkempt hair was the same as the Witch's.

"When I'm fighting to protect someone, I'll never lose!"

Kai's scream became a wail of despair.\
Tia listened on with complex emotions ans she landed beside the cage of thorns.

"I'm sorry, Kai."

Tia took her real last remaining candy from her pocket and tossed it in her mouth.

"I lied when I said I ran our of candies."

Her girlish arms split open, revealing a Harpy's white wings.\
Kai laughed darkly as he watched Tia transform.

"…Then, let me reveal a lie of my own. Your body will never go back to being a Harpy. From now on, you will have to live the rest of your life in that suffocating form."

Tia had thought that once she found a way to fly, she could ask the Witch to return her to her original body — her Harpy form. But apparently it wouldn't work out that way.\
Regardless, Tia was not shaken. She quietly accepted the reality with little more than an '*Oh, really?*' in her mind.\
It seemed that Kai found Tia's serenity disturbing.

"Say, what do you think you are now that you've lost your original form? A human? A Monster? What do you think I am, unable to be either human or Monster? What kind of creature is that?"

"A Harpy. From the moment I was born until the moment I die… I am, and you are too, Kai… a Harpy."

To prove her point, Tia sang.\
She sang the song Harpies sang to kill Monsters — to kill her fellow Harpies.

—"*On the day of tears, you will learn your worthlessness. (I will tear your skin one piece at a time) (And tear out your wings)*\
—*In the mud, no one will witness your flower. (I will slice your flesh one chunk at a time) (And end your songs)*\
—*You will rot, melt, and dissipate. (I will crush your bones one at a time) (The sky is far away)*

—"*Your demise is evident, you shall not see tomorrow. (I will tear, and slice, and crush you) (You have lost the sky)*\
—*Regardless of how much value you wear. (I will tear, and slice you) (You have lost your song)*\
—*That value is not your own. (I will crush, and melt you) (You shall know how hard the ground is)*

—"*On the final day, you may laugh at your worthlessness. (I will burn your skin) (How dark, how dark)*\
—*You will become a buried corpse without a gravestone (I will char your flesh) (In the cold silence of the gorge)*\
—*To whom will you offer your prayers? (I will turn your bones to ash) (So I sing for you)*\

—"*Your corpse will rot, and the birds will cry (The tears have long since dried) (And yet I still sing)*"

When a Harpy must turn the Monster-killing song on her own kind, she made sure to use all three voices.

—*So I sing for you*

Kai's body collapsed into the dirt.\
Even without any visible wounds, as a Harpy, the mana that comprised Kai's body crumbled from within.\
As Tia sang, she glanced over to Rose and pointed at the cage of thorns. Rose nodded and dispelled the cage. The black flames dissipated, and along with the black petals, fell to the ground as nothing but ash.\
Still singing, Tia plucked the crimson rose flower ornament from Kai's hands. At the center of its petals laid a single crimson stone.

—The Ancient Magical Artifact, 'Rebecca, the Chromatic Garden.'

The instant she picked it up, she understood exactly how to use it. That must have been it's nature as an Ancient Magical Artifact.\
Unlike modern magical devices, even magical beings such as Monsters could use Ancient Magical Artifacts. Furthermore, 'Rebecca, the Chromatic Garden' specifically was so powerful it would kill any human who attempted to use it, making it a tool only Monsters could wield. That was why Kai could use it.\
When Tia focused on it, the gem at the ornament's center let out a faint glow.\
This glimmer dropped to the ground, then took the form of a human. What appeared was a woman with brilliant red hair the color of crimson roses draped upon a black dress and a veil covering her eyes — Perfectly recreating the form of the First Thorn Witch, Rebecca Roseberg.\
Kai, lying on the ground, summoned the last of his strength to lift his head.

"…Oh Witch, oh Great Witch… Please don't hate me…"

The Witch knelt down to face Kai. The flowy sleeves of her dress and her radiant curly red hair splayed out on the ground.\
Kai shed a tear as he reached out his hand.

"…Oh Witch, oh Great Witch. Take my colors. I'll give them all to you."

The Witch's fingers gently touched Kai's blond hair. From that point, the color drained, leaving Kai's hair and skin bleach-white. His colors and mana were absorbed into the 'Chromatic Garden.'\
With the inside of his body already hollowed out by Tia's song, he began to crumble apart like a pillar of salt.\
Even with a prominent crack forming across his face, Kai smiled like a young child resting peacefully in the arms of his mother.

"Great Witch… Great… Witch… *O Rose, O Rose, I will steal you, and we will go anywhere.*"

His creaking voice weakly strung together a faint melody. The song of a Harpy's love that has continued through the ages. Where had he learned the song? Did he overhear Tia singing it, or had someone else sang it for him? … There was no way of knowing the truth.\
Then, the Witch waved her hand gently and a single rose blossomed in her palm. The Witch laid the rose, a vibrant color much like Kai's hair had been, beside Kai's head.

No one knew exactly how much the Witch had seen, how much she had heard, or how much she had known.\
Only that the Witch granted him this flower.\
That fact alone put a smile to Kai's face.

"*I will steal you… and we will go anywhere*… I really did… want to go with you…"

Without a sound, his head crumbled away. Nothing was left aside from his clothes, a pile of white dust, and that single rose.\
Thus marked the end of a male Harpy who deceived both man and Monster throughout his long life.

(He couldn't give up on his song to the very end. Kai really was a Harpy.)

Tia held the red rose ornament, the 'Chromatic Garden,' to her chest and glanced up at Rose.\
Even after showing off such awesome power, Rose casually stood there just as he always did. His immense silhouette loomed over Tia.

"…Rose, are you going to take the Witch with you?"

The Witch straightened out her black dress.\
Behind her black veil, her eyes gazed at Rose. Or at least she had a feeling they did.\
Tia, Wren, and even the Witch turned to face Rose… Who frantically waved his hands.

"Huh? Oh, no, no. I'm not here to retrieve the 'Chromatic Garden' at all."

Rose's almost shockingly in-character response completely deflated the tension, and Wren said with a sigh:

"Then what *did* you come here for, Rose? Aren't you like… some really important guy in the Kingdom of Ridill!?"

"Well… I know officially I'm supposed to be here to retrieve the Artifact, and the Black Lion Emperor and my Grandmas all think so, but… I just wanted to find where it was."

"Why?"

To Wren's question, Rose stopped stroking his fuzzy red beard and pounded his chest.

"I want to return my ancestor's soul that's inside me to where it belongs. That's why I wanted to find the 'Chromatic Garden.'"

Tia chirped "Pyofu!"\
Wren had mentioned to her that the Roseberg Family split the soul of the First Thorn Witch, Rebecca Roseberg, into two halves. One half resided in the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Rebecca, the Chromatic Garden,' while the other half was passed down through the Roseberg Family's descendants.\
So that meant the Roseberg descendant in question was Rose? And Rebecca's soul was inside him?

"… Can you do that?"

Tia asked ever so cautiously, to which Rose could only stroke his head. "Eh, well…"

"I never really looked too much into how to do that… Um, do you think you could do that, Ancestor?"

Wren and Tia screamed.

"You're asking *her*!? Aren't you supposed to be a Sage!?"

"Piyo! I know this! When you make others do your work, it's called being *lazy*!"

"Wahh, I'm sorry, okay! I was already busy enough looking for the 'Chromatic Garden,' you know~…"

Ignoring the Sage and his lame excuses, the Witch in her black dress pointed at the flower ornament in Tia's hands — the 'Chromatic Garden.'\
Then, the flower ornament responded. Tia felt the sensation of information flowing through hands and directly to her brain.

— "*With the 'Chromatic Garden's power, it could be done.*"

(…That's the Witch's voice!)

Tia closed her eyes and repeated the words flowing into her head.

"*The Contradiction is severed, and understanding grazes the top as it falls to the very bottom. Though the round vessel may be frail, the true vessel is resilient. The fruits of broken flowers fall to the plate without losing their nectar. The fruits do not know the years, and fall to the plate without losing their nectar.*"

Tia fluently lilted the confusing and evasive incantation with a distinctive song-like rhythm.\
Behind her closed eyelids, she saw countless glass spheres containing myriad colors. She watched as the color drained from one of them. Thus, in exchange for the color and mana she had accumulated, the Witch's miracle went into effect.\
Tia placed her fingers above Rose's heart. Warmth — not simply body heat, but something very different — gathered at her fingertips.\
It was cold, yet hot; terrifying, yet beautiful… Tia felt the fragment of a soul between her fingers.\
She held tightly to this invisible object, then brought it into the 'Chromatic Garden.' The crimson gem at the center of the ornament twinkled brightly.\
Pale hands ran across the ornament's surface. Not Tia's hands, but the hands of the Witch's human form. The Witch's hands mimed grabbing something, then suddenly a book appeared in her grasp.\
She must have pulled the book from within the 'Chromatic Garden.' Tia could feel how the Witch's hands dragged the item from within.\
The Witch held the book out towards Rose.

"Take this."

Her voice was cold, yet hot; terrifying, yet beautiful.\
Rose jumped in shock, but Wren seemed to notice something.

"Wait, isn't that book the one I…"

The Witch glared at Wren through her veil, and he shut up right away.\
Without hesitation, Rose took the book in both hands and flipped through its contents. Then, he nodded in satisfaction.

"Then I will take this book back home with me. You need the 'Chromatic Garden,' right Tia?"

"Is that really okay with you, Rose?"

"Yeah. It's what my ancestor wanted anyway."

Rose held the book against his chest with care as he looked back at his ancestor, the Witch whose face was hidden behind that veil.\
His face was gentle, peaceful, and relieved.

"Since I was a baby I've heard all kinds of stories about my ancestor… You know, I was always really scared of you, so I never really tried to learn more about you."

It was a very human response to shy away from the things that scared you. To not want to see them or even know about them.\
Even so, Tia was surprised to hear that the cheery Rose she knew had grown up with such a childhood.

"But now that I have learned all this… Yeah, I'm not scared any more. Now I can go home with my head held high. Thank you, Tia, Wren, Ancestor!"

The Witch's form disappeared without a word. Her artificial body evaporated, absorbed into the 'Chromatic Garden.'\
Then, the crimson flower ornament in Tia's hands faintly glowed as if saying something.

"Piyo… Witch?"

The Witch whispered to Tia alone. Tia nodded with a "Pyofun!"

"…Yeah, thanks!"

She closed her eyes, and behind her eyelids, a glass sphere filled with a brilliant rainbow lost its color.\
This was the color and mana that once adorned Tia's own wings… If she was going to use it, she might as well make something pretty with it.

"*With just a single petal, a single petal, time stops in Spring. For just a moment, brilliant colors cover the land.*"

A sea of green flourished around Tia. It raced across the barren battlefield that now bloomed with flowers of all colors.\
It was the same magic as the out-of-season flowers that bloomed in that garden. "Wow…" gasped Wren. "Amazing," muttered Rose.\
The colors that once adorned Tia's hair and wigs now formed a brilliant flower garden. At its center, Tia stood smiling, her white hair and outstretched wings waving in the breeze.

"It's a gift from the Witch!"

It was a tribute to that which was lost, but also a blessing to all life going forward.

—"*Not bad.*"

She had a feeling the beautiful Witch whispered that to her.
