## [V11C41] Please, Just Disappear

Tia, Wren, and Hütter split up to pursue their separate missions. Hütter and Wren ran off to gather materials, while Tia headed for the roof of the Second Spire: Golden Needle.\
Since her Magical Flying Device was out of commission, Tia couldn't simply fly her way there. That said, if she went back to her Harpy form, she could probably jump off from a higher point and glide there. To do that, she first needed to get to said higher point.\
She decided not to leave the Magical Flying Device behind. On the off chance that Old Man Kappel had some kind of spare mana pack or whatever, she wanted to leave open the possibility that it might be usable again.\
So, with it still on her back, she ran off towards the Second Spire: Golden Needle.\
As Hütter said, she was off to save whoever she could, no matter how few that may be.

(Mr. Hütter really is amazing.)

Tia never asked for anyone's help. The same went for all Monsters. Monsters were always quick to decide '*If I can't do it by myself, then it can't be done.*'\
Yet regardless, Tia was so determined to do what couldn't be done that she begged for Hütter's help. Folulutia, the Queen of Harpies, begged for a human's help.\
And Hütter, despite knowing that Tia was a Monster, was willing to oblige.\
For the first time, Tia understood what it meant to have a mentor. Hütter was the best teacher she could ask for and, in a different way than Wren and Sevil, an irreplaceable figure in her life.

(I can do it, Mr. Hütter.)

Just as the Second Spire came into view, Tia heard the sound of footsteps approaching. This was no Monster. These were human footsteps.\
Why would a human be here? Thinking it strange, Tia turned around to see.\
She saw the outline of Dammer closing the distance with unbelievable speed. With a familiar longsword in his hand.\
Wasn't that 'Ignatius, the Severer?' The same Ancient Magical Artifact that once stole Tia's flight feathers?

"Die, Monster."

Tia instinctively grabbed the lever on her Magical Flying Device. But, of course, she already exhausted all of its mana.\
She considered using choral magic, but that would take time to take effect.\
In that brief moment of indecision, Dammer had already closed the distance. That pale blade he held aloft swung at Tia's neck.\
'Ignatius, the Severer' afflicted Monsters with incurable wounds. Even the tiniest scrape would mean her life.\
Just before the blade hit its mark, however, a powerful gust of wind knocked Tia off her feet.

"Pyauw!?"

Tia rolled on the ground like a ragdoll. Then, spitting dirt out of her mouth, she got up to see where the wind had come from.\
In the sky above, she saw Frederik hovering with flight magic. His face even paler than usual, he held one of his hands out towards Tia. He had knocked Tia away with his wind magic.\
Dammer spat and screamed at Frederik.

"Get the fuck out of my way, Frederik!! This thing's not human! It's a Monster in human skin!"

***

Frederik felt like he was about to throw up.

(I know.)

Why in the world would the Extermination Office's ace run away from battle? And then covering for a Monster? Who would be okay with that? …Yet despite all of that, the instant he saw Dammer raise that sword against Tia, Frederik did a quick incantation to send a gust of wind her way.

(What am I even doing?)

Dammer hadn't done anything wrong. As a member of the Extermination Office, he was trying to kill a Monster that infiltrated the Wedge Tower by pretending to be human. If anything, *he* was the one who was out of line.\
Veins bulged on Dammer's forehead as he swung at Tia once more. Tia must have lost use of her Magical Flying Device, as she tried to run away by waddling shakily on the ground. '*That way she runs… Oh, yeah, she's definitely a Monster.*' Frederik couldn't doubt his eyes any longer.\
Why was she such a natural at flying? Why did her songs move his heart so? Because she was a Harpy. Everything about her stemmed from what she was.

To think he had been swayed by a Monster's song, and thought he had finally had a restful sleep!\
To think he had called a Monster his 'rival,' and thought he had finally enjoyed flying!

He, the Monster Hunter Frederik Lange of all people!!

With his mind all topsy-turvy, Frederik accelerated his flight magic. Then, he inserted himself between Dammer and Tia, deflecting 'Ignatius, the Severer' with the shaft of his spear.\
His thoughts of hatred towards Monsters conflicted directly with his actions to defend Tia. It made him sick.

"Frederik…"

He heard Tia's voice behind him.

(Why am I leaving my back exposed to a Monster?)

Right now, Tia could kill Frederik with ease. And yet, Tia did nothing.\
Frederik gritted his teeth and growled.

"…Get out of my sight."

"Frederi–"

"I said get out!!"

Frederik found he could not muster anything more to say to Tia.\
Tia ran off. Frederik swung his spear to block Dammer from following.\
Dammer glared at Frederik. The pupils in his bloodshot eyes widened.

"Ohh, so that's how it is. You've been brainwashed by the Monster's sweet song, you traitor. I guess that means I've got to kill you."

Dammer swung 'Ignatius, the Severer' and Frederik parried the strike.

(I can't kill Dammer. He's a human, after all… But then what about Tia? She's not human, so why did I let her go?)

His shock, his thoughts, and his panic all worked to slow Frederik's reactions. In the first place, he was already trying to push his wounded body beyond its limits. Blood soaked his bandages and it gradually became harder to move his body.\
Meanwhile, Dammer showed neither hesitation nor restraint. His ravenous eyes saw Frederik as nothing more than an enemy to be eliminated.\
Dammer made a big sweeping attack. Frederik evaded it. Then, when Frederik tried to jab Dammer's stomach with the blunt end of his spear, Dammer quickly closed the distance.\
Frederik gasped. Usually, Dammer would ensnare his target with his whip and paralyze them with lightning magic. He wouldn't get any closer until he was ready to move in for the kill.\
Regardless, Dammer just did exactly that. With unexpected speed.\
Frederik had no way of knowing that Dammer was currently being controlled by 'Ignatius, the Severer.' It made reckless, underhanded attacks that Dammer would typically never consider. It moved in absurd ways with no regard for the damage it might do to the muscles in his arm. That allowed it to get the drop on Frederik.\
Keeping 'Ignatius, the Severer' in one hand, Dammer's left hand drew his knife and jammed it into Frederik's right shoulder.

"Gah!? Ngh… argh…"

A splatter of flesh blood went everywhere. Frederik groaned in pain as he fell to his knees.

***

"Frederik!"

The white-haired girl shrieked.\
Now that Frederik was on his knees, Dammer's field of view expanded to reveal the form of that white-haired girl. He could still catch up to her. He had to kill her. He would kill her. It was time to kill.\
Dammer brought out his whip with his left hand. Its tip wrapped around Tia's ankle.

"Gyauw!?"

Tia screamed and tumbled. Her amber eyes trembled in panic as she looked up at Dammer.\
No matter how he looked at her, she just looked like an ordinary girl. But he would not be deceived. This was a Monster in human skin.

"KILLKILLKILLKILLTHEMONSTERSKILLTHEMALLKILLKILLKILL"

By this point, he wasn't sure how much of the voice in his head or the voice coming out of his mouth belonged to 'Ignatius, the Severer', and how much was his own.\
Dammer lifted 'Ignatius' high and brought its blade down on Tia.

—Droplets of fresh blood danced in the air.

Dammer's hands felt the sensation of slicing through flesh. He felt the warm blood splattering on his cheeks. And, he felt a blinding pain.

"……Hah?"

Orange feathers scattered all over.\
'Ignatius, the Severer' had cut into the torso of an orange Harpy.\
And that Harpy's talons clasped on to Dammer's shoulders with enough force to gouge through his flesh.

"S– Sis…"

In response to Tia's voice, the orange Harpy's throat warbled.

"Luu… Aah…"
