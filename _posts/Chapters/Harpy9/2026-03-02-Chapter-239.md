## [V9 Characters]

## Core Characters

*Tia Vogel / Folulutia*\
A Harpy who wears a human skin.\
Has learned that most of her fellow Harpies were killed by Wedge Tower mages.\
Will arrive at the Wedge Tower shortly.

*Wren Bayer*\
The Apprentice Assistant Representative. Good at copying other peoples' handwriting.\
In service to the Witch of the Boundary, forged a book called '*The Truth of the Thorn Witch*'\
The Chancellor has his eyes on him under suspicion of cuckolding him.

*Adelheid Sevil Lamea Krewing*\
The Emperor's sister. The Barbarian Sword Princess who wields a curved sword.\
Had realized Gerald's true identity, but kept silent because he was her (prospective) future brother-in-law.

*Smoke Fox / Caspar Hütter (Fake)*\
A third-rate con man investigating the cause behind the Wedge Tower cutting off ties with the previous Emperor.\
To be honest, his intelligence truly shone whenever he was tasked with thinking up one excuse after another.


## Apprentice Mages

**Röhm's Class:** *Class led by a genius woman who uses both Modern and Classical Magecraft*

*Roswitha Ohlendorf*\
Classical School mage with a pointy hat. Controls fish of water.\
While she takes pride in hailing from a famed Classical Magecraft family, she also understands just how much dirty work they were involved with.\
As a result, she secretly sympathized with Sophie.

*Ella Frank*\
The Apprentice Representative and a glasses-wearing honor student. Was poor at emitting mana, but succeeded at mana emission following her surgery.\
Currently thinking of what she can do to help her fellow Apprentices.

*Sophie Schwarzenberg*\
Hails from a famous family of Shamans.\
Has five curses embedded in her body, but can only use curses that kill people painfully.\
Lifted the curse that had been placed on Rose.\
Thinks Rose is a criminal who was chased out of the country on false charges.

**Sombart's Class:** *Class that specializes in practical Modern Magecraft*

*Julius Löwenich*\
Son of renowned mage Samuel Löwenich.\
Understands the reason his father was chased out from the Wedge Tower, but now is conflicted over what to do from here.

*Flame Spirit Agniol*\
Julius' contracted spirit.\
After being attacked by the Primordial Beast, has sustained severe damage.

*John Rose*\
Red-haired, fuzzy, muscular man.\
Had his mana sealed by a cursed ring, but the reason for that is still unclear.\
The kind of guy who angles his entire body to make an angled barrier.

*Oliver Lange*\
Member of the Lange Monster Hunting Family. The spiky-smiley 'Red Rain.'\
Currently on standby at the Lange Hamlet. Intends to stick with his brother once he wakes up.

**Armster's Class:** *Class focused on imbuement magic that creates magical devices*

*Rukiye Sorge*\
A stubborn girl with the air of an artisan. Has recently been getting friendlier.\
Her heart is quietly aching since the theory of Finn being the Werewolf made sense to her.

*Finn Nohl*\
A Werewolf with Crystal Rivets embedded in the soles of his feet to maintain a human form.\
Has been trying to retrieve the fang that Dammer had stolen from him.

*Gerald Anker*\
Real name: Gerhard Blanke. Younger brother of the Margrave of Warmberg.\
All nervous, worried that Sevil would realize who he really was at any moment.\
She knew right from the start.


## Wedge Tower Spiremasters

*Möbius*\
Headmaster. Tall, silver-haired swordsman.\
Wielder of the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Ignatius, the Severer.'\
Impaled by the crystals from the crystal-contaminated Wedge Tower, suffering grave injuries.

*Miriam*\
Assistant Headmaster. Blonde woman in a nun's habit. Devoted to her faith.\
Forcibly reactivated the Wedge Tower's Wall that had momentarily dissipated, then had that wall sealed through the power of 'Despina, Chain of Fools.'\
Yet as the cost for pushing herself too far, has aged dramatically.

*Abel*\
Spiremaster of the First Spire: White Smoke.\
A gentle lady who's always smiling.
One of very few people who knew that the Wedge Tower was an Ancient Magical Artifact.

*Lowein*\
Spiremaster of the Second Spire: Golden Needle. A one-armed old man. Loves his dog.\
Loudmouthed and imposing. Frederik's master.
Defended Spiremaster Alto while she used choral magic.

*Alto*\
Spiremaster of the Third Spire: Water Bubble. A straightforward old lady.\
Somewhat of an artist. An old-timer at the Wedge Tower who's known Old Man Kappel for ages.\
Countered a Harpy's song with choral magic.


## Mages of the First Spire: White Smoke (Spiremaster: Abel)

*Melchior John Hegelich*\
Guidance Office Director. Middle-aged man with a blond bowl-cut and glasses.\
A Modern School mage. Was a frequent target of Samuel Löwenich's schemes.\
Wounded after being attacked by Eyeball Birds.

*Anneliese Röhm*\
Member of the Guidance Office. A kind young woman with short bangs.
Former rivals with Extermination Office Director Haydn and mentor to Frederik and co.\
In exchange for Crystal Rivets, betrayed humanity to side with the Monsters.\
Specializes in a combination of detection and attack magic. Known as the Hundred-Eyes Witch.

*Sombart*\
Member of the Guidance Office. A smiley bastard of a teacher. Loves bad men.\
Admired Samuel Löwenich and offered to work with Hütter to uncover the reason behind his exile.\
Currently responsible for watching his pigeons. Even in this emergency situation, is solely focused on taking care of those pigeons.

*Armster*\
Member of the Guidance Office. A fidgety middle-aged man who always looks like he's on the brink of death.\
Specializes in imbuement magic. Hütter seems to have opinions about the way he acts.\
In place of the Maintenance Office Director, was keeping watch on the hut in the gardens.


## Mages of the Second Spire: Golden Needle (Spiremaster: Lowein)

*Dammer*\
Member of the Extermination Office. A Man with a huge X-shaped scar across his face. Wields a whip.\
Wears a fang as a necklace.\
Made the X-shaped scar on his face by his own hand in order to give the impression that he had been marked by the Primordial Beast.\
Tia had realized this fact.


## Mages of the Third Spire: Water Bubble (Spiremaster: Alto)

*Linke*\
Director of the Library Office. Elegant woman with long hair neatly tied up.\
The wielder of 'Despina, the Chain of Fools.'\
Served Assistant Headmaster Miriam with all her heart.\
To maintain the Wall that Miriam had risked her life to regenerate, she offered her life to place a seal using 'Despina, the Chain of Fools,' and subsequently died.


## Monsters

*King of Monsters*\
White hair and silver eyes. Used to have black hair but gave it away to the Chancellor in exchange for him making the Crystal Rivets.\
Appears to have an obsession with faith, but…?

*The Primordial Beast*\
A Monster born from the Abyss. Loves human talents and is obsessed with breaking down those talents with his own strength.\
Leaves an X-shaped mark on the faces of those he prefers.

*Charonlala*\
A Harpy with wings and hair that resemble a raging inferno.\
Tia's elder sister. Is in love with a human pianist.

*Chancellor*\
An unsettling man wearing a hood. Made the Crystal Rivets.\
Intensely hated by the other Monsters.

*Jill*\
A Greater Monster in the form of a handsome blond man.\
A vampire who just positively adores art that those cute little humans make. '*Mwah, mwah!*'

*Jack*\
A spirit of Winter. Even though he fought against and lost to Tia and the others, dissipating into mist, he revived now that Winter has arrived.
