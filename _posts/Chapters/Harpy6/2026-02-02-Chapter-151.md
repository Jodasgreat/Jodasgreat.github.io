## [V6 Characters]

## Core Characters

*Tia Vogel / Folulutia*\
A Harpy who wants to fly.\
To ensure her safety in the Wedge Tower, is determined to locate Fiene, the weakness of Headmaster Möbius.\
Has formed a reliable partnership with Julius. Very reassuring.

*Smoke Fox / Caspar Hütter (Fake)*\
A third-rate con man who is investigating the reasons behind the Wedge Tower cutting off relations with the previous Emperor.\
Has formed an unthinkable partnership with Sombart. Very nauseating.

*Wren Bayer*\
Assistant Representative for the Apprentice Mages. A brainy pretty boy.\
Is curious to know the true intentions of the people who Tia was involved with.

*Adelheid Sevil Lamea Krewing*\
The Emperor's sister. The 'Barbarian Sword Princess' who uses a curved sword.\
Puts herself on the line to investigate the Wedge Tower's secrets, is determined to support Tia.\
Unflappable and tomboyish, but could show lust if she wanted to. Regardless, her overpowering aura would drown out the sexual appeal.\
Seems like the prince she was meant to marry has come to the Wedge Tower…?



## Apprentice Mages

**Röhm's Class** *Class taught by a genius woman who uses both Modern and Classical Magecraft.

*Roswitha Ohlendorf*\
A Classical mage with a pointy hat. Controls fish made of water.\
Because she takes pride in Classical Magecraft, she is conflicted on how to deal with Ancient Magical Artifacts.

*Ella Frank*\
Representative of the Apprentices, a glasses-wearing honor student. Bad at emitting mana.\

*Sophie Schwarzenberg*\
Hails from a famous family of Shamans.\
Has five curses embedded in her body, but can only use curses that kill painfully.\
Came to the Wedge Tower because she didn't want to curse people to death, but…


**Sombart's Class** Class focused on Modern Magecraft that prioritizes practicality.

*Julius Löwenich*\
Son of Samuel Löwenich, a prominent mage.\
Is investigating the reason his father was expelled from the Wedge Tower.

*Flame Spirit Agniol*\
Julius' contracted spirit. \
Was previously contracted to Samuel Löwenich.\

*John Rose*\
Red haired, fuzzy, and muscular.\
Due to matters with his family, can only stay at the Wedge Tower for one year.

*Oliver Lange*\
Member of the Lange Family of Monster hunters. The spiky-haired 'Red Rain.'\


**Armster's Class** Class that focuses on imbuement magic and the creation of magical devices.

*Rukiye Sorge*\
Stubborn girl with the eye of an artisan.\
Lately, her attitude seems to have been mellowing out a little bit.

*Finn Nohl*\
Out of the Apprentice Mages, he and Sophie are the youngest. \

*Gerald Anker*\
A boy with long bangs. Has strong physical prowess.\

## Spiremasters of the Wedge Tower

*Klaus Möbius*\
The Headmaster. A tall, silver-haired swordsman.\
Wields the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Ignatius, the Severer.'\
Classmates and former best friends with Otto from the Protection Office. A man who never gets invited to drinking parties.

*Miriam*\
Assistant Headmaster. A blonde beauty in a nun's habit.\
Deeply religious. Has a poor throat and cannot sing.\
Former Director of the Library Office, holding the position prior to Director Linke.

*Abel*\
Spiremaster of the First Spire: White Smoke.\
A gentle auntie who's always smiling.\
Assumed the title of Spiremaster following Samuel Löwenich's fall from grace.

*Lowein*\
Spiremaster of the Second Spire: Golden Needle. A one-armed old man.\
Has a big, scary voice. Master to Frederik and co.

*Alto*\
Spiremaster of the Third Spire: Water Bubble. A straightforward old lady.\
Has the touch of an artist. She's an old-timer in the Wedge Tower, and has known Old Man Kappel for ages.



## Mages of the First Spire: White Smoke

*Melchior John Hegelich*\
Director of the Guidance Office. A blond-haired bowl-cut glasses-wearing middle-aged man.\
A Modern School mage who was familiar with Samuel Löwenich.\
Samuel rather favored him.

*Anneliese Röhm*\
Member of the Guidance Office. A nice young lady with short bangs. Suffers from mana organ damage.\
Former rivals with Extermination Office Director Haydn and a mentor for Frederik and crew.\
Detection and Reconnaissance were her specialties. She was a genius with the title 'The Hundred-Eyes Witch.'

*Sombart*\
Member of the Guidance Office. A bad man who loves bad men.\
Adored Samuel Löwenich and proposed to cooperate with Hütter in order to learn the reason behind his expulsion.\
Hütter's internal assessment of him has been demoted from 'smiley bastard' to 'smiley piece of shit.'

*Armster*\
Member of the Guidance Office. A jittery teacher.\
Occasionally appears in the garden, but…?


## Mages of the Second Spire: Golden Needle

*Frederik Lange*\
Member of the Extermination Office. Mr. Smiley who excels at flight magic.\
His father was eaten by a Monster, and he hates Monsters more than anyone else.\
Concerning Helena and Rikard, his thoughts are 'Why do you suddenly turn into idiots?'

*Helena*\
Member of the Extermination Office. Frederik's classmate.\
A Saint of the Ras Belsch Orthodoxy. User of the Ancient Magical Artifact 'Eurydice, the Cackling Foam.'\
Was once fixated on behaving as a Saint should, but learned to guard her mind through self-pity, so now just says rude things casually.\
Concerning Frederik and Rikard, her thoughts are 'Repent, you worthless losers!'

*Rikard Oks*\
Member of the Extermination Office. Frederik's classmate. Wields an axe.\
Wants to be the kind of Monster Hunter who sings and dances, making people laugh. As he fights with his feet planted on the ground, tries his hardest to come up with new jokes.\
Concerning Frederik and Helena, his thoughts are 'What weird people…'

*Lutz Otto*\
Member of the Protection Office. A tired, middle-aged man.\
Classmates with Headmaster Möbius.\
Lost his wife and child thirteen years ago.



## Mages of the Third Spire: Water Bubble

*Kappel*\
Director of the Management Office. An old man and an inventor.\

*Winston Barrett*\

*Linke*\

## Other

*Samuel Löwenich (Deceased)*\
Julius' adoptive father. Was exceptionally talented both as a mage and as a man of wealth.\
Rose to the rank of Spiremaster of the First Spire: White Smoke in order to grasp the knowledge of the Wedge Tower, but fell from grace.\
After being expelled from the Wedge Tower, suffered from a strange illness that rotted away at his internal organs. Long ago, when he was working at a magical academy, defeated Hegelich in a factional conflict. Despite that conflict, Hegelich caught his eye. Samuel wanted to make him his right hand man, but he refused.\
Hegelich was unaware that doing so only made Samuel even more interested in him.
