## [V10 Characters]

## Core Characters

*Tia Vogel / Folulutia*\
A Harpy who wears a human skin. Disowned by her sister.

*Wren Bayer*\
The Apprentice Mages' resident pretty boy.\
One of very few who know the truth behind the First Thorn Witch and the Roseberg family.

*Adelheid Sevil Lamea Krewing*\
The Emperor's sister. The Barbarian Sword Princess who wields a curved sword.\
Her darling has come to the rescue, and yet said darling is now all but guaranteed to find her. She's in quite the pickle.

*Smoke Fox / Caspar Hütter (Fake)*\
A third-rate con man.\
Guaranteed to be aching all over after Poppo Showtime.\
His ties to the Black Lion Emperor have been exposed, but the fact that he's a fraud is still under wraps. That is to say, people now believe the story that the Dream Mage used to be a performer when he was younger.

## Apprentice Mages

**Röhm's Class:** *Class led by a genius woman who uses both Modern and Classical Magecraft*

*Roswitha Ohlendorf*\
Classical School mage with a pointy hat. Has been working hard to whittle a whole bunch of sticks so she can take down the Primordial Beast.

*Ella Frank*\
The Apprentice Representative and a glasses-wearing honor student.\ 
Was poor at emitting mana, but succeeded at mana emission following her surgery. Currently practicing a certain form of magecraft.

*Sophie Schwarzenberg*\
Hails from a famous family of Shamans. There's now a chance she might have her seal undone. She wants to talk with Finn at least one more time.

**Sombart's Class:** *Class that specializes in practical Modern Magecraft*

*Julius Löwenich*\
Son of renowned mage Samuel Löwenich.\
His new ambition is to become a legendary villain, take hold of the Wedge Tower, and turn it into a place where even poor children can study if they put their minds to it.

*Flame Spirit Agniol*\
Julius' contracted spirit.\
She's back! And she's tiny! Behold!

*John Rose*\
Red-haired, fuzzy, muscular man. Is really one of the Seven Sages from a neighboring country, the Fifth Thorn Witch.\
Unable to use the magic that lets him collect a ton of acorns.

*Oliver Lange*\
Member of the Lange Monster Hunting Family. Calls himself the 'Red Rain.'\
Made sure to eat well, sleep well, and rest well so he can better support his brother. Now is the time to show the fruits of his labor.

**Armster's Class:** *Class focused on imbuement magic that creates magical devices*

*Rukiye Sorge*\
A stubborn girl with the air of an artisan.\
Said she would not participate in the operation, opting to stay behind.

*Finn Nohl*\
A Werewolf who disguised himself as an Apprentice Mage.\
Wants to take back the fang Dammer stole from him.

*Gerald Anker*\
Real name: Gerhard Blanke. Younger brother of the Margrave of Warmberg.\
His brother is here. He's screwed. He's already resigned himself to his fate.


## Wedge Tower Spiremasters

*Möbius*\
Headmaster. Tall, silver-haired swordsman. Currently a heavily wounded patient under strict orders to rest.\
Sacrificed his best friend's daughter in the name of peace for all of humanity.

*Miriam*\
Assistant Headmaster.\
To deify her best friend, raised that friend's daughter as the Daughter of God.\
Theorized that she may have been under the influence of some form of mental interference magic, then promptly passed away.

*Abel*\
Spiremaster of the First Spire: White Smoke. The one who knows the most about the Wedge Tower's dark secrets.

*Lowein*\
Spiremaster of the Second Spire: Golden Needle.\
Prioritizes resolving the current crisis, even if that means owing a debt to outsiders.

*Alto*\
Spiremaster of the Third Spire: Water Bubble. User of choral magic.\
Like Lowein, was unaware of the Wedge Tower's secrets.


## Mages of the First Spire: White Smoke (Spiremaster: Abel)

*Melchior John Hegelich*\
Guidance Office Director. Suffered heavy injuries after being attacked by eyeball birds.\
On the verge of having a stroke after Hütter's embarrassing display onstage spiked his blood pressure.

*Anneliese Röhm*\
Member of the Guidance Office. The Hundred-Eyes Witch who betrayed humanity to side with the Monsters in exchange for the Crystal Rivets.\
Now awaiting her favored rival in the crystal-contaminated Wedge Tower.

*Sombart*\
Member of the Guidance Office. A smiley bastard of a teacher.\
Doesn't really give a shit what happens to the Wedge Tower.

*Armster*\
Member of the Guidance Office and a dollmaster. Is actually an advisor on magical crafts serving directly under the Black Lion Emperor.\
He was given the title 'Soul of Creation Armster' of the Imperial Magic Cross Flame Heaven Four Artisans… but he caved in and started working for Abel almost immediately.

*Meine*\
Branch Director of the Medical Office. Has known Dammer since they were kids. They didn't get along very well, but she would probably be sad if he died. Or something like that.


## Mages of the Second Spire: Golden Needle (Spiremaster: Lowein)

*Lutz Otto*\
Mage of the Protection Office. Magic sword user.\
Now that he knows that his own daughter was forced to become the contractor to 'Chariclea, the Wedge Tower,' is determined to do whatever it takes to rescue her.

*Frederik Lange*\
Mage of the Extermination Office. Member of a Monster-hunting family.\
Currently en-route to the Wedge Tower, riding on Winston Barrett's back.

*Helena*\
Mage of the Extermination Office. A Saint who possesses an Ancient Magical Artifact.\
One of her partners is on the verge of death, the other has lost his mind… Ah, how sad… how sad…

*Rikard Oks*\
Mage of the Extermination Office. Member of a Monster-hunting family.\
His heart is pounding with excitement now that he's learned the person he looked up to so much went on to master the art of magecraft to become the Dream Mage!

*Dammer*\
Member of the Extermination Office. A Man with a huge X-shaped scar across his face. The man who stole Finn's fang.\
For him, who was born in the Wedge Tower and knows nothing of the world outside, he cannot conceive of any honor other than risking life and limb to kill Monsters.


## Mages of the Third Spire: Water Bubble (Spiremaster: Alto)

*Winston Barrett*\
Member of the Management Office. Known as the 'Soaring Mage.'\
While he was out getting in contact with various outposts, Frederik latched on to him at the Lange Hamlet and now he had no choice but to carry him to the Wedge Tower.

## Others

*Kai / Chancellor*\
Contractor to the Ancient Magical Artifact 'the Chromatic Garden,' and the one who gave Tia the ability to assume a human form.\
Aligned himself with the Monsters, calling himself the 'Chancellor.'

*Fiene*\
Otto's daughter. The Daughter of God.

*Henrik Blanke*\
The Margrave of Warmberg. Gerald's (Gerhard's) elder brother.\
Until recently, was on standby in a relatively calm section of the southern front. He found that deployment strange, but it wasn't long before he received the order to sortie to defend the Wedge Tower.

*Poppo Four*\
A somewhat shy female pigeon. She's very well behaved, so she is well suited to roles that require her to stay quiet and hidden, but she has a habit of getting lost very quickly.\
Is often picked on by the tsundere Poppo Three, who starred in the interlude from Volume 2. *Pyen-pyen* (sobbing noises).

## Monsters

*King of Monsters*\
White hair and silver eyes. Used to have black hair but gave it away to the Chancellor in exchange for him making the Crystal Rivets.\
Has an obsession for faith; desires a god for the Monsters.

*The Primordial Beast*\
A Monster born from the Abyss.\ 
Loves human talents and is obsessed with breaking down those talents with his own strength.\
Left a scar on Roswitha's mother's face long ago.

*Charonlala*\
Tia's elder sister, a Harpy.\
Oblivious to human logic, has fallen in love with a human and had a falling out with her sister who has lost her reproductive abilities.

*Jill*\
A Greater Monster in the form of a handsome blond man.\
Watched the Poppo Showtime from a safe distance.

*Jack*\
A spirit of Winter. Even though he fought against and lost to Tia and the others, dissipating into mist, he revived now that Winter has arrived.\
Now, who will he freeze next?
